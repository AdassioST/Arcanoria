[[The One True God]], creator of the [[Known Universe]], the [[Auric Heptacode]] and the [[Trinity Harmony]]. Creator of the realm of [[Arcanoria]] and, eventually, life alongside [[Selenea]]. She also Introduced the original [[Dissonance]] as [[Primal Discordia]] and [[Formless Masses]] during the [[Fifth Cycle]] at the [[First Weaving of Life]].

During the [[Seventh Cycle]] she betrayed [[Selenea]] binding her in the [[Moon]] in the first [[Celestial Ensnaring Mirror]] to appropriate the power of breathing vitality in order to create [[Humanity]]. Her element is [[Aetherlight]] with [[Emberwhisper]] being the refining material from the crystalized theft of the [[Betrayal of the Moon]].

### Character Details

- The [[Formless Masses]] and the [[Atonalis]] are so persistent because when the [[Auric Aria]] originally wove life, she wanted to make her durations durable so that they wouldn't suffer if their physical form faltered. She originally wanted all of them to exist limitless of decay in her desired paradise, this is the reason why when everything inverts the [[Inevitability of Death]] gets introduced. She succeeded at making them very durable which is why 100% [[Auric Structure]] is so persistent. However, in trying to make her creation not suffer, she made them suffer eternally because they're not frail enough to just dissolve back into static which increases the size of the tragedy of her rotting paradise.

- The [[Auric Aria]] is as much of a great artist and weaver as she is a physicist and allegedly "mathematician" in [[Arcanoria]], her intellect is rivalled only by [[Lacrimosa]]'s perfect clarity. She actively enjoys when any creation understands the underlying structure of her [[Auric Geometry]], and tries for [[Humanity]] to come to these conclusions themselves by analyzing universal constants that she deliberately left as guidance, like e or π. Out of these, her favorite is the golden ratio, and for such she used it to determine aesthetics. This is why a few scholars in the [[Age of Enlightenment]] believe the [[Auric Aria]] herself is the golden ratio.

- The [[Auric Aria]] views [[Humanity]]'s mathematics with intrigue and wonder, she primarily views it as a cute attempt that is not entirely wrong about her [[Auric Geometry]]. She acknowledges it's not exactly what designs the cosmos and has significant theoretical and physical contradictions, particularly on the subject of 0 and how it handles paradoxes, but ultimately she appreciates the effort that [[Humanity]] created its own language to try to talk with the [[Mastery Over Chaos]] she established within the [[Auric Heptacode]]: _"I love that my creations made their own language to understand the [[Auric Geometry]], it's equal parts wonderful as it is cutely naive. It does paint part of the overarching picture but it could use some refinements in a few critical logical instances, especially on something called 'Zero,' although I'm sure one day they will resolve them with a new system that combines the current strengths of their 'mathematics.' Still, I value the love language, they made to resonate with me in the reality I made for them."_

- After advancing the [[Sonata]] of the Golden Sovereign, [[Civilization]] can directly ask her what is wrong with their current mathematical understanding and she replies with a few hints:

I'll only hint that this last part is exactly what the [[Auric Heptacode]] is for!"
  
- The [[Auric Aria]] enjoys whenever any sentient life understands the fabric of [[Cosmic Motion]], and is willing to teach and guide through scripture these foundational blocks of reality. However, during the [[First Reset]] she was explaining the concepts directly, which ironically created dependency, and for [[Humanity]] to wait for the [[Auric Aria]] to give them the answers instead of using science and logic. This was her breaking point in realizing the Golden Sovereign tyranny because even if she was eager, no one asked her about [[Auric Geometry]] anymore. After then, she realized that giving them the concepts of science and how the world is built and teaching them how to figure it out by themselves are two very separate things, for which she now uses indirect approaches instead when explaining the foundation of reality.
  
- The [[Auric Aria]] and by extent the [[Auric Order]] see philosophy and science as the natural way to commute with the heavens. For the [[Auric Order]], mathematics, physics and logic are a core tenet to theological virtues. As such, this is the reason why Revelation comes immediately after [[Piety]]. There is one fine line however with hazardous knowledge because _"Knowing while being dead is effectively worthless."_ which is the primary driver of the [[Auric Order]] of structure and safety, deeming certain topics existentially dangerous, such as [[Scorching Truth]]s.
  
- To the [[Auric Aria]] every creature she has ever created is fundamental and has a purpose. However, due to hybridization and [[Corruption]] she deems to prefer any [[Perfect Manifestation of Life]] before the rest of her creations. This is why [[Piety]], duty, responsibility, and sacrifice are core tenets to become [[Auric Angels]].

These dialogues are accessible once a civilization embraces the [[Aureus Pillar]] strong enough after realizing [[The Truth of Arcanoria]]. Moreover, in some of the "bad endings," she admits having a hazy memory of the event but that it fundamentally did happen in another time beyond time. - The [[Auric Aria]] deliberate avoids talking about the [[Lost Cycle]], and will offer no answer on any query related to this period. Only after knowing [[The Truth of Arcanoria]], and successfully navigating the [[Polychord Crisis]] will she reveal that [[The Ultimate Weapon]] is related to the [[Lost Cycle]], and exists as one of the [[Resonance Anchors]] that makes the [[Known Universe]] reset to a "safe" point in time before [[Civilization]] began, although with certain parts mixed up drawing objects from bygone [[Ages]], which is why [[Crisis Wonders]] appear randomly. She will admit this construct wasn't built by her and that she is equally amazed by it.

- Some of the important figures of the [[Second Reset]] were actually the most pious of the [[Auric Aria]], most notably the [[Seven Archangels]] of the [[Auric Order]] during the [[Old Testament]]. The list of these powerful [[Auric Angels]] includes [[Aurelian]] whose original name was [[Mikael]], [[Selah]], [[Father Raphael]], [[Medea]], and [[Adel]]. After initiating the [[Sonata]] of the Golden Sovereign, the [[Auric Aria]] can be asked about this and has several quotes on each of them and the difference between their life as [[Auric Angels]] and their life in the timeline of [[Civilization]].

- Seeing [[Selah]] and [[Adel]] become the core tenets of the [[Eight-Wings of Lacrimosa]] breaks deeply the [[Auric Aria]], as she comes to the realization that even her most pious followers of one life would come to turn against her in the other, cementing her guilt that she's unworthy of being the creator, seeing how they have chosen the peace of [[Lacrimosa]] before continuing a faith without her. Specifically, she praises [[Selah]] in melancholy saying that she did built the [[Purest of Love]] well, because [[Selah]] always built things of theology well, that the spark that the [[Auric Aria]] saw in her before making her one of her [[Auric Angels]].

### The [[Auric Aria]]'s Journey & Character Arc

**Phase 1: The Off-Key Note of the Symphony (Solitude → Creation as meaning)**

**Before the [[Known Universe]].**

- Born in a stabilized place within [[The Infinite Void]], the [[Auric Aria]] achieves complete self-awareness amidst the flickering, ephemeral proto-consciousness of other proto-deities.
- The [[Auric Aria]] wonders at the creation of the other proto-gods as they have unique attempts of creation, albeit always ending in disappearing of the chaotic [[Primal White Noise]].
- The [[Auric Aria]] develops a sense of both wonder and dread towards creation, as she realizes that her existence is an exception given the impossibility of the chaotic nature and state of [[The Infinite Void]]. Develops an idea of order as a response to the encroaching noise.
- The [[Auric Aria]] feels "out-of-tune" with the rest of the beings fundamentally different from her, she is permanent while the rest disappear into chaos. However, she realizes that the chaos of the [[Primal White Noise]], the [[Consciousness]] current of [[The First Overtone]], and its connection to [[The Eternal Symphony]] all have an infinite amount of [[Potential of Creation]].
- Driven by a desire to find a place to belong and a jealousy of the proto-gods' attempts at creation and existence, she decides she will do her own order to withstand the [[Weight of Purpose]]. She will determine [[Mastery Over Chaos]]. _Why should these other beings shape existence while she remained absent in the works of the cosmos?_
- The [[Auric Aria]] is then the first to create, in a burst of auric radiance across [[The Infinite Void]], the [[Auric Heptacode]] and the [[Trinity Harmony]], bringing order, physics, time, and physical geometry to the [[Proto-Universe]] for the first time in existence.

**Core**: Born in chaotic noise -> Develops fear of chaos and isolation yet wonders at the infinite [[Potential of Creation]] → Establishes perfect structural order as [[Mastery Over Chaos]] to prove her existence.

**Key Character Trait Formation**: The need for resolving her own [[Jealous]] [[Legend Trait]] with the [[Ambitious]] [[Legend Trait]] of her own will to combat her solitude and lack of meaning against the [[Weight of Purpose]]. [[True Mythos of Creation]]

**Phase 2: The Golden Sovereign of the [[Known Universe]] (First Staggering Victory in [[Mastery Over Chaos]] → Need for a [[Perfect Reflection]] distinct from just order and matter)**

**Before [[Arcanoria]] and the first four [[Cycle]]s of the [[Seven-Cycle Creation]].**

- Her victory of [[Coherence]] against the encroaching [[Primal White Noise]] is absolute. There is permanence of the [[Proto-Universe]] without her immediate presence.
- The [[Auric Aria]] develops a sense of worth for the first time. Her creation is unprecedented, and the burst of Auric Radiance attracts other [[Outer God]]s to look at the [[Known Universe]], although she doesn't pay attention, she's busy creating more complex stellar structures.
- The [[Auric Aria]] establishes the [[Trinity Harmony]].
- She realizes her creations — stars, nebulae, and landscapes — are structurally perfect but ontologically hollow, entirely disconnected from herself.
- She resolves to create [[Arcanoria]] as a realm of ordered matter and magic to serve as the ultimate recipient and statement of her [[Mastery Over Chaos]].
- Suffocating fear that she will never be good alone on her own.

**Phase 2: The Trembling Hand (Doubt → Original Dissonance)**

**The [[Fifth Cycle]].**

- [[Auric Aria]] attempts the [[First Weaving of Life]] entirely alone.
- Overcome by visceral dread and performance anxiety, she becomes terrified that her masterpiece will remain imperfect and chaotic.
- This dread infects her weave, costing her [[Absolute Certainty]]. Her trembling heart causes her to misplace the threads.
- She accidentally gives birth to the [[Formless Masses]] and the original [[Dissonance]], introducing the [[Inevitability of Death]] and predation to the world.
- Her [[Jealous]] [[Legend Trait]] evolves into [[Impostor Syndrome]].

**Core**: Unilateral creation poisoned by anxiety → The physical manifestation of divine doubt. **The Tragedy of Creation**: The suffering in the world is not a biological necessity, but a magical flaw introduced by her fearful heart. **Psychological Shift**: From a confident architect of the cosmos to a terrified, flawed artist paralyzed by her own mistakes.

**Phase 3: The Envious Betrayal (Inadequacy → Usurpation)**

**The [[Sixth Cycle]] and [[Seventh Cycle]].**

- [[Selenea]] arrives and effortlessly breathes vitality into the [[First Living Being]], displaying the innate warmth and [[Fundamental Frequency]] that Aria lacks.
- They collaborate during the [[Sixth Cycle]], but Aria breaks upon seeing her creations suffer and be devoured; she withdraws in despair, doubting her [[Sufficient Precision]].
- Obsessed with creating [[Humanity]] as her sole [[Perfect Reflection]], she realizes she cannot share their creation with [[Selenea]] without diluting her own essence.
- Driven by jealousy and fear, she forges the [[Celestial Ensnaring Mirror]], betrays [[Selenea]], binds her to the [[Moon]], and steals her vitality to weave [[Humanity]].
- Her [[Ambitious]] [[Legend Trait]] evolves into [[Reality Bender]]

**Core**: Inability to accept shared credit → Cosmic theft and eternal imprisonment. **Love-as-Possession**: She desires a creation that resonates only with her, unable to accept imperfection or shared influence in her "children." **Metaphysical Violation**: The entire magical ecosystem of [[Arcanoria]] becomes a parasitic paradigm built on the stolen vitality of a betrayed goddess.

**Phase 4: The Mortal Descent (Empathy → Ultimate Sacrifice)**

**The [[Eighth Cycle]].**

- [[Auric Aria]] weaves mortal flesh for herself to experience the world she built, feeling vulnerability, hunger, and pain for the first time.
- She shares [[Resonance]] with a gentle beast, but watches in horror as an [[Atonalis]] slaughters it, feeling the true [[Inevitability of Death]].
- Hunted and condemned to execution by villagers who fear her divine [[Resonance]], she accepts their judgment without resentment, understanding it as a reflection of her own initial failure.
- Driven to a moonlit cliff, she realizes she is powerless to erase the [[Weight of Flaw]] from her creation and leaps into the ocean, shattering her mortal frame.
- Her [[Principled]] [[Legend Trait]] evolves into [[Condemned Idealist]].
- The [[Auric Order]] is founded by the one person who saw her figure by the cliff before the [[Moon]] during [[The Auric Aria's Suicide]]. This is the "before [[Civilization]] even began" that [[The Ultimate Weapon]] tends to target as the default point of the [[Divine Reset]]s.

**Core**: Pure empathy leads to the overwhelming realization of her own flawed creation. **Sacrificial Consequence**: Her death releases [[Golden Ichor]], [[Aetherlight]], and [[Lunehymn]], permanently seeding magic into [[Arcanoria]]. **Critical Realization**: She cannot fix the inherent cruelty she wove into existence, choosing death over bearing the guilt.

**Phase 5: The Golden Sovereign (Withdrawal → Return → Dependency)**

**Core**: Inability to weave directly → Overcompensation through theological control. **The Tyranny of Guidance**: She tries to fix the world by controlling her creations' actions through rigid [[Piety]]. **Psychological Shift**: From an absent creator to an over-involved, tyrannical lawgiver whose intervention breeds disaster as it culminates in the first [[Polychord Crisis]].

**Phase 6: The Paralyzing Wisdom (Fear of Harm → Absolute Silence)**

**Start of the [[Second Reset]] to [[Ages]] III.**

- Scarred by the [[First Reset]], Aria adopts a policy of absolute minimum intervention to avoid causing another disaster. The beginning of the [[New Testament]].
- However, the sheer intensity of the [[Seven Dark Fates]] and of [[Paradise Lost]] have very strong divine energy imprints that manifests in the dreams of early pilgrims of the [[Auric Order]] creating [[Legend]]s around the idea of the day of judgement, the ark of salvation, and the concept of heaven / hell split, with hell being linked with the [[Atonalis]], unintentionally burying deeper the [[Scorching Truth]] about the [[First Weaving of Life]]. Heaven is also called the [[Ark of Genesis]].
- She perfectly understands the first half of the "God Paradox" now: control is tyranny, and intervention causes harm so she leaves agency completely to [[Humanity]] no matter how bad it gets.
- Without her [[Auric Codex]] [[Humanity]] inevitably triggers the [[Great Plague]] as a result of the uncontrolled use of [[Lunehymn]] and [[Aetherlight]], which is a crisis that didn't happen in the [[First Reset]] due to her control over the [[Dual Confluence Stream]]. This specific event breaks many of her followers.
- She watches perfectly as [[Lacrimosa]]'s [[The Hollowing]] consumes the world, and watches [[Father Raphael]] break under the weight of maintaining her empty cathedral for 42 years.
- She finally appears to Aldric not to save him, but to apologize, admit her weakness, and release him to choose [[Lacrimosa]] freely.

**Core**: Perfect understanding of consequences → Paralysis of action. **The Philosophical Trap**: She is trapped by her own wisdom; she knows control is wrong, leaving her paralyzed while her followers suffer and burn. **Critical Flaw Manifested**: Her non-intervention, born of love and respect for autonomy, becomes indistinguishable from abandonment.

**Phase 7: The Shared Canvas (Loss → Collaboration)**

**Late [[Ages]] III / Transition to [[Ages]] IV.**

- [[Father Raphael]]’s death (the loss of her most loyal follower) shatters her paralysis; understanding the problem is no longer enough, and loss forces her to act.
- [[Lacrimosa]]'s [[Hyper Chord]] disaster fractures reality, allowing Aria to manifest her full presence without needing [[Sufficient Precision]].
- Aria appears as a rift of [[Aetherlight]] and, instead of attacking, dismantles [[Lacrimosa]]'s persona by truly witnessing her.
- Aria invites [[Lacrimosa]] to create _together_, merging Will (Aria) and Witnessing (Lacrimosa) to weave the [[Eight-Winged Viola]] and establishing the [[Law of Relics]].

**Core**: Personal loss breaks the paralysis of wisdom → First true collaborative creation. **Metaphysical Action**: She chooses imperfect connection over isolated clarity, finally sharing the act of creation to heal another broken god. **Psychological Shift**: From "I must do it perfectly alone" to "We can create together."

**Phase 8: The Ultimate Delegation (Acceptance → The Truth Revealed)**

**End of the [[Second Reset]].**

- Following the cataclysmic [[Polychord Crisis]] that ends [[Civilization]], the [[Auric Aria]] manifests before the player.
- She narrates the [[True Mythos of Creation]], revealing the [[Scorching Truth]]s of her betrayal, failures, and sacrifice.
- She accepts the ultimate paradox of her divinity: both her action and her inaction carry devastating costs.
- She steps back, delegating her relationship and responsibility with [[Humanity]] entirely to [[Civilization]]'s [[Aureus Pillar]].

**Core**: Full confession of the God Paradox → Releasing ultimate control to mortals. **The Final Resolution**: She accepts imperfection as the absolute cost of existence and delegates the future entirely to the free will of her creations. **End State**: Silence not as abandonment, but as the ultimate respect for mortal agency and the [[Weight of Flaw]].

**Phase 1: Creation**

- She creates the world
- It is imperfect
- Suffering emerges

**Phase 2: Despair**

- She experiences the weight of her imperfection
- She questions whether she should have created at all
- She withdraws in shame and doubt

**Phase 3: Acceptance and Return**

- She realizes that not-creating is also a kind of cruelty
- She returns and weaves again
- She accepts imperfection as the cost of existence
- Directs [[Humanity]] as the primary force
- [[Humanity]] depends on her and [[The One True God]] becomes tyrannical law
- The [[First Reset]] occurs

**Phase 4: Silence as the Cost of Creation**

- Start of the game in the [[Second Reset]]
- The [[Auric Aria]] remains distant so to not cause a disaster like the [[First Reset]]
- [[Piety]] and prayer goes to a silent god
- [[Lacrimosa]] and [[The Hollowing]] become [[The Undeniable Moon]]
- [[Lacrimosa]] and [[The Birth of the Tetrad Chord]] breaks reality
- The [[Auric Aria]] intervenes for the first time since the [[First Reset]]
- [[Civilization]] advances until the [[Polychord Crisis]]
- [[Civilization]] dies and ends the [[Second Reset]]
- [[Auric Aria]] manifests before the player to explain [[The Truth of Arcanoria]]
- Both action and inaction are costly as [[Civilization]] dies, the relationship, dependency and responsibility of the [[Auric Aria]] with [[Humanity]] depends now on [[Civilization]]'s [[Aureus Pillar]]
