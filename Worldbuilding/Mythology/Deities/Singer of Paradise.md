#creature #faith 

_The Recitative and Chorus after the Aria meant to Sing the Dawn of Creation._

This mythical [[Pure Light]] creature is made at the resolution of the [[Sonata]] of the Interwoven Threads of Gold and Silver during the climax of reaching the Garden of Life at the apex of the [[Parlor of the Moon]].

The [[Singer of Paradise]] original frame was made during the [[Sixth Cycle]] but was abandoned half-way when the [[Auric Aria]] broken with empathy retreated to the inner sanctum of the [[Parlor of the Moon]] in the Heart Core.

During the [[Sonata]] this bird is the representation of the relationship between [[Selenea]] and the [[Auric Aria]], and it only gets resolved at the very end of the movement of Forgiveness of the [[Pale Moonlit Violin]] Concert that [[Selenea]] had prepared for the [[Auric Aria]] at the very beginning in the [[Ballad]] of the [[Parlor of the Moon]].

[[Singer of Paradise]] are the phoenix-like quetzals strikingly colored birds of distinct hues of rose gold and silver that inhabit the Garden of Life in the [[Parlor of the Moon]]

These multi colored birds are based on the recipe of [[Strand]] loyalty of [[Memory Singer]]s but transcending the hubris of [[Humanity]]. Ironically, they serve as a stark contrast of the [[Age of Velvet Elegance]] which are the closest counterpart in the idyllic gardens of the [[Velvet Throne]]s but with an entirely different purpose that reclaims the original intention of the birds as the singers of the dawn of creation.
