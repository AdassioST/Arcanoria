_"The Throne of the Heavens, for anyone suicidal or fool enough that doesn't die trying to reach the apex of its glory."_

The [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] are a [[Landmark]], biome and key religious location of high [[Vibrational Density]] in the upper layers. They are a contradiction of [[Sky Glass]] and waterfalls carved into the sky, forming an idyllic wound in the world's highest spine, as beautiful as it is lethal. They are amongst the highest peaks in [[Arcanoria]], and hold the clearest view of the [[Grand Thread Rings]] of all [[Landmark]]s.

**Three Laws govern all who enter:**

_The Mist Remembers:_ The silver mist is a sort of vaporized [[Lunehymn]], carrying fragmented dreams of every pilgrim who has climbed before. Prolonged exposure is to risk experiencing someone else's death as vivid hallucinations.

_The Mountain Dreams in Fractals:_ The [[Sky Glass]] formations are not random. They are the mountain's dream-logic made visible, the same recursive pattern repeating at every scale, from frost [[Crystal]]s to the arrangement of the peaks themselves.

_The Abyss Listens:_ The [[Lunar Abyss]] is not empty. It is receptive. Sound carries downward without decay, and anything shouted into the chasm returns transformed, as though something below has answered in a language that only resembles the original words.

The location is sacred to both the [[Aureus Pillar]] and the [[Chorus Pillar]] but for different reasons, it is a marvel of [[Auric Geometry]] and it is a sanctuary of [[Lunehymn]] and dream-like states necessary for [[Outer God]] communion.

The location itself is composed of several mountain valleys that grind jagged fangs of veined granite and black ice that rise in the shape of crescents and arched pass ways above a frozen wasteland that never fully surrenders to winter. Amidst the impossible terrain there are several waterfalls that pour not into rivers but through veins of [[Sky Glass]] that flow upward as visible ribbons of fractals behind translucent stone before spilling from cliff lips in thin white curtains that dissolve long before reaching ground.

The [[Sky Glass]] itself forms in layered shelves across the highest plateaus, simultaneously delicate in appearance and impossible to shatter, its surface fractal-threaded with the frozen geometry of dendrites, spiraling forms, and snowflake architectures repeating at every scale. The mountain is said to be quietly demonstrating the same pattern at every magnification, forever.

**The [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] do not offer wisdom, power, or salvation. They offer only friction and the resistance that forces pilgrims to become more than they were.**

Culturally, they are a place of "do or die" for anyone seeking a deep understanding of oneself. These are the idyllic places that create or break a person anew. It is one of the most profound personal journeys in [[Arcanoria]]. Its structure is divided by height into these core regions:

- [[Sky Glass]] Ascent: The uppermost zone and accessible by very few, the [[Grand Thread Rings]] entire structure of its [[Fractal Kaleidoscope]]s is seen at every layer of the ascent. In the [[Strand Pool]]s, the clearest [[Sky Glass]] forms in layered shelves. [[Coherence]] is at its peak here, but so is exposure. It is filled with towers, ramps, galleries and stairs of ice, raised and spun with the architecture of dreams. The air thins, and spells cast at this altitude risk triggering a localized [[Chaotic Resonant Cascade]] that can shatter the nearby [[Sky Glass]] formations and changing the topology of entire passes. The internal corridors of the ascent change continuously making it mandatory to carry a [[Resonance Stabilizer]] and a [[Celestial Astrolabe]] to guide the path. 

- Whispering Gallery: Rows of natural amphitheaters carved with perfect acoustics. Very jagged fractal shapes with both high and low [[Coherence]] which tend to make [[Spellweaving]] unreliable. The inner galleries shift quickly in topology. Here, a whisper at one end can be heard clearly at the other — it is said that the mountain answers. This is the typical end of pilgrimages. Legend holds that when a pilgrim speaks their original [[Motif Awakening]] Wish aloud, the Gallery answers: if the root of fulfilling your wish lies in the future, the sound rings in [[Consonance]] as a sign to press onward. If it lies in the past, [[Discordant Interference]] shatters the sound as a sign to turn inward, to fix what has not yet been resolved. If drinking concentrated [[Lunehymn]], it is also possible to ask the mountain questions about the own's unconscious and the [[Consonance]] or [[Dissonance]] reply back as a yes or a no, which makes it useful for [[Piety]] and Revelation for oneself.

- Crescent Veils: Mist-shrouded ridges, glacial terraces, twilight-blooming frost flowers with zones of high [[Coherence]] and [[Leylines]] converging. The mist here is thickest; it is also the most dangerous. Breathing it too long induces [[Lunehymn]] Narcosis that induce the vivid hallucinations of past pilgrims' deaths, forcing you to relive their final moments regardless of where these occurred, this is the maximum point the [[Lunehymn]] mist touches, and it has a lot of [[Aetherlight]] rays that collide with one another nullifying the ascent of more mist.

- Silver Cascades: The Upper Habitable Zone, primarily for [[Secluded Refuge]]s and [[Sacred Site]]s. The name stems from the [[Lunehymn]] waterfalls threading through [[Sky Glass]], in visible fractal light mirroring stained glass with iridescent flickers. These zones are known for having [[Time Bubble]]s, identifiable by reflections hanging suspended in the cloudy ice. While most of them are visible as actual bubbles that go upward with the cascades, some of them are large enough that a person can get trapped into the [[Time Bubble]] experiencing the normal phenomenon within these [[Strand Pool]]s.

- Boreal Reach: The Lower Habitable Zone and Entry to the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]]. The characteristic trees and [[Glimmerfern]] fronds give its signature name. On the upward ascent, when almost reaching the silver cascades, the trails become deliberately misleading, with switchbacks double back, forcing climbers to trust memory, and [[Absolute Certainty]] over sight. There are some regions with [[Forsaken Flower]]s that paint the scene with wounded red amongst the snow.

- Fracture Seams: The lower zones below the Boreal Reach where [[Consonance]] and [[Dissonance]] clash, silver mist rising. Here, the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]]' famous [[Coherence]] gradient is most violent featuring the most of the famous "Magic Dead Zones" where spells fail on the border of many [[Dissonance]] Spikes where spells backfire catastrophically. The ground itself is unstable; the seams shift with the iconic mirrored pools of fractals.

- [[Lunar Abyss]]: Impossible chasm, no sounded bottom, the place where warm mist is rising. Filled with mirrored fractal pools, and past a certain point in depth "up" and "down" become indistinguishable. Paradoxically, it is the zone of the highest [[Vibrational Density]] of the entire [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] when its inner layers align, even more than the summit of the [[Sky Glass]] ascent. The mirrored pools here are massively large and reflect not the sky above, but a different sky with [[Constellation]]s that do not match the stars visible to the climber. Drinking from one of these pools is said to grant a glimpse of the future, always true, never complete, always terrible. For this reason, the [[Lunar Abyss]] has become a favored site for [[Chorus Pillar]] [[Prophet]]s seeking communion with [[Outer God]]s, though it is impossible to determine what answers from below, and is rarely what they intended to reach. _"Go down enough and you will reach the heavens. They are so above as below, as within, as without." - [[Aurelian]], the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Resonance]]._

**The [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] experience periodic [[Resonance]] Tides** which are waves of high [[Coherence]] that sweep up from the [[Lunar Abyss]] like the tide coming in, following the [[Moon Phase]]. During a Tide, [[Spellweaving]] is amplified; [[Atonalis]] tend to hide and during the Ebb, Magic Dead Zones expand, and demons hunt. A "King Tide" occurs every [[Cycle]], coinciding with the [[Phase of Zenith]], and this is considered both the optimal time for pilgrimage and also the most dangerous.

On the [[Sky Glass]] Ascent, [[Legend]]s speak of a **Phantom Summit**, a peak visible only in peripheral vision, vanishing when directly observed. Those who have reached it return with no memory of the ascent of this last phantom summit, only a profound sense of peace and a single new insight about their [[Motif Awakening]] Wish, and a fragment of [[Sky Glass]] in their pocket that was not there before. Whether this final part is real, a collective hallucination, or the mountain dreaming of itself, no one knows. However, the [[Sonata]] of the Pale [[Moon]] proves this place exists and it is actually necessary to establish one of the [[World-Bending Relics]] in the Phantom Summit to peer the veil of the [[Parlor of the Moon]] and allow for the reunion of [[Selenea]] and the [[Auric Aria]].

Theologically, they are very similar to the Mirrored Waterfalls of the [[Parlor of the Moon]]. It was the place that the [[Auric Aria]] wove for [[Selenea]] as a physical anchor in [[Arcanoria]] which explains the abundance of [[Lunehymn]].

After [[Lacrimosa]] and the [[Law of Relics]] they are a prime place for gathering [[Luceatfilum]] given the amount of seams and transitions they have.

Despite the mountain being filled with [[Sky Glass]] it is commonly referred to as "false [[Sky Glass]]" because of two reasons:

- The [[Sky Glass]] of the mountain loses most of its properties the moment it is severed from the mountain, it's almost as if the [[Sky Glass]] is part of the living system of the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]], severing its connection makes it degrade into dust.

- Real [[Sky Glass]] has the property of being flexible which makes it impossible to get cut with it. The [[Sky Glass]] on the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] however is mostly jagged and doesn't have the tactile properties that real [[Sky Glass]] does. Only certain sections of the higher regions have real [[Sky Glass]], and the way to recognize it is through [[Resonance]].

### The Idyllic and Fatal Paradise

_"Pilgrims climb the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] for many reasons: to test the truth of their [[Motif Awakening]] seeking direction, to a glimpse of their own future in the [[Lunar Abyss]]' pools, to prove their [[Mastery Over Chaos]] — or simply to discover whether they are strong enough to survive a place that does not care whether they live or die. Most of them find the latter in suicidal missions." - [[The White-Touched Archivist]]_

From a distance, the range seems almost gentle, softened by perpetual crescent-shaped veils of mist curling around each summit like sleeping breath, aurora-flickers of residual [[Coherence]] painting the night sky above glacial terraces where frost-shrouded flowers bloom only in twilight. The air tastes of iron and ancient magic. That gentleness is a lie mesmerized by the breathtaking beauty of the inner chambers that pool near the ledges of each waterfall. However, the most crucial detail is how jagged it also is in [[Coherence]]. There are zones of massive [[Coherence]] at the same time there are regions of [[Dissonance]] regarded as "Magic Dead Zones." The intense clash between the [[Lunehymn]] in the [[Leylines]] at the edges of these seams between [[Consonance]] and [[Dissonance]] causes a rise of mist of silver dust that gives the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] its iconic name.

Beneath the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]], invisible until the edge is already underfoot, lies the [[Lunar Abyss]]: an impossible chasm splitting the mountain's heart at the lowest point of the crescent's inner bowl. No bottom has ever been sounded. Its walls are smooth in places, unnervingly so, as though something vast once passed through them, serrated in others as stacked overhangs folding into one another until all sense of scale dissolves and the eye finds nothing to hold.

The waterfalls threading through [[Sky Glass]] do not stop at the chasm's edge. They pour into it, vanishing into a darkness that only becomes visible through the concentrated [[Lunehymn]] that begins creating ripples of light that create a reflection of the cosmos in the same pattern as the Burning Ship Fractal. This gives the iconic name of [[Lunar Abyss]] to the lower levels. These pools are the result of the warm mist that rises from below, warm enough to melt snow on contact, creating strange microclimates where ice and vapor spiral together in forms that mirror the [[Auric Geometry]] of the peaks above, as though the [[Lunar Abyss]] and the summit are the same structure inverted, each dreaming of the other. 

The sound of running water, however, persists beneath the ice everywhere on the mountain: promising, treacherous, eternal. The direct connection of [[Lunehymn]] in large quantities is why they are usually regarded as a [[Sacred Site]] for any prophets of the [[Chorus Pillar]]. While the climb is a pilgrimage that creates [[Religion]], these peaks care little for [[Humanity]]. They simply persist, jagged, luminous, indifferent, in the sublime and the lethal share the exact same ground, every waterfall a fracture, every idyllic basin a lid over the [[Lunar Abyss]] below, offering the only gift they have ever given anything that dared to climb them: **the friction that creates [[Ballad]]s.**

#### The Prelude

This is the first major moment of convergence for the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Ages]] II, although they arrive as strangers, without any [[Legend Title]]s properly yet, and for entirely different reasons:

- [[Luminaire]] and [[Sephira]] arrive at the border of the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] Reach by the Eastern Ridge. [[Sephira]] is a bounty hunter that was tasked to escort [[Luminaire]] in her mission, she was sent because she had gained fame during a tavern brawl where she acquired the title of the cornered fire rat.

- [[Orphael]] arrives alone from the South to the Boreal Reach following the [[Legend]] of the [[Lunehymn]] [[Strand Pool]] spring and a deep desire to test himself to stop feeling the crushing shame of his [[Lustful]] nature colliding with his [[Empathetic]] [[Legend Trait]]. He knew the risks and from [[Ballad]]s heard that the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] offer in the Whispering Gallery clarity about oneself, he was on a suicidal mission to understand in his do or die for personal growth as pilgrimage.

- [[Cordelia]] is following [[Amadea]] because they had met earlier during one of [[Amadea]]'s plays, [[Amadea]] brusquely told her to go away. [[Cordelia]] decided they are friends anyway and simply never left.
