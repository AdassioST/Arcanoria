_CRX - CONTAIN. RESEARCH. EXPLOIT._

Also known as the Institutionalized Organisms of Suffering or [[Atonalis]] Containment Facilities which are commonly called CRX Facilities because any equipment tied to them usually begins with the CRX suffix like [[CRX Product]]s describing [[Atonalis Technology]] and its byproducts like [[Velvet Nectar]].

[[Civilization]] can engage to a varying degree with the [[Institute]]s shaping directly ages like the [[Age of Velvet Elegance]] which uses the [[Parasite Atonalis]] economy by either becoming a patron of [[Enclave]]s and [[Institute]]s or by integrating them directly for accessing [[Atonalis Technology]].

They are human-made organisms evolved from [[Dissonance]] and [[Cusp]] [[Enclave]]s that use [[Atonalis]] and human suffering as means to acquire anomalous properties and further their specific agenda. The earliest [[Institute]]s emerge organically in [[Ages]] IV from the existential need to understand the [[Atonalis]], the crisis of the [[Morpheus Abysmal]] during the [[Atonalis]] Reign in [[Ages]] IV, and several forms of proto-[[Dissonance]] experimentation rooted in cruelty and survival.

The early [[Institute]]s are not born as noble academies, but as practical monstrosities. They arise from the remnants of research facilities, coliseums, hidden orders, proto-[[Dissonance League]] [[Enclave]]s, the [[Court of Delicacies]], the Slayers' Amphitheater, and covert societies such as the Owl society of [[Amadea]], all of which begin accumulating live [[Atonalis]] specimens as subjects for study.

Despite their name, [[Institute]]s are deliberately cruel places of furthering knowledge and [[Humanity]] by inducing states of torture, controlled fracture, and engineered suffering. The Velvet Cage is a prominent [[Dissonance]] [[Enclave]] that begins the production of [[Velvet Nectar]] out of subjugated [[Spellweaver]]s, and it becomes one of the most infamous ancestors of institutionalized suffering.

Most [[Institute]]s are self-destructive organisms because they rely upon the study, commodification, and weaponization of forces born from suffering while simultaneously producing more of that same suffering inside their walls. In practice, this means many become overrun by the very quantity of [[Atonalis]] they help create, eventually collapsing into [[Atonalis Nest]]s or no-man's-land ruins.

All [[Civilization]]s tend to despise [[Institute]]s because they deal with dangerous knowledge and require inhumane conditions to function. They represent the embodiment of the [[Weight of Value]] by disregarding the human condition in favor of outcomes, efficiency, and survival at any cost.

During the fracture of the [[Dissonance League]], [[Institute]]s diverge into three broad trajectories: containment facilities, commodified use of [[Atonalis]], and deliberate weaponization of [[Atonalis]] as unpredictable non-discriminate tools of warfare ​. This fragmentation is one of the reasons they never become a stable scientific tradition, but rather a lineage of cursed institutions feared even by their creators.

A prominent [[Institute]] was formed from the exiled members of the [[Obsidian Feather Society]] of [[Amadea]] under the guise of "We die in the silence so that you may sing of tomorrow." Even when founded under defensive ideals, the containment breaches of these places tend to have catastrophic consequences, because the same secrecy that protects the outside world also conceals decay within ​.

There is an instance of an [[Institute]] that conditioned traumatized children through repeated exposure to the sound of cracking [[Soul Leitmotif]]s as their companions turned into [[Atonalis]], making them identify the sound of fracture as the greatest possible sign of danger. This illustrates the moral core of the institution: it does not merely study suffering, it standardizes it into doctrine and trains others to live inside it.

[[Institute]]s tend to be considered godforsaken places that the [[Auric Aria]] heavily despises due to the amount of cruelty inflicted on fellow humans under the justification that the ends justify the means at the complete disregard of human life. Even by the brutal standards of crisis ages, they are viewed as places where [[Humanity]] begins to imitate the worst instincts of the cosmos in the name of protecting itself.

Their original purpose is epidemiological: understanding how a [[Soul Leitmotif]] fractures into a [[Dissonance Core]], learning the nature and anomalous properties of [[Atonalis]] as means to create technology or refine byproducts like [[Velvet Nectar]], and eventually developing countermeasures against demonic threats . From this purpose comes the paradox of the [[Institute]]: some of the most vital knowledge on survival is discovered in the most morally bankrupt places in [[Arcanoria]].

### Formation of the [[Institute]]s and the [[Dissonance League]]

The first true wave of [[Institute]] formation begins in [[Ages]] IV during the instability caused by the [[Atonalis]] Reign and the empowerment of the [[Morpheus Abysmal]]. This age floods the world with hostile [[Atonalis]], overrun territories, experimental [[Dissonance]] [[Enclave]]s, and crisis-driven attempts to understand how suffering transforms into organized predation.

These early forms come from several places at once rather than a single founding state. [[Iridia]], [[Amadea]]'s [[Shadow Order]], proto-[[Dissonance]] [[Enclave]] experimentation, the [[Court of Delicacies]], militant [[Civic]] traditions, and regional facilities built around live specimen capture all contribute to the emergence of the first institutional containment cultures. 

However, the real foundation of the [[Institute]]s is in [[Ages]] V, with the formation of the [[Dissonance League]] through the [[Great Sovereign]] of [[Miss Nyctilia]] who creates the first intercity consortium of [[Enclave]]s that would become formal [[Institute]]s with shared methods, terminology, and procedural doctrine. This moment produces the illusion of scientific stabilization right before the first major inter-facility breach cascade proves that any network linking multiple failed containment sites only multiplies catastrophe.

The collapse of the [[Dissonance League]] is therefore not a side note but a defining event in the identity of the [[Institute]]. The modern image of the [[Institute]] is born from failure: collapsed leagues, overrun halls, sealed wings, cruel handlers, and procedures written in the blood of those who classified too little too late.

#### Their Founding Response and [[Atonalis Technology]]

An [[Institute]] is a research, containment, and study facility oriented around the capture and examination of [[Atonalis]], which are entities of [[Consciousness]] without [[Coherence]] that cannot be attuned. Everything in the institution is built around one question: how can [[Humanity]] survive things that should not be survivable.

Their work quickly expands beyond mere observation. [[Institute]]s develop procedures for containment, suppression, trigger mapping, byproduct extraction, [[Atonalis]]-derived technologies, field neutralization, and in more corrupt branches, the deliberate manufacture or deployment of [[Atonalis]] as strategic weapons.

This makes the [[Institute]] both a laboratory and a siege mentality turned into architecture. It is where the fear of being helpless before chaos becomes a bureaucratic machine.

[[Institute]]s use what later becomes known as [[Atonalis Technology]], a field of applied practice derived from the empirical study of [[Dissonance Core]]s, [[Resonance Anchors]], behavioral triggers, and the residual logic of suffering encoded into an entity's wound-pattern.

This technology includes various applications of [[Field Magic]], [[Cymatics Arts]], [[Resonance]] dampeners, [[Dissonance]] shielding, specialized observation chambers, trigger inventories, byproduct extraction protocols, and measurement instruments capable of estimating the concentration of [[Resonance Anchors]] within an entity's [[Dissonance Core]]. It also includes the procedural knowledge necessary to interact with entities whose behavior is stable only when precise ritualized conditions are met.

The most important conceptual breakthrough is that an [[Atonalis]] is not classified only by how dangerous it is, but by how many resources it takes to safely study, contain, and if necessary neutralize it. This insight becomes the basis of the [[Dissonance]] Containment Protocol ([[DCP]]) and distinguishes the [[Institute]] from ordinary hunters, exorcists, or battlefield doctrine.

### The [[Dissonance]] Containment Protocol ([[DCP]])

The [[Institute]] Containment System — formally the [[Dissonance]] Containment Protocol ([[DCP]]) — is the foundational procedural framework developed by the earliest proto-[[Institute]]s during [[Ages]] IV and later codified as the [[Dissonance League]]'s standard in [[Ages]] V by [[Miss Nyctilia]].

It draws simultaneously from the empirical study of [[Atonalis]] behavior, breakthroughs in measuring [[Resonance Anchors]] within [[Dissonance Core]]s, the analysis of [[Rose Seed]]s left after neutralization, and the catastrophic lessons of facilities overrun by entities misclassified on their containment requirements that are only studied through [[Memory Field]]s of their remnants.

The system's single most vital function is Resource Accuracy. Any entity classified lower than its true requirement does not merely receive insufficient tools; it receives no tools capable of stopping it, and the cost is almost always death ​.

By [[Ages]] V, the [[Dissonance League]] adopts the [[DCP]] as its common language of survival. The protocol becomes mandatory only after multiple containment breaches transform linked facilities into [[Atonalis Nest]]s during the wider chaos of the [[Holy War]], proving that procedural ambiguity is itself a lethal vector.

#### The Triad of Classification Structure: [[CRL]], [[DTL]] and [[CU]]

A full [[Institute]] entry is built on multiple axes rather than a single danger rating. This prevents the common mistake of assuming that a manageable entity is harmless, or that a destructive entity is difficult to pacify.

Every proper entry should track at minimum the following: the resources needed to study and contain it which is the Containment Resource Level ([[CRL]]), the scale of destruction it can cause if unrestrained which is the [[Dissonance]] Threat Level [[DTL]], and the quantity of latent [[Resonance Anchors]] in its [[Dissonance Core]] which is measured in [[Consciousness]] Units [[CU]], alongside the behavioral taxonomy of the [[Eight-Born Paths]] that governs how its nature, how it feeds and responds to stimuli.

In practical terms, this means an entity may be easy to pacify but catastrophic when ignored, or difficult to contain while possessing only narrow destructive range. The protocol is designed around this asymmetry to prevent chaos.

#### Primary Classification: Containment Resource Level ([[CRL]])

This is the most critical axis of an entry and study of any type of [[Atonalis]]. It answers the core question of containment:

- _How many resources, personnel, and infrastructure are required to safely study, contain, and neutralize this entity?_

Misclassification downward is a death sentence, while over-classification wastes resources that a smaller facility simply does not have. The [[CRL]] exists in 5 levels according to containment capacity and procedural requirements which somewhat mirrors the nature of [[Atonalis]] development from [[Nascent]] to [[Primal Discordia]].

#### [[CRL]]-1 — Near Passive Containment

**Personnel Required:** Any researcher with basic safety orientation, no [[Spellweaving]] required.

**Infrastructure:** Usually any sealed physical room with a reinforced observation window, standard lock mechanisms, and minimal types of [[Cymatics Arts]] required.

**Containment Logic:** The entity's [[Dissonance Core]] is too [[Nascent]] or inactive to generate [[Discordant Interference]] capable of breaching significant physical matter. Its anomalous properties are limited to close proximity or contact. Researchers observe from behind windows; direct handling is possible with protective gloves and any type of magic dampener, but is not recommended without supervision​.

**Study Protocol:** Single researcher may document and log. Extraction of byproducts, if any, requires two personnel.

**Neutralization:** A single trained [[Spellweaver]] or even a capable civilian with the correct dampening tool, such as a tuned bell, a specific binding ritual, or a physical restraint, is sufficient.

**Misclassification Risk:** Low immediate danger; a [[CRL]]-1 that is secretly [[Sectile]] will exhaust its handler and begin feeding before the escalation is noticed. The most common warning sign is delayed parasitic [[Resonance]] appearing in behavior logs.

**Example:** Many types of [[Formless Masses]] that are still in their early territorial posturing phase, showing limited predatory drive and no pseudo-[[Spellweaving]] capability yet. They are manageable and easy to contain and study ​.

#### [[CRL]]-2 — Structured Handling

**Personnel Required:** A trained [[Institute]] Handler who has completed one to three months of [[Eight-Born Paths]] specific certification. No advanced [[Spellweaving]] is required, but basic [[Resonance]] literacy is mandatory.

**Infrastructure:** A designated containment room with dampening walls, an observation anteroom, and a documented interaction protocol specific to the entity. Trigger inventory must be stored on-site and updated after every interaction [[Cycle]].

**Containment Logic:** The entity has developed behavioral patterns rooted in its wound and responds to specific stimuli, but those stimuli are still learnable and reproducible. It can be dangerous if mishandled yet can remain stable when its ritual requirements are followed with precision.

**Study Protocol:** Minimum of two personnel, one handler and one observer with documentation duty. Sessions are time-limited and followed by review for contamination, trigger drift, and emotional bleed.

**Neutralization:** One experienced [[Spellweaver]] with [[Eight-Born Paths]] specific knowledge, or two standard [[Spellweaver]]s acting in coordination.

**Misclassification Risk:** Moderate. A [[CRL]]-2 that is secretly [[CRL]]-3 often appears manageable for weeks until its fracture trigger activates instability. Most deaths at this level come from routine turning into over-familiarity.

**Example:** The lower-state variant of [[The Cradle of Echoes]] whose spatial tendrils are present but limited may remain stable under strict handling conditions. It does not require heavy infrastructure to calm it, but failure to apply the correct pacification ritual can still allow it to devastate a settlement.

#### [[CRL]]-3 — Expert Custody

**Personnel Required:** A path specialist, meaning a researcher dedicated to the specific entity and its [[Eight-Born Paths]] behavior. They are not interchangeable, because the specialist knows the entity's wound, quirks, fracture triggers, and interaction language ​.

**Infrastructure:** Dedicated chamber with stronger dampening, ritual preparation antechamber, trigger-locked storage for associated relics or cursed objects, and remote observation only. This is usually the first level where normal glass observation is abandoned ​.

**Containment Logic:** The entity exhibits partially self-directed anomalous properties and pseudo-[[Spellweaving]]. It may sense intent, emotional states, and the [[Resonance Anchors]] of nearby personnel, meaning containment becomes ritualized rather than merely physical ​.

**Study Protocol:** Minimum of three personnel, one specialist, one assistant, and one emergency handler outside the chamber. Session logs are mandatory and reviewed by senior staff.

**Neutralization:** A group of three to five [[Spellweaver]]s with specific briefing on the entity's path and behavioral triggers .

**Misclassification Risk:** High. A [[CRL]]-3 mislabeled as [[CRL]]-2 is one of the most common sources of mass-casualty incidents in early [[Institute]] history, because it appears bureaucratically excessive until the entity enters a truly unstable state and the compounded breach causes the fall of the entire facility.

**Example:** A [[Fracted]] [[Obsessian]] with recursive spatial loops can trap handlers inside compulsive patterns that only a specialist fully understands how to interrupt. In these cases, expertise is itself a containment apparatus.

#### [[CRL]]-4 — Coordinated Team Containment

**Personnel Required:** A dedicated multi-team structure composed of containment, study, and emergency response teams that train together and rotate frequently. No single individual should hold the entire operating burden of the entity.

**Infrastructure:** A designated wing of the [[Institute]] with layered dampening, independent sub-chamber controls, redundant suppression systems, and full containment corridors. This is no longer a room but a structured operational environment.

**Containment Logic:** The entity has reached [[Ascendant]]-grade operational danger. Its anomalous properties can affect entire corridors or sectors, and its pseudo-[[Spellweaving]] may approximate the destructive complexity of higher-order casting.

**Study Protocol:** Weekly cross-team reviews are mandatory. Containment failure drills are routine, and at least one specialist must remain on call at all times.

**Neutralization:** Usually requires a large coordinated group of [[Spellweaver]]s acting under shared tactical doctrine. Improvised attempts are close to suicidal.

**Misclassification Risk:** Catastrophic. A [[CRL]]-4 treated as a lower class rarely produces a recoverable breach and often becomes the event that ends a facility​.

A related danger at this level is cumulative exposure of staff to the wound-pattern of the entity. Some [[Institute]] traditions describe this as [[Dissonance]] Sedimentation, the process by which prolonged exposure causes handlers to begin mirroring the entity's path psychologically and behaviorally.

#### [[CRL]]-5 — Dedicated Facility Containment

**Personnel Required:** The entity does not merely have a team; it has a branch of the [[Institute]] dedicated to it. Personnel are selected, trained, rotated, and psychologically managed for the sole purpose of preventing escalation, reunion, propagation, or partial release.

**Infrastructure:** A dedicated facility or a facility-scale containment complex with the highest level of shielding, isolation, atmospheric separation, multiple independent systems, and often regional distance between linked sub-sites.

**Containment Logic:** Full containment is often impossible. The purpose of [[CRL]]-5 is not perfect imprisonment, but partial containment, suppression of escalation, and prevention of catastrophic conditions that would allow the entity to complete itself​.

**Study Protocol:** Largely remote whenever possible. Direct interaction is rare, heavily scripted, and treated as a strategic risk rather than normal research.

**Neutralization:** Considered functionally impossible through normal means and often dependent on age-defining campaigns, legendary intervention, or conditions too costly to reproduce which are usually considered crisis.

**Misclassification Risk:** Existential. A [[CRL]]-5 entity mistaken for a lower class does not merely breach a chamber, it rewrites the fate of the facility and whatever region stands around it.

The clearest example is [[Lyd, The First Beloved]], whose six [[Beloved Shard]]s must be kept separated across dedicated sub-facilities with the utmost emphasis placed on preventing the [[Interregnum]] from hatching. and the [[Beloved Kernel]] from forming. In this case containment is not about a single monster in a room, but about preventing a distributed apocalyptic birth condition from ever completing itself.

#### Secondary Classification: [[Dissonance]] Threat Level ([[DTL]])

The second major axis measures how much harm an entity can cause if containment fails completely. This is separate from the resource cost of holding it, because some entities are easy to pacify yet devastating when neglected, while others are difficult to contain or handle but limited in raw area of destruction.

This axis is often logged through practical destruction bands such as Personal, District, Regional, National, or [[Civilization]]al threat. The exact language may vary by [[Institute]], but the purpose remains the same: to express the maximum scale of harm that an unrestrained entity can produce if given the opportunity to feed.

This distinction is essential to the realism of the system. A level that requires little containment may still be capable of obliterating a town if the single pacification condition is ignored, which is exactly the kind of asymmetry the [[DCP]] was built to recognize. One example is an [[Atonalis]] in its [[Fracted]] state that may behave depressed until it reaches the breaking object of its original wound and goes volatile in its rampage to end its own existence while bringing down anything alongside itself. The [[CRL]] is of Level 2 because it only requires the separation from the breaking object, but the [[DTL]] is much higher than regular [[CRL]]-2 entities.

#### Tertiary Classification: [[Consciousness]] Units ([[CU]])

The third major axis measures the amount of latent power and [[Resonance]] of consumed [[Consciousness]] contained within an entity's [[Dissonance Core]] through its concentration of [[Resonance Anchors]]. [[Institute]]s standardize this through the use of [[CU]], or [[Consciousness]] Units, which serve as a procedural estimate of stored power and escalation risk.

[[CU]] is not identical to destructive scale, because an entity can possess a relatively modest reserve of stored power while still expressing highly lethal anomalous behavior. Instead, [[CU]] is most useful for tracking development, feeding progression, instability, hidden growth, and potential fuel discharge while in captivity.

The rough procedural ranges usually mirror the developmental stages of [[Atonalis]]: lower CU for [[Nascent]], rising through [[Sectile]], surging through [[Fracted]], and becoming extreme by [[Ascendant]] and [[Primal Discordia]]. After neutralization, these estimates can be confirmed more reliably through the rarity, purity, and beauty of the resulting [[Rose Seed]] once the entity has been vanquished.

Finally, under [[CU]], it also identifies the entity according to the [[Eight-Born Paths]], which govern wound-pattern, feeding behavior, predatory logic, and the type of psychological contamination it imposes on handlers.

This means the [[Dissonance]] Containment Protocol is never universal across all [[Atonalis]]. A [[Signath]] cannot be approached under the same assumptions as a [[Carnalix]], and an [[Erosyx]] requires entirely different safeguards than an [[Obsessian]] or [[Animach]], and it becomes even more nuanced when dealing with hybrid [[Atonalis]].

For this reason, every serious [[Institute]] entry tracks path identity as operational doctrine rather than flavor text. The path determines what stimuli matter, how the entity escalates, what sort of personnel are vulnerable, and what kind of error is most likely to kill the team.

#### Facility Clearance of [[Institute]]s

_"You can build an entire Grade IV facility around a [[CRL]]-1. You cannot build a [[CRL]]-1 facility around a Grade IV entity. The direction of error is everything." — [[Miss Nyctilia]]._

Not all [[Institute]]s can handle all classifications. This is one of the core practical truths of the [[DCP]]: a small site may successfully study low-resource entities for years and still be completely incapable of surviving a single transfer above its grade.

In practice, facilities develop internal clearances comparable to the distinction between ordinary hazardous study and fully isolated existential containment. The more advanced the entity, the more the building itself becomes part of the containment mechanism.

This also means that the decision to transfer an entity is never trivial. Sending a specimen to a site below its effective containment capacity is not an administrative mistake but an execution order written by paperwork.

The [[Institute]] Grade Clearances run on 4 levels:

- **Grade I Facility**: Cleared for [[CRL]]-1 and [[CRL]]-2 entities only. Most frontier outposts and smaller research stations.

- **Grade II Facility**: Cleared for [[CRL]]-1 through [[CRL]]-3. Requires a certified Path Specialist on permanent staff.

- **Grade III Facility**: Cleared for [[CRL]]-1 through [[CRL]]-4. Requires dedicated containment wing, multi-team structure, and [[Dissonance]] Sedimentation monitoring program for dealing with large quantities of [[Formless Masses]].

- **Grade IV Facility**: Cleared for all levels including [[CRL]]-5. There are very few of these. Each [[CRL]]-5 entity typically requires that the facility is built around it, not that the entity is transported to an existing facility.

Finally, a formal [[Institute]] entry reads as follows:

**[[Eight-Born Paths]]**: [[Discant]]

**[[CRL]]**: 3 — Expert Custody

**[[CU]]**: Approximately 614 [[CU]] on [[Fracted]].

**[[Fracted]] Trigger Object**: The family cloth woven by her son, [[Eyras]] — logged as [[Cursed Objects]], stored in trigger-locked Class II vault, sub-facility annex, remotely stored from containment chamber.

**Behavioral Notes**: Entity does not hunt physically. Its presence generates aura of recursive grief that drains lifeforce from nearby beings. It does not leave its original site. Providing a functioning loom within the chamber produces sustained behavioral pacification. Removing the loom precipitates an acute [[DTL]] escalation event within 48 [[Bar]]s.

**Neutralization Estimate**: 3–5 [[Spellweaver]]s, path-briefed. Priority target: [[Dissonance Core]] found within its lower chin. Entry of the fracture trigger object ([[Eyras]]'s cloth) into the chamber will trigger immediate [[Fracted]] escalation — this is an absolute containment prohibition.

**Facility Clearance Required**: Grade [[CRL]]-3 certified facility minimum. This entity cannot be transferred to a [[CRL]]-1 or [[CRL]]-2 facility regardless of its apparent behavior during stable periods.

### The Danger of [[CRL]] Misclassification & Emergency Breaches

_"Complacency kills faster than ignorance." — [[Miss Nyctilia]]_

The greatest danger in the entire system is not that an [[Atonalis]] is powerful, but that it is classified incorrectly. The [[CRL]], and the [[DCP]] at large, exists because the history of the failed and overrun [[Institute]]s proved that underestimation kills faster than ignorance.

A low-resource entity can still possess enormous destructive capability if its pacification condition is narrow. A high-[[CU]] entity may still be manageable if its ritual conditions are fully understood. A seemingly stable entity may become unrecoverable the moment a fracture object, emotional trigger, or path-specific stimulus enters the chamber.

The foundational lesson repeated across [[Institute]] doctrine is simple: one may overbuild around a lesser threat and survive the waste, but one cannot under build around a greater threat and survive the correction. The direction of error is everything. Those who fail to grasp becomes a cautionary tale sung in the [[Memory Field]]s after the last of the [[Atonalis Nest]]s have become a tomb of [[Fated Flower]]s.

#### The Containment Response: The [[Emergency Breach Color Codex]]

The [[Emergency Breach Color Codex]] is the immediate response language used by [[Institute]]s developed thowhen the nature of the threat must be communicated faster than full procedural description allows. It exists to prevent delay, confusion, and dangerous curiosity during active failures to cut the catastrophe and have immediate accurate response to it.

The color code system exists because not all emergencies are the same kind of danger, and conflating them into a single general alarm has historically caused the wrong personnel to respond to the wrong threat with the wrong tools. A researcher trained to handle physical fire evacuation is not prepared to respond to an active [[Signath]] loose in the archive. A containment team trained for entity breach is not trained to follow [[Exogenesis Threat]] silence protocol. Each code addresses a fundamentally different survival logic.

The code list and priority of the [[Emergency Breach Color Codex]] is the following in descending order, from lowest priority to highest priority:

| Code             | Color                  | Threat Type                                                          | Response Priority                                 |
| ---------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| [[Code Gold]]    | [[Strand]] Gold        | All clear, normal operations restored                                | Administrative confirmation                       |
| [[Code Blue]]    | [[Flux]] Blue          | Medical emergency, personnel down                                    | Immediate medical response                        |
| [[Code Violet]]  | [[Void]] Violet        | Fire, structural hazard, topological surge                           | Physical evacuation                               |
| [[Code Verdant]] | [[Resonance]] Verdant  | Atmospheric toxicity, life support failure                           | Zone seal, sub-code protocol                      |
| [[Code Crimson]] | [[Cindergale]] Crimson | Hostile attack or active [[Atonalis]] breach                         | Immediate deterrence and path response by [[CRL]] |
| [[Code White]]   | [[Luminance]] White    | Information hazard, epistemic contamination                          | Information blackout, no new acquisition          |
| [[Code Pink]]    | [[Crystal]] Pink       | Active distress signal, network intervention required                | Endure, stall, preserve, await reinforcement      |
| [[Code Black]]   | [[Dissonance]] Black   | [[Exogenesis Threat]], under the watch of the [[Shrieking Terror]] | Nomenclature blackout, Exogenesis sub-protocol    |

Each color response is not only a hazard marker, but also a behavioral command. All personnel are trained to react to the color first and ask questions only after the situation is stable. In active breach conditions, specificity is often more dangerous than silence.

The codex can stack when multiple failures occur simultaneously, but the highest-priority code always governs the response order. In most cases, environmental codes outrank administrative concern, while epistemic threats outrank all ordinary evacuation logic. Any [[Code Black]] on an [[Exogenesis Threat]] is the single highest priority of all the codex that overrides all previous notices.

This code system was created by [[Miss Nyctilia]] and it uses her voice as the broadcasting system through her advanced mastery of [[Field Magic]], and [[Voice Projection Arts]]. It began with the first seven colors that introduced [[Code Black]] after the first major breach of the [[Dissonance League]] with the [[Shrieking Terror]] that she barely survived by singing the song of her homeland.

#### [[Code Gold]] — The Signal of Relief

[[Code Gold]] signifies that the breach has been resolved and the area has returned to monitored stability. All containment systems are intact, no active threat remains, and personnel may resume normal operation under standard surveillance.

[[Code Gold]] is not a passive default state. It is an active broadcast that must be confirmed by supervision before work continues. [[Institute]] tradition insists on this because many overrun facilities once mistook silence for safety.

You should:

- Verify the validity of the [[Code Gold]] and its origin.
- Resume standard duties only after official clearance has been confirmed.
- Verify that all seals, dampeners, and observation systems are functioning normally.
- Report any lingering damage, contamination, or irregular readings before re-entering routine work.
- Never assume the absence of alarms means the absence of danger.

**Standing maxim: Silence is not [[Code Gold]]. Only [[Code Gold]] is [[Code Gold]].**

Moreover, it's of utmost importance to verify whether the [[Code Gold]] signal is valid as there are some insidious and sufficiently intelligent [[Atonalis]] in higher facilities will try to weaponize the information to induce a false state of relief. Not all [[Code Gold]]s are issued by the highest clearance, some are the bait used by the [[Atonalis]] about to consume you.

The hijack of the signal of [[Code Gold]] was one of the main failures of the original [[Dissonance League]] from which all other [[Institute]]s after came to learn in the aftermath of the collapse during the [[Holy War]] of [[Ages]] V.

If this false [[Code Gold]] is detected by upper management, it will be issued as [[Code Gold]] Type 2 with an immediate secondary [[Code White]] saying _"CODE GOLD TYPE 2: THE ORIGINAL SIGNAL WAS FALSE. EXTREME PRECAUTION OF INFOHAZARDOUS ENTITIES. ALL FOLLOWING CODES ARE VOID"_

#### [[Code Blue]] — Personnel Medical Emergency

[[Code Blue]] signifies that a staff member requires immediate medical attention. This may include physical injury, [[Dissonance]] [[Corruption]] or contamination symptoms, unconsciousness, acute exhaustion, or any other sudden collapse of [[Composure]] or bodily health.

[[Code Blue]] is a response priority because personnel survival must be preserved before secondary containment tasks can continue. [[Healing Magic]] teams and regular physicians are expected to respond before the cause is fully understood, because in [[Institute]] environments delay kills more often than uncertainty does.

You should:

- Alert medical staff immediately.
- Stabilize the injured person if doing so does not expose you to additional harm.
- If given clearance and or having proper knowledge of [[Spellweaving]], use a basic application of [[Breathing Arts]] to stabilize the victim long enough for them to survive, and use any type of [[Field Medicine Arts]] for superficial wounds.
- Clear the surrounding area if the patient may be suffering from a contagious or anomalous condition. Avoid using [[Zephyr Arts]] indiscriminately.
- Avoid speculation about the cause until the patient is out of active danger.
- Never treat a medical event as minor until it has been formally assessed.

This code deliberately does not specify the cause of the medical event during an active incident. Naming a cause too early can contaminate the scene with assumptions before a medic has assessed the patient. Intervention is necessary but only on the minimum level to buy time while a specialized medic arrives.

#### [[Code Violet]] — Fire or Otherwise Structural Failure

[[Code Violet]] signifies all types of topological and spatial hazards, such as fire, heat breach, or severe structural failure inside the facility. This includes ordinary combustion, magical ignition, collapsing support systems, and any event that makes the structure itself unsafe to occupy such as the collapse of gateways.

The facility architecture of an [[Institute]] is designed to contain fires, and other hazards in the spatial vicinity that will not usually spread outside its area of origin. However, all these anomalies and incidents can be dangerous, and [[Code Violet]] is a physical survival code. Personnel are expected to evacuate through marked routes, avoid unstable corridors, gateways and not waste time attempting to recover documents, tools, or valuables.

You should:

- Remain where you are, provided it is safe to do so.
- Move away from the affected zone immediately if you smell any smoke.
- Do not attempt to fight any fires unless you have no other option.
- Use blast doors, safety hatches, and reinforced barriers whenever possible.
- Avoid gateways, compromised stairwells, or any route exposed to heat, collapse or any similar issues in the surrounding area.
- Seal all protection doors behind you if you are able to do so safely.
- Report the source of the crisis or failure to higher clearance as soon as possible.
- Never remain in a burning or collapsing sector in order to preserve property.

Because many [[Atonalis]] are sensitive to environmental disruption, an active [[Code Violet]] may secondarily destabilize contained entities and precede a breach code.

#### [[Code Verdant]] — Toxic Fields or Life Support Failure

[[Code Verdant]] signifies a life support failure, where the area has either become actively toxic, the atmosphere is no longer safe, or life support systems in [[Resonance Field]]s have failed. It may also indicate magical contamination, corrosive [[Dissonance]] spill, or respirator-grade hazard conditions of anomalous [[Atonalis]] properties.

[[Code Verdant]] is the only code with a mandatory full-zone seal. When it is declared, the environment itself is treated as hostile until proven otherwise.

You should:

- Seal your sector if you have the means to do so.
- Follow life support sub-codes immediately.
- Use respiratory protection, filtration systems, or sealed shelter protocols if available. 
- Use [[Field Magic]] as defensive measures considering primarily: [[Cymatics Arts]], [[Coherence Arts]], and [[Breathing Arts]].
- Avoid unnecessary movement through contaminated air or unstable pressure zones.
- Report visible leaks, fumes, residue, or pressure loss to staff of higher clearance.
- Never inhale, touch, or bypass the affected atmosphere unless explicitly ordered and equipped for it.

**Standing maxim: If you can smell the air, you have already been exposed.**

#### [[Code Crimson]] — Active Entity Breach or Hostile Attack

[[Code Crimson]] signifies an ongoing attack perpetrated by a hostile party. In a [[Code Crimson]], the facility automatically enters external lockdown, and or internal lockdown if the threat is internal.

A [[Code Crimson]] usually signifies that a contained [[Atonalis]] has breached its assigned boundaries and is active within the facility. This is the standard breach alarm for physical, anomalous, or predatory escape.

[[Code Crimson]] is the clearest declaration that the facility is under active assault by an escaped, uncontrolled entity or a rivalling faction. This may include hostile [[Atonalis]], unauthorized breach entities, or any force capable of directly endangering personnel through violence, penetration, or containment violation.

You should:

- Remain in your current sector, provided it is safe to do so.
- If the threat is nearby, take shelter behind blast doors, safety hatches, or other reinforced barriers, and place the sector in lockdown if possible.
- Use [[Silence Arts]] immediately to hide from the hostile entity.
- If given the ability, use [[Dimensional Arts]] or equivalent dimensional safety net as a last-resort protective measure through a [[Time Bubble]].
- Notify staff of higher clearance immediately if you have information on the position, movement, or identity of the threat.
- Never attempt to engage the threat on your own unless authorized to do so.
- Evacuate only if remaining in place would increase the risk of direct contact or escalation.

[[Code Crimson]] broadcasts always include the breached entity's if it came from an internal breach in the facility. It uses the [[CRL]] so that responding personnel know the resource scale of the threat. A Crimson-1 calls the nearest handler. A Crimson-3 clears the surrounding wing and calls the path specialist. A Crimson-5 triggers facility-wide lockdown and begins catastrophic event protocol.

#### [[Code White]] — Information Hazard

[[Code White]] signifies that the threat is dangerous to know, not merely dangerous to encounter. It is used for rogue [[Signath]], [[Obsessian]], epistemic hazards, memetic destabilizers, or any anomaly that causes harm through awareness, repetition, interpretation, or observation and misinformation.

[[Code White]] is a warning that the event itself may be contaminating thought. In a White event, knowledge is not power — it is infection, and it prompts the personnel to act on the Motto of: 

You should:

- Stop acquiring new information about the event immediately.
- Treat your own personal [[Coherence]] as the main guidance. Do not doubt your own senses, only what you feel is real, not what you see.
- Do not ask what happened, where it is, what it looks like, or how it got out.
- Follow only pre-briefed protocol and instructions already known before the alarm began.
- Avoid discussing the event with other personnel unless you are in a designated safe room.
- Treat written or spoken reports from the incident as contaminated until cleared.
- Never expose yourself to additional descriptions, recordings, or witness testimony unless authorized by higher clearance.

**Standing maxim: Feel, Use your gut. Never doubt your senses, no matter if reality doesn't make sense. [[Coherence]] is your lifeline.**

A [[Signath]] loose in any wing dissolves epistemic certainty, causing personnel to simultaneously believe contradictory things with equal conviction and lose trust in their own senses. An [[Obsessian]] in breach generates recursive behavioral zones, where personnel begin repeating actions without awareness and cannot exit a mental loop long enough to physically leave.

The worse case of a [[Code White]] is when it follows a [[Code Gold]]-2 response, as it means the entity is actively weaponizing the [[Emergency Breach Color Codex]] to its benefit, making all other broadcasts after dubious in information and null in validity.

#### [[Code Pink]] — Active Distress Signal

[[Code Pink]] signifies that the facility requires external support from the [[Institute]] network. It is a relational emergency code rather than a containment one, and it operates on two escalating sub-levels depending on the nature and severity of the need.

[[Code Pink]] is the only code whose primary audience is not the personnel inside the facility but the other [[Institute]]s in the wider network. It is a signal that the facility can no longer resolve its situation alone and is formally requesting intervention, reinforcement, or rescue.

**Pink-1 — Network Alert:** A hazard, pathogen, or condition originating inside the facility has the potential to spread between personnel or between sites. This sub-code signals the network to monitor incoming transfers, quarantine shared resources, and prepare for possible containment escalation across linked facilities. Internal response continues normally under the relevant active code.

**Pink-2 — Rescue and Reinforcement Request:** The facility is under active threat that exceeds its current capacity to manage alone. This may be triggered by a sustained [[Code Crimson]] that has overwhelmed containment teams, a [[Code Verdant]] or [[Code Violet]] event that has depleted enough personnel to compromise basic functions, or any crisis in which the facility's own resources cannot guarantee survival without external intervention. When Pink-2 is active, the facility formally places itself under network emergency jurisdiction.

You should:

- Continue following the active primary code alongside [[Code Pink]].
- Endure and stall until help arrives.
- Preserve as many personnel and as much documentation as possible for the incoming response team without inviting more danger.
- Avoid escalating engagement with the threat beyond what is necessary to buy time. Use any type of [[Field Magic]] to protect oneself.
- Keep communication lines open for incoming coordination with the network.
- Never assume that help is close. Assume that you must survive the interval alone.

A [[Code Pink]] usually transitions on the failure of a [[Code Crimson]] if the breach proves beyond the facility's neutralization capacity. In that case, the response priority of [[Code Pink]] does not replace [[Code Crimson]], it evolves to run alongside it as a secondary broadcast to the wider network. One of the most dangerous combinations is having a [[Code White]] and a [[Code Pink]] at once given that some of the information becomes dangerous which is why [[Code Pink]] must be treated carefully before intervention.

#### [[Code Black]] — [[Exogenesis Threat]]

[[Code Black]] is the highest priority order of the entire [[Emergency Breach Color Codex]]. It means that the [[Scorching Truth]]s of the facility have alerted the [[Shrieking Terror]], and the first of the [[Original Eight]] is hunting within or adjacent to the facility. All personnel must immediately reduce their information profile to the minimum necessary for survival and follow Exogenesis sub-protocols.

[[Code Black]] is the only emergency code in the [[DCP]] that does not name its threat directly in broadcast or documentation. This is not bureaucratic caution. It is the mechanism of survival itself. The [[Shrieking Terror]] is the [[Primal Discordia]] of the [[Anxithor]] path whose predatory logic is rooted in the presence of fear-saturated knowledge: it hunts by tracking [[Resonance]] signatures dense with terror, certainty about [[Scorching Truth]]s, and awareness of the specific nature of what is coming for it. The more a person knows about it — its name, its nature, how it kills, how it moves — the louder their [[Resonance]] signature burns to it, and the faster it arrives as a preemptive strike to erase its name from history.

The [[Exogenesis Threat]] protocol is therefore a system of deliberate informational self-reduction. Even the name itself is meant to be a loophole so that it doesn't alert the [[Shrieking Terror]]. It does not eliminate danger. It reduces the signal each individual emits by collapsing the volume of specific knowledge they actively hold.

Personnel are instructed to maintain one thought: _movement, not understanding._ 

The moment a person stops to think about what is hunting them, they have given it a louder signal. They have to repeat a simple lullaby to cloud their mind and prevent other thoughts while offering comfort and deceiving the [[Shrieking Terror]] into believing they no longer think or know about it.

_"Light above me, [[Moon]] beside me,  
Thread beneath me, song inside me.  
Across the sea, to lands unknown,  
Golden stars, keep watch at home.  
Auric light, and silver gleam,  
Carry me, in quiet dream.  
The dark may pass, the dark may flee,  
I remain, and so shall sea."_

This song is the [[Soul Leitmotif]] of [[Miss Nyctilia]].

**Sub-protocols are as follows:**

- **Exogenesis-1 | EXO 1 — Passive Reduction:** Broadcast on facility-wide tone. Personnel cease active discussion of any ongoing research. All open documentation is sealed. Study sessions are suspended. Personnel are told only that a threat is present and that standard [[Exogenesis Threat]] awareness applies. They are not told the nature of the threat, because the act of being told is itself a knowledge acquisition event that can make them the next target.

- **Exogenesis-2 | EXO 2 — Active Suppression:** Triggered when the threat has entered the facility perimeter. All personnel shift to need-only communication. The facility moves to acoustic dampening protocols where voices are suppressed, [[Silence Arts]] and [[Resonance]] shielding is reinforced to reduce the emotional broadcast of terror emanating from frightened staff. Personnel move in pairs, without verbal confirmation of what they are doing, following only color-coded physical floor markers and pre-memorized movement routes.

- **Exogenesis-3 | EXO 3 — Total Nomenclature Blackout:** Full communications lockdown. No entity is named in any broadcast. No event is described in specific terms. Incident reports during active Code Black use approved substitution language. Death caused by the [[Shrieking Terror]] is logged as spontaneous decoherence event or [[Instant Combustion Syndrome]]. A missing colleague is logged as "unscheduled relocation" and "indefinite vacations." A destroyed wing is an "atmospheric expansion" event. The substitution language is not designed to deceive investigators after the fact. It is designed to ensure that no one reading a live update during the event accumulates enough specific knowledge to become a high priority target for the [[Shrieking Terror]].

**Standing maxim: SING THE GOLDEN SONG.**

The approved broadcast phrase for [[Code Black]] during Exogenesis-3 is:

_"Something is in the building. Move to where the lights are golden. Do not ask why. Sing the golden song."_

This phrase is carefully constructed: it confirms a threat exists without naming it, it directs movement without describing the path, and it does not provoke the question of what specifically is hunting them. Asking what is hunting them is the last mistake many personnel make, and it offers comfort and a deception to the [[Shrieking Terror]] disguised as both a story and a prayer.

Personnel who survive Code Black events without injury are placed under a mandatory 72-hour information quarantine, during which they are not permitted to receive debriefs, read incident reports, or discuss the event with colleagues, because the instinct to understand what just happened is itself a post-event vulnerability to [[Resonance]] residue from the predator's passage.

The first recorded [[Code Black]] was filed as follows. Cause of death: spontaneous decoherence event, [[Instant Combustion Syndrome]] of three personnel. Estimated time of event: between the third and fourth [[Bar]]. Contributing factors: unknown. Facility status: all remaining personnel relocated under Exogenesis-3. Event duration: until something else drew the attention of the cause of the spontaneous decoherence event elsewhere.

The [[Shrieking Terror]]'s name does not appear in the report. It never does, as hoarding too much information of [[Scorching Truth]]s will make you the next target.

#### Legacy

The [[Institute]]s are one of the darkest expressions of [[Humanity]]'s survival instinct in [[Arcanoria]]. It is born from fear, sharpened by crisis, and justified by the claim that only cruelty organized into method can defend the world from what suffering has already created. The founders of the next generation of [[Institute]]s usually realize that these existed through the archeomagical exploration of [[Memory Field]]s of the intense trauma they left behind during and after their fall.

For this reason, [[Institute]]s are never purely villainous nor redeemable. They are places where real knowledge is extracted from unforgivable means, where some of the greatest tools against demonic catastrophe are discovered by people becoming machinery for the suffering they claim to fight​. 

Their legacy is therefore inseparable from contradiction. They preserve [[Humanity]] by becoming inhuman, contain monsters by learning from them, and defend the world while constantly risking becoming the next ruin that must itself be sealed shut before it too becomes a tomb of [[Forsaken Flower]]s atop a [[Memory Field]].
