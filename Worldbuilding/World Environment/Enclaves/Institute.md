#chaos #society

Also known as the Institutionalized Organisms of Suffering or [[Atonalis]] Containment Facilities.

They are human made societies that use the [[Atonalis]] and human suffering as means to acquire anomalous properties and further their specific agenda. The early [[Institute]]s are formed by the remnants of the [[Dissonance League]] after they inevitably collapsed.

Despite their name [[Institute]]s are deliberately cruel places of furthering knowledge and [[Humanity]] by inducing states of torture, the Velvet Cage is a prominent [[Dissonance]] [[Enclave]] that begins the production of [[Velvet Nectar]] out of subjugated [[Spellweaver]]s. Most [[Institute]]s are self destructive organisms because they become overrun by the sheer number of [[Atonalis]] made from collective suffering.

- All [[Civilization]]s tend to despise [[Institute]]s because they deal with dangerous knowledge and require inhumane conditions to function. They represent the embodiment of the [[Weight of Value]] by disregarding the human condition. During the fracture of the [[Dissonance League]], they came to become either containment facilities, commodified use of [[Atonalis]], and deliberate weaponization of [[Atonalis]] as unpredictable non discriminate tools of warfare.

- A prominent [[Institute]] was formed from the Owl society of [[Amadea]] under the guise of "We die in the dark so that you may live in the light." However, the containment breaches of these places tend to have catastrophic consequences.

- There is an instance of a [[Institute]] that conditioned traumatized children of hearing the crack of [[Soul Leitmotif]]s as a lot of his fellow friends turned into [[Atonalis]] and made him identify the cracking as the greatest sign of danger.

- [[Institute]]s tend to be considered god forsaken places that the [[Auric Aria]] heavily despises due to the amount of cruelty inflected on fellow humans under the justification that the ends justify the means at the complete disregard of human life.
