_"The Eight [[Atonalis]] that predate all life before life even existed in [[Arcanoria]]."_

The [[Primal Discordia]] that came from the original fracture of the [[Seven-Cycle Creation]]. These are the primordial [[Atonalis]] that date to before even life itself, and correspond one to each of the [[Eight-Born Paths]]. They are highly dangerous even for a [[Primal Discordia]], and represent the main fractures of the [[Auric Aria]]. 

The strongest of all [[Atonalis]] is the original [[Violux]] who killed the gentle beast by the [[Leylines]] and wounded the [[Auric Aria]] during the [[Eighth Cycle]], as her unfiltered [[Golden Ichor]] made it the strongest of all demons.

The conversation continues to use the sign, and [[Sympathetic Vibration]] tether while incantating the words in synesthesia that they've developed over the [[Cycle]]s: 

Vocalized section of the [[Morpheus Abysmal]]:

"Are... are you there, [[Ludwine]]?"

"I'm sorry, I lied to us both. The truth is..."

"Today is not my birthday. It's just that if I had a name... and a birthday... maybe we could... die like a real person... right?"

"The three of us. Happy, forever after"

"What is that look on your face? Come to think of it. We haven't had our cake yet."
