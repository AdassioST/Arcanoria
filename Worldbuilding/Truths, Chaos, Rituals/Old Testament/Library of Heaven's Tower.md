#crisis

One of the induced [[Genocides of the Auric Aria]] that was punishing the formation of early [[Institute]]s that were deliberately experimenting and torturing some parts of [[Humanity]] to develop knowledge on [[Atonalis]], from their hatching, formation, and anomalous properties. The [[Auric Order]] never liked these institutes given their pursue of knowledge through cruel experimentation on fellow humans, but the final crossing of the line came when the league of universities used [[Auric Angels]], the ascended [[Humanity]], for their experimentation.

The [[Auric Aria]] compounded trauma of denying [[Formless Masses]] and [[Atonalis]] as her creation while simultaneously turning both her [[Perfect Reflection]] and even worse her personally picked and most [[Piety]] imbued prophets into monsters were the final line that she decided this society was irredeemable. 

Inspired by the biblical mixture of the tower of babel (lack of language through induced noise by dosing [[Primal White Noise]] as memetic hazard in the exchange of ideas by exploiting [[Scorching Truth]]s) and the sodom and gomorrah fire and brimstone for subduing an angel.
