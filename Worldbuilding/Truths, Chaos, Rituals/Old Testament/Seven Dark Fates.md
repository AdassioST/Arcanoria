The "Second Phoebe" and the most cruel phase of the [[Genocides of the Auric Aria]]. It is named the [[Seven Dark Fates]] due to each being an [[Act of Fate]] in which each of the threads of the [[Auric Heptacode]] were progressively severed — descending from [[Strand]] (Thread 7) to [[Resonance]] (Thread 1) — dismantling the physical and magical foundations of the world one by one until nothing remained but entropy.

- _"God removing its capacity to honor love and memory goes as good as you would expect."_ — [[The White-Touched Archivist]]

### The [[Golden Ichor]] of the [[Grand Auric Cathedral]]

The [[Seven Dark Fates]] did not begin as an immediate act of divine wrath. Their origin lies in the catastrophic failure of the great diplomatic summit held within the central cathedral of the [[Auric Order]].

After cycles of proliferating [[Holy War]]s, the [[Auric Aria]] had shifted from direct intervention to a diplomatic approach, proposing a formal covenant in her most sacred grounds, a structured meeting intended to establish a binding end to all ongoing wars. The [[Chorus Pillar]] arrived to the audit armed.

![[Pasted image 20260430145435.png]]

Initially baffled, the [[Auric Aria]] establishes her authority confused of the threats, saying _"What is the meaning of this? You dare raise a blade against me?"_ warning against the insolence and the grave crime they were about to commit that violates violently Article VIII of the [[Auric Codex]]. However, rather than negotiate, they moved to kill her incarnation. While she was speaking, a blade aimed at her throat grazed her cheek instead, she dodged instinctively the fatal strike, but she was wounded. The first drop of [[Golden Ichor]] fell to the soil of her holy grounds.

She froze, touching her cheek to process what had just happened. Realizing that the blow was specifically aimed to silence her capacity to speak, she did not immediately retaliate. Instead, she began laughing, and then the laughter broke. She entered a spiraling [[Composure]], cataloguing her every sacrifice before the assembled court:

- Her voluntary mortal incarnation during the [[Eighth Cycle]] and the suffering it entailed.
- Her death by the moonlit cliff, which seeded [[Aetherlight]] and [[Spellweaving]] into the world.
- Her return from the [[Lost Cycle]], drawn back solely by [[Humanity]]'s desperate prayers when they nearly perished to [[Corruption]] and the [[Outer God]]s.
- The sacrifice of [[Selenea]], her only ever loved equal, bound eternally within the [[Moon]] so that all of them could exist as something more than mindless beasts.

She then revealed, publicly for the first time, the entire foundational [[Scorching Truth]]s of the [[Moon]] and creation, truths never before spoken before mortal ears. The room went silent until another blade broke the silence threatening to kill her again.

This time it did not reach her. She uncurled her massive wings from beneath the nebulae dress, deflecting the blow, and spoke in a voice of terrible calm:

- _"It seems I was wrong to tolerate this [[Corruption]] found even in the societies of my children. Perhaps [[Arcanoria]] is due for a new [[Humanity]] that will actually tolerate love. What is one more Phoebe."_

Before the mob could act again, she lunged skyward with a shockwave felt across the entire palace that made every single vitral and window of the cathedral shatter by the sheer intensity. Then, her voice echoed across the continent:

- _"If you really want to see how a world looks without my grace, so be it. I'll give you exactly a god forsaken land. If you really want me gone, I can unweave for all of you what I once wove."_

#### The [[Cycle]] of Silence, the Ark and Day of Judgement

The [[Chorus Pillar]] interpreted the [[Auric Aria]]'s departure not as a warning, but as an exile, and celebrated what they believed to be a victory. The full meaning of her final words remained unclear to them for the duration of the coming [[Cycle]].

Crucially, however, none of the people nor the assassins of the [[Chorus Pillar]] present in the clash with the [[Auric Aria]] in the [[Grand Auric Cathedral]] survives much longer, as the [[Shrieking Sentinel]] hunts every single [[Consciousness]] present during the [[Auric Aria]]'s spiraling [[Composure]] moment due to the amount of [[Scorching Truth]]s they now know. This is partially why the [[Auric Aria]] confessed the truth, she knew none of them would survive the aftermath as they were hunted by the first [[Primal Discordia]] of the [[Original Eight]]. It was a cruel and deliberate act on her behalf to have an [[Atonalis]] as hitman to get rid of them for her long before she unleashed the apocalypse or they could go around spreading the truth.

Before ascending, the [[Auric Aria]] issued one final command to her most loyal [[Auric Angels]]: to collect a pair of every animal species in [[Arcanoria]] in preparation for the coming reckoning. She then sounded the [[Goldweaver Auric Harp]] across the globe on a [[Resonance]] signal heard by all, understood by few, that the [[Day of Judgement]] would soon come. The [[Chorus Pillar]] received this as a bittersweet sign: joy at their apparent freedom from what they had branded tyrannical rule, threaded with a quiet fear that some could not name.

One full [[Cycle]] passed in silence. In that time, the story of the construction of the Ark unfolded, the gathering of creatures and the preservation of what the [[Auric Aria]] had deemed worth saving from the annihilation she had promised.

On the anniversary of the cathedral incident, the seven auric trumpets of the [[Auric Angels]] sounded across [[Arcanoria]], heralding what would be recorded as the [[Day of Judgement]].

A polyphonic chorus broadcast across the [[Great Harmonic Loom]]:

- _"GOD IS COMING. GOD IS COMING. GOD IS COMING."_

The [[Auric Aria]] used the [[Resonance Anchors]] of [[Piety]] as a frequency filter — not to spare the most devout of the old [[Auric Order]], but to identify anyone whose [[Piety]] she deemed worthy of [[Humanity]]'s continuation. Those individuals were lifted from the soil and drawn into the void pockets of localized [[Time Bubble]]s, shielded from the apocalypse to follow.

After the worthy of each city joined her embrace and the chorus changed

- _"GOD IS HERE. GOD IS HERE. GOD IS HERE."_

After the judgement ended, and everyone deemed worthy or pious, the true reckoning began for those condemned to the living hell, signifying that hell isn't punishment, it is solely the absence of the [[Auric Aria]]'s structural grace.

### The Unwinding of the [[Auric Heptacode]] in the [[Seven Dark Fates]]

The [[Auric Aria]] began severing the [[Auric Heptacode]] one thread at a time, descending from Thread 7 to Thread 1. The severing of [[Strand]] — the first thread cut — also momentarily severed her own capacity to feel love and empathy, as [[Strand]] is directly bound to [[Echoing Bonds]] and the [[Weight of Change]]. This absence of feeling explains the compounding cruelty with which each subsequent fate was delivered. Each disaster did not replace the previous — they accumulated, layering catastrophe upon catastrophe as more of [[Arcanoria]]'s magical foundations were stripped away.

The disasters unfolded as follows:

- **[[Strand]]** — Collapse into vast [[Time Bubble]]s across wide territories, fracturing continuity and isolating populations within temporal anomalies.
    
- **[[Crystal]]** — A blizzard of subtle snowflakes that crystallized on contact everything they touched: flesh, flora, fauna — turning them into jagged [[Crystal]] sculptures where they stood. On the most critical manifestations, the rivers ran crimson with the blood of the wounded. _"Midas touch made of unbreakable ice."_
    
- **[[Cindergale]]** — Swarms of ash clouds that caused widespread respiratory disease, accompanied by electrical firestorms that consumed crops, brimstone, and pestilence.
    
- **[[Void]]** — The removal of all shadows and reflections, paired with localized tears in space, triggering an Exodus of gravity. Suspended objects mirrored the devastation of the [[Great Fracture]].
    
- **[[Flux]]** — Unceasing rains of various types of liquid that would not stop.
    
- **[[Luminance]]** — The collapse of all light, creating a permanent and total darkness where even the concept of illumination was consumed.
    
- **[[Resonance]]** — The final flood of entropy, sweeping across the ruined continents and unraveling what little structure remained.

#### The Snapping of [[Resonance]] and the Entry of [[Primal White Noise]]

When the [[Auric Aria]] severed the final thread of [[Resonance]] — the same thread bound directly to her own [[Soul Leitmotif]] — the last barrier separating [[Arcanoria]] from [[The Infinite Void]] broke entirely. The catastrophic entry of [[Primal White Noise]] flooded the devastated cities, and the entropy that had been consuming the world reached its absolute completion.

It was the snapping of [[Resonance]] — the thread most intimately linked to herself — that fully restored her capacity for empathy and love. She had begun regaining it halfway through the progression, but it only fully returned at the moment the final thread tethered to her soul broke.

The mask shattered.

The [[Auric Aria]] gazed upon what she had done in horror and guilt. Despite being correct in her conviction that a lesson had to be taught — and that a blank slate was necessary — the weight of the destruction and suffering she had caused struck her in full. She created the [[Fractal Kaleidoscope]] on the [[Grand Thread Rings]] as a symbol and covenant, a promise witnessed across the ruined sky: she would never forsake the world again.

- _"God will never abandon you, even if you don't believe in her."_

This became the first change in her approach and the earliest seed of what would eventually become the gentler [[New Testament]].
