A massive construct built by [[Lacrimosa]], also known as the False Moon by the resistance of [[The Hollowing]], with the main purpose of routing [[Consciousness]] into [[Lacrimosa]]. It is suspended several leagues above the sky and aimed to encase the real [[Moon]]. [[Auric Order]]

It is a vast celestial object with three main eyes and a hollowed center. It uses the connection of the [[Auroral Ribbons]] to work as as [[Resonance Anchors]] that can broadcast [[Resonance]] signals all across the vast area where the [[All-Loving Moon]]. This is doable because the [[Auroral Ribbons]] encode the broadcasted signals into [[Frequency Harmonics]] and transmit them directly through the [[Symphonic Veins]] of the world's [[Leylines]]. This allows the signals to stretch across the vast areas where the [[All-Loving Moon]] has an [[All-Loving Anchor]] which are constructs of towers mirroring several winged eyes and are deliberately built by the [[Purest of Love]].

The construct and its interaction with the [[Auroral Ribbons]] is necessary due to [[Lacrimosa]]'s hard rule that she can only begin the process of [[The Hollowing]] if the [[Consciousness]] consents to it. She uses this construct to facilitate the manipulation and informational warfare to get [[Humanity]] to submit to her will and reach to her [[Purest of Love]]. Thus, the broadcast of the [[Auroral Ribbons]] are frequently used to diminish morale and invite those suffering to surrender their weapons. 

Furthermore, the broadcasting is meant to target specifically those who are defiant to [[The Hollowing]] and weaponizes [[Sympathetic Vibration]] to feel the overwhelming sensation and relief of the gentle [[All-Loving Moon]] pull. This effect becomes progressively more hypnotic the more [[Consciousness]] [[Lacrimosa]] has absorbed, and it's incredibly more effective as other kingdoms fall and surrender, either by the fatigue of maintenance, the starvation and famines as a result of the defecting populace, the disruption of [[Trade Routes]] or to anyone who is tired of being an instrument to society.
