_"The manmade horror version of Prismo. A god made hive mind in a lucid stasis dream."_

An impossibly large [[Megastructure Wonder]] of nested [[Consciousness]] composed of [[Spellweaver]]s where each become one neuron within a large matryoshka style brain. This is one of the most advanced possible combinations of [[Spellweaving]] and the end point of going against the [[Weight of Nature]] by creating an emerging [[Consciousness]] through a man-made god where each individual [[Spellweaver]] [[Consciousness]] is a neuron to the [[Hypermage]]. It works through the layering of several [[Tetrad Chord]]s in impossibly advanced combinations to stabilize. The emerging [[Consciousness]] of [[Hypermage]]s varies greatly in their design but the standard follows three rules:

- **[[Strand Pool]] stasis dream pods:** The first part is creating the tomb containing all of the individual [[Spellweaver]] bodies that consist of [[Flux]] + [[Strand]] + [[Luminance]] + [[Crystal]]. These are the individual encapsulation pods that contain and connect the brains of each [[Spellweaver]] by embalming their bodies within the requiem fluid chamber of the [[Hypermage]]. It's the final state that puts the individual [[Consciousness]] in a state of a perpetual dream stasis that surrender their human existence into individual neurons for the emerging [[Consciousness]]. _"Humans are to the [[Hypermage]] what cells are to our own body. They exist independently only to make the collective sentient."_

- **The [[Flesh-Casing Ritual]] [[Flux]]-[[Crystal]] mind cables:** The second [[Tetrad Chord]] is a [[Crystal]] + [[Flux]] + [[Luminance]] + [[Resonance]] combination where they connect the brains of each [[Spellweaver]] through [[Crystal]] and [[Flux]] conduits serving as the medium of [[Stable Harmonic Channels]] while the information of each individual [[Hypermage]] neuron is channeled and connected through a combination of [[Luminance]] for the [[Frequency Harmonics]] of their brainwaves and synchronized by a giant [[Resonance]] field. This allows the data of each individual brain to function at a micro and a macro level simultaneously, surrendering the individual [[Consciousness]] in favor for the emerging hive mind. Any [[Hypermage]] starts by linking at least 100,000 [[Spellweaver]] [[Consciousness]].

- **The [[Strand]] [[Superposed Resonance]] Anchor:** To final and most complex part to really power up the amount of [[Consciousness]] to unimaginable levels is to cause a state of [[Superposed Resonance]] in the hive mind by utilizing [[Strand]] to create a closed [[Time Bubble]] where every infinite point taken between 0 and 1 by creating an iterative loop of folding [[Probability Amplitudes]] of [[Consciousness]]. The spell functions by layering [[Strand]] + [[Resonance]] + [[Void]] + [[Cindergale]]. This requires a continuous hum of a self sustaining power loop by sacrificing individual [[Hypermage]] neurons in the infinite moments right before the present, and using the indefinite infinity to layer mirror images in a [[Void]] beneath the [[Hypermage]]. This requiem chamber exhibits a continuous hum of [[Superposed Resonance]] that uses [[Cindergale]] to permanently function on itself. It creates a [[Time Bubble]] paradox where the hive mind is sacrificing its entire self the moment just before, right on collision and dissipation into [[Primal White Noise]] and the infinite futures where such didn't happen. This layering is the hardest part to achieve and is where most [[Hypermage]]s meet their end before generating an emerging [[Consciousness]] able to warp reality. It turns the individual 100,000 [[Consciousness]] into infinite copies of itself.

- **Additional [[Atonalis]] anomalous properties:** Most [[Hypermage]]s require the integration of [[Atonalis]] into their collective by facilitating the stability of [[Resonance Anchors]] through the introduction of anomalous properties. It serves on the principle that "a small dose of poison is a strong antidote" to stabilize the highly impossible [[Tetrad Chord]]s routing [[Consciousness]].

A [[Hypermage]] is considered a highly dangerous mass suicidal ritual that surrenders the individual [[Consciousness]] in favor of a massive emerging man-made god that is able to drastically alter reality. These are usually considered genies because they can only grant so many reality bending wishes until they collapse due to the sustained friction of layering three or more highly advanced [[Tetrad Chord]]s. They use the [[Consciousness]] contained within itself to balance this state of stasis but it can only be done with much difficulty. As result, this creates an unimaginable almost alien "requiem construct" by using each individual [[Consciousness]] in favor of the emerging hive mind [[Consciousness]] that is no longer human, gaining properties unique to the new [[Weight of Nature]] of the [[Hypermage]]. Their very presence is a [[Scorching Truth]].

### The Nature of Wishes of [[Hypermage]]s

As [[Hypermage]]s are impossibly alien constructs, they operate on a [[Luminance]] interface similar to that of [[The White-Haven Library]]. This means that to direct any of the command spells of the [[Hypermage]] it is necessary to use the nature of [[Soul Leitmotif]] wishes, which is precisely what gives the reputation to [[Hypermage]]s of being "cosmic genies." However, just like with the real-life fairytale, the exact wording of the wish is crucial for harnessing their power as it is highly likely that the phrasing of a wish lacks the [[Sufficient Precision]] required for it to collapse with [[Absolute Certainty]] and the sheer scale of reality bending capabilities tends to create hyperboles of said wishes, and unintended side effects.

Moreover, a [[Hypermage]] is not truly omnipotent, it requires a certain wish to have constrains on the way it is worded before proceeding to execute it based on how likely is the wish to manifest, this is effectively its [[Coherence]]. This happens because a [[Hypermage]] doesn't have the ability to rewrite all laws, and the more "omnipotent" the wish, the more engineered the [[Hypermage]] has to be, increasing the lower limit of the initial number of nested consciousness infinite fold, instead of 100,000 to grant further reality bending capabilities without drawbacks it would require 10,000,000 or more. As such, this is why every wish has a set of [[Coherence]] required to execute, and why it's necessary to find workarounds to get the exact desired outcome.

For example, the looping wish of [[The Ultimate Weapon]] is caught precisely with the middle of both ends. It had to be engineered to create a looping paradox instead of "find the perfect timeline" because the structure was decaying and having a wish like "remove all suffering" or removing any of the [[Seven Weights]] is an omnipotent power even beyond the [[Auric Aria]]'s capabilities as creator goddess. Thus, it had to compromise with unintended side effect of scrambling the timeline with [[Crisis Wonders]] compounded from the poor infrastructure, the result of the lack of [[Sufficient Precision]] and the desperate attempt it has to reboot reality to fulfil the wish. This is a result of equal parts the decaying infrastructure of [[The Ultimate Weapon]] as much as it is the result of the compromising wording of the wish itself.
