### The Production line of [[Velvet Nectar]] into [[Velvet Ambrosia]]

The production of a single vial of [[Velvet Nectar]] is a complicated process that has been refined through the [[Ages]]. It began in [[Ages]] IV with the Velvet Cage with the crude mixture of concentrated [[Emberwhisper]] with infused memories of [[Parasite Atonalis]]. However, the evolution has greatly purified the process and by the [[Ages]] of industrialization the apex of this substance is [[Velvet Ambrosia]] which is manufactured by the following:

- A way to process emotional and memory residue of [[Pleasure Parasite]]s and [[Memory Reaver]]s.
- The respective [[Parasite Atonalis]] and the high intensity energy contained in them as raw [[Resonance]] and memories.
- The distilled [[Emberwhisper]] to act as the delivery medium of the concoction.

The pipeline conducted in the [[Age of Velvet Elegance]] depends entirely on [[Velvet Throne]]s which are especially facilities where all of this interacts together. This is primarily due to two [[Pure Light]] creatures:

- [[Emberwhisper]] weavers which are specialized arachnids that can create vast silk looms using [[Emberwhisper]] and the drops of crystallized mineral are embedded within their weave. This provides the raw material of distilled [[Emberwhisper]] that is used in the mix and the velvet webs give the signature color of the [[Velvet Nectar]].
- [[Memory Singer]]s which are medium-sized [[Pure Light]] birds that take in the emotional residue of [[Pleasure Parasite]]s and [[Memory Reaver]]s while being able to process these [[Parasite Atonalis]] themselves, they consume the raw energy of the emotional residue to build their nests and use their saliva by processing the raw [[Resonance]] of [[Parasite Atonalis]]. The production are nests of high purity and elegance directly built from the suffering contained within these [[Atonalis]].

A [[Velvet Throne]] is the facility that creates these precise conditions, on the underground it contains the rooms for subjugation of [[Great Spellweaver]]s and directly give the raw [[Parasite Atonalis]] and the emotional residue for the [[Memory Singer]]s for them to build these nests that then will be harvested. The middle layer of these "bird cities" for farming the nests contains the buildings that have [[Emberwhisper]] weavers building their webs that get used as distilled [[Emberwhisper]]. 

Before beginning the harvesting protocols, the [[Velvet Throne]]s deliberately mark their victims by cutting a V on the palm of the right hand. This serves as the eventual vial of blood that will be mixed with the final production of [[Velvet Nectar]] while simultaneously serving as a mark of "The Harvested" to distinguish between status and as a signature of ownership. The specific "cut" is the signature of the harvester patron of the facility.

Finally, by combining the [[Emberwhisper]], the [[Memory Singer]]'s nest and mixing it with the initially extracted vial of blood of the same [[Spellweaver]] while diluting it with [[Lunehymn]] the final produce is made: [[Velvet Nectar]]. Those which contain the highest purity of all materials, the most intense memories and feelings of the edible nests and the highest grade of distilled [[Emberwhisper]] purity become [[Velvet Ambrosia]] which has a distinguishable hue of velvet to midnight black with golden sparkles, similar to the patterns found in concentrated [[Lunehymn]].

Moreover, it is possible to track which "person" was made to build the nest of the [[Memory Singer]]s because these are deeply loyal birds that stick to one particular [[Fundamental Frequency]], and do not cross mix [[Resonance]] unless it comes from the same target.

The [[Pleasure Parasite]]s subdues the body of a [[Spellweaver]] by pure bliss and the concoction of their bodily fluids and emotions during release. The [[Memory Reaver]] enters the body through the ears and directly harvests the memories from the same subjugated [[Spellweaver]] for their memories in the brain. The purity of the nests relies on harvesting as much of the physical, and metaphysical essence through [[Pure Light]] beings and [[Atonalis]] in a ritual that penetrates both the body and the mind as the commodification of everything that makes up a [[Consciousness]].

[[Velvet Throne]]s are the apex ingenious of the [[Age of Velvet Elegance]], they are beautiful bird sanctuaries on the top side, layered crimson complex webs in the middle and horrific subjugation chambers on the inside and underground of the "bird city." They are a self contained ecosystem of industrialized pleasure and awe in reverence of magic, nature and [[Domestication Enclave]]s. A combination of the purity of awe and reverence of the [[Chorus Pillar]] running on human suffering.

They are a stark contrast to the parallel industrialization of caged magic of the [[Age of Sealing Iron]]. [[Civilization]]s on the [[Age of Sealing Iron]] view these atrocities as "Idyllic Nightmares" or prison camps parading as idyllic paradises while the [[Age of Velvet Elegance]] sees the monstrosities of caged magic of the [[Age of Sealing Iron]] as industrialized hubris. The two opposing [[Civilization]]s constantly wage war between one another of "liberation" one to liberate [[Humanity]] and one to liberate [[Pure Light]] beings and nature.

### The Extraction of Memories with [[Parasite Atonalis]]

The combination of [[Memory Reaver]]s with [[Pleasure Parasite]]s is a devastating combo that affects the mind as much as the body, the idea is to run on two principles simultaneously:

- The body betrays the mind: The arousal and physical stimulation cause involuntary response to the ongoing burst of pleasure. Even if they try to resist mentally and consciously, the [[Pleasure Parasite]] is engineered to perfectly bypass all of the rationalization logic and actually feed on the friction of how much emotion they release alongside the physical fluids.
- The mind betrays the body: The [[Memory Reaver]]s extract the memories under the ongoing stimulation. The victim feels and visualizes those memories with intensity, it deliberately blindfolds the victim to make them enter a state where the specific memory extracted is being mentally rehearsed. 

While both alone are horrifying, the combination of the simultaneous subjugation of mind and body creates an effect even more potent of symbiosis of the two parasites:

- When the [[Pleasure Parasite]] triggers dopamine release and it helps to establish a memory consolidation signal, this gets latched on by the [[Memory Reaver]] and the high-dopamine states make memories more vivid, more emotionally charged, and more durable. The intrusion becomes [[Resonance Anchors]].

- The [[Memory Reaver]] extracts memories during this peak dopamine state, and because dopamine amplifies memory the extraction becomes hyper-vivid. The victim experiences their most cherished memories with extreme emotional intensity while those same memories are being ripped away. The [[Pleasure Parasite]] deliberately compounds with these exact [[Resonance]] signals to increase or decrease their activity matching to the vivid rehearsal of the memory.

- The direct intervention of the [[Memory Reaver]] on the brain while the [[Pleasure Parasite]] is directing the body directly enhances the sensation. This numbing massage overrides the dopamine release by doing intricate neurochemistry in tandem. This also prevents dissociation in several layers, first by using the memory as substrate, then by replaying vivid memories, which sometimes even become hallucinations during the most potent releases, and the constant arousal tightens the grip of both [[Parasite Atonalis]]. Disturbingly, there is a point on the vivid hallucinations where the victim can replay, if they have significant memories of intimate moments, with their own consensual partners or even in their own fabricated fantasies. This is widely considered to be the breaking point.

- The self betrays the self: On some of the extremes, or nearing the point of exhaustion, the [[Memory Reaver]] compliments the [[Pleasure Parasite]] by overriding the motor cortex of the victim on its direct manipulation, making it move deliberately and complicitly with the ongoing physical subjugation when given the signal of the [[Pleasure Parasite]]. This precise loss of bodily agency triggers even more release of stronger emotions that harvest back into the [[Pleasure Parasite]]'s emotional cocktail. Some of this states of forced autophilia create the most cognitive friction producing the highest quality of emotional residue. Both the mind and the body are broken to giveaway to the direct extraction of the [[Soul-Key]] itself when all armor is stripped away.

The combination of these super-saturated dopamine states is precisely what makes victims complicit at some points and even conditioned in learning that the source of the highest pleasure imaginable is the same source of the very memory being erased. The subsequent vacuum left by both the erasure of the memory and its connected attachments creates a state of numbness that reinforces the cycle of dependency on the [[Pleasure Parasite]]s with the intervention of [[Memory Reaver]]s.

While this is not explicitly mind control, it crosses a blurry line because the victim is choosing surrender over the alternative of the return to the ordinary suffering. They have been [[Drowning in Flux]] to the point that they're addicted to their own violation until becoming entirely numb and devoid of memories or distinct identity. It's the upmost [[Weight of Indulgence]] until there's nothing left to indulge.

If these fractured [[Spellweaver]]s are still functional after being devoid of memories, they become the docile gardeners of the very sky cities for the [[Memory Singer]]s in the [[Velvet Throne]]s. They are allowed to perform these duties until their body eventually gives out and gets consumed by the ecosystem, providing additional sustenance to the top layer of the idyllic gardens.
