#scorchingtruth

_"Cruelty and meaning are woven from the same thread."_

An [[Scorching Truth]], of an experiment gone terribly bad during the [[Fifth Cycle]] where the [[Auric Aria]] faltered and created the [[Formless Masses]] and first [[Primal Discordia]], introducing to [[Arcanoria]] the [[Inevitability of Death]] and the original [[Dissonance]].

 [[Selenea]] comes to [[Auric Aria]] when she is crying after the disaster of the [[Fifth Cycle]] and she breathes the brittle form she was holding turning it into the 
  [[Fated Flower]]. This scene enhances their dynamic and makes the [[Auric Aria]] vulnerable.
