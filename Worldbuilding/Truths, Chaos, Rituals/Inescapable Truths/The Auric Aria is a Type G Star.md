_"The Brightest Star is not the Largest nor the Oldest."_

The [[Scorching Truth]] that reveals the true nature of [[The Infinite Void]] and the horrors that exist within. This truth depends on the  [[Lunar Abyss]] and its [[Coherence]] mirrored pools that are actually gravitational lensing based by the presence of [[Outer God]]s, and disproves the [[Auric Aria]]'s privileged status as the rest of the [[Outer God]]s as in reality she is the equivalent of a type G star while [[Selenea]] (Type A) and [[Lacrimosa]] (Type F) as seen in the [[Lunar Abyss]]. 

#### The Truth of Size in the [[Lunar Abyss]]

The lower chasm of the [[Lunar Abyss]] is the only place that allows piercing the [[Stellar Veil]] from the inside. When pilgrims descend deep enough into its mirrored pools, they do not see the normal night sky of [[Arcanoria]]. They see a totally different arrangement of lights, a different night sky entirely. The truth lies in the fact that this alternative sky is not a reflection of [[Arcanoria]]'s cosmos. it is [[The Infinite Void]] seen through the mortal lens of [[Coherence]], which converts [[Outer God]]s into star-like manifestations. 

Thus, the alternative night sky is effectively an [[Outer God]] map of the entities nearest to [[Arcanoria]]. It also reveals that the [[Auric Aria]], the brightest star at the center, appears as a Type G star, In the general cosmology, she's actually smaller by a thin margin than [[Lacrimosa]] or [[Selenea]], which are Type F and Type A respectively. This also reflect their [[Anthropomorphic Shape]] sizes, the [[Auric Aria]] is 1.68 meters, [[Lacrimosa]] is 1.70 meters, and [[Selenea]] is 1.74 meters.

Furthermore, against the [[Dark Forest Paradox]] she's much smaller. The [[Dark Forest Paradox]] is similar to Betelgeuse as a Red Supergiant, in his [[Anthropomorphic Shape]], his Treant host at the center of the Time Forest is about 10 meters. This difference in size, furthermore explains why it dislikes the time continuum of the [[Trinity Harmony]], as it is trying to fit in a super tight corset to interact with [[Arcanoria]].

#### The [[Consciousness]] Awareness of [[The One True God]]

Despite being one of the first deities on her cluster in [[The Infinite Void]], she is not the oldest, as [[Lacrimosa]] awakened in the pocket of [[Void]] before the [[Auric Aria]] achieved complete self awareness. They are similar in age, but she did awaken first than the [[Auric Aria]], and both awakened before [[Selenea]].

Other [[Consciousness]] are theoretically older than her, but there is no record of any other [[Outer God]] that predates her existence that wasn't one of the flickering [[Consciousness]] that dissolved back into the static, for which these can't be considered real deities.

Finally, the [[Auric Aria]] is not the largest nor the oldest god. However, her intelligence and her [[Mastery Over Chaos]] are undeniable, she's able to subdue beings much larger than her, much like how [[Humanity]] was able to conquer the world, the behemoths, the monsters, and the mountains much larger than them in physical size. Her ability to weave with [[Sufficient Precision]] in [[Auric Geometry]] and perfect order established the rules of the cosmos, and is able to capture and subdue beings of magnitudes larger in their formless shape.

This is further explored as propaganda in the [[Ages]] XII as [[Humanity]] establishes order on their own cosmos and with the advent of [[Celestial Ensnaring Mirror]]s they are able to turn [[Outer God]]s as large as the [[Dark Forest Paradox]] into celestial batteries capable of wielding and binding their raw power, proving the anthropocentric lens of their creator, although this clashes directly with the [[Chorus Pillar]] and ends in [[Ages]] XIII in the [[Primal White Noise]] [[Cataclysmic Aftermath]] unless deliberately stopped by having changed the fabric of [[Cosmic Motion]] through [[A True Sine Wave]].
