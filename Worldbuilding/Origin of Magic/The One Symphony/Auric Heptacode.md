Displayed in sigils as a heptagram referring to the seven bindings of [[The Principles of Magic]]. It represents the Brightest Star in the Sky, referring to the [[Auric Aria]], while providing the structure that the center, [[Arcanoria]], is held by the seven golden threads of the [[Auric Heptacode]] and the [[Great Harmonic Loom]].

Primarily used during [[Ages]] II, and is the central motif to the [[Age of Glyphs]], and is a vital symbol for all studies in [[Cosmic Motion]] and Celestial Holography, despite being founded in the [[Age of Glyphs]], it is also a recurring core symbol for the [[Age of the Seven Threads]], the [[Age of Mirrors]], the [[Age of Alchemy]], the [[Age of Hymns]], and with a deep scientific understanding, the [[Age of Holography]].

### The Octagram of the [[Chorus Pillar]]

The octagram is a blasphemous symbol to the [[Aureus Pillar]].

The occult, [[Esoteric Enclave]]s and the [[Chorus Pillar]] tend to use a modified version of the [[Auric Heptacode]]'s heptagram opting for instead an octagram, or an Eight Point Star, as a primary symbol to instead refer to the [[Eight-Born Paths]], the hubris of the [[Eighth Cycle]], the [[True Mythos of Creation]], and generally as an heretic display towards the doctrine of [[The One True God]], signaling that [[Outer God]]s are as much of a deity, and by proxy are also meant to be revered, as the [[Auric Aria]]. 

During [[The Hollowing]] in [[Ages]] III, the octagram was also adopted by [[Lacrimosa]]'s [[Purest of Love]] religion to refer to the [[Eight-Wings of Lacrimosa]], and to add the [[Auroral Ribbons]] alongside the primary seven bindings of [[The Principles of Magic]], representing that the [[All-Loving Moon]] is as essential to the heavens as the elements that bind the [[Great Harmonic Loom]]'s [[Grand Thread Rings]] and reality itself.

After learning [[The Truth of Arcanoria]], Selenic religions tend to adopt and integrate the [[Moon]] into the octagram as acknowledgement to the [[Betrayal of the Moon]], mirroring the idea of [[Lacrimosa]]'s symbology but with the real [[Selenea]]'s [[Moon]].
