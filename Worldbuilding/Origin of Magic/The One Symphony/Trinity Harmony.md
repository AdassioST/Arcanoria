The [[Trinity Harmony]] is the threefold law that binds the continuum of time into order through grouping the bindings of the [[Auric Heptacode]]. It starts from the latest element in the past, [[Strand]], and builds towards the furthest in the future, [[Crystal]].

By order the bindings in the [[Trinity Harmony]] are grouped by:

- The Triad of the Past. The [[Potential of Creation]]:
	- [[Strand]] (The Distant Past): It is the first part of the threefold law, and thus it is the binding which connects both the [[Trinity Harmony]] and [[Auric Heptacode]], despite being the last of the bindings, it controls the link to the time continuum.
	
	- [[Void]] (The Intermediate Past): It is the meaning of sacrifice, worth, and memory that continues to shape upon what a [[Consciousness]] has experienced. It has a balanced personal weight while maintaining as "fresh" enough to dictate present behavior.
	  
	- [[Flux]] (The Nearest Past): It is the last element separating the past, as it requires texture and depth of history but it is imbued with the immediate present creating emotion and reflection to experiencing current existence.
  
- The Existing Present. The [[Key of Attunement]]:
	- [[Resonance]] (The True Present): The precise middle upon which the reality was built. It is the wave that layers an "I" upon everything, holding both the weight of what has been as much as it does the conviction of what will be. 
	  
- The Triad of Future. The [[Mastery Over Chaos]]:
	- [[Cindergale]] (The Nearest Future): It is the first element that separates the future, and directs the immediate direction that the flows of intent, concentration, and the path in which will becomes action.
	  
	- [[Luminance]] (The Intermediate Future): It is the knowledge that shapes as much long-term behavior as it guides immediate direction. It provides means to achieve a destination but it is present enough to form the bridge.
	  
	- [[Crystal]] (The Distant Future): The final part of the threefold law, it collapses all the possible futures into a single vision. It allows imagination to flow along the possibilities that will eventually become the new present.
