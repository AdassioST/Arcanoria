#spellweaving

![[The Principles of Magic-2.png]]

Magic in [[Arcanoria]] flows through seven foundational principles, each bound to a virtue and a constraint, drawing directly from one of the golden threads in the [[Auric Heptacode]]. The principles are divided into the seven bindings of [[Spellweaving]] and cast upon the various layers of [[Consciousness]] from [[The First Overtone]], and from these layers, the principles create physicality in the [[Known Universe]].

In [[Arcanoria]], they greatly amplify the magical [[Resonance]] of [[Frequency Harmonics]] due to the existence of the [[Great Harmonic Loom]] using a [[Soul Leitmotif]] with [[Music as Catalyst]]. The bindings themselves are equal to masterfully playing a song:

- You find your note and key (1). [[Key of Attunement]].
- You play a precise interval (2).  [[Sufficient Precision]].
- You imbue it with feeling (3).  [[Emotional Authenticity]].
- You understand the strength required to apply dynamics and play it (4). [[Essence Sacrifice]].
- You sustain it with focus (5). [[Perfect Focus]].
- You resolve it with perfect certainty (6). [[Absolute Certainty]].
- You harmonize it with other players (7). [[Echoing Bonds]].

The expertise combination of [[Sufficient Precision]], [[Perfect Focus]], and [[Absolute Certainty]] is called [[Mastery Over Chaos]]. While [[Emotional Authenticity]], [[Essence Sacrifice]], and [[Echoing Bonds]] create the [[Potential of Creation]]. The combination of all bindings are the [[Triadic Virtues of Spellcraft]].

### The Seven Bindings

Woven from the [[Auric Heptacode]], these seven bindings form the invisible rules for [[Spellweaving]] upon which they govern every [[Spellweaver]]'s spell integrity, cost, and potency. Each binding names a core psychological and physical domain, and anchors a facet of [[Consciousness]] to an elemental aspect keyed uniquely to the [[Spellweaver]]'s soul.

Every single person is naturally aligned to a binding once they trigger a [[Motif Awakening]] and, while the proficiency in said element is given by nature, authentic [[Spellweaving]] demands walking the delicate edge between fused elements. Thus, it is possible to create combinations of two or more bindings, and it's an essential practice of advanced [[Spellweaving]]. Still, it is a carefully crafted dance of profound mastery, personal growth, and binding knowledge, as fusions of several elements are more challenging to sustain without diluting direction and potency.

The seven bindings, its core, constrain, personal root, [[Motif Awakening]], and attuned elements are as follows:

*First Auric Thread of Wave Mechanics.*
Attuned Element [[Resonance]]. (Sound + Wind)

The art of harmony begins in serenity — when the self grows quiet enough to hear the world's song, and whole again to hold its own voice.

**Constraint:** The [[Spellweaver]]’s intent must align with their [[Fundamental Frequency]] [[Soul-Key]]. Potency is amplified by unwavering purpose and dampened by inner conflict.

**Personal Root & [[Motif Awakening]]:** Seeking belonging after isolation / Finding your place in the world. Those "Out of tune" who learn to align their [[Soul-Key]] to the world.

*"A string only rings true when perfectly tuned to the purpose of a key."*

### The Dynamics of [[Spellweaving]]

_"The ultimate key to power let the self resonate with the universe so perfectly that chaos has no choice but to submit to your song."_

[[Spellweaving]] requires the use of all of the seven bindings simultaneously, while they may not be fully conscious at once, the interplay between these seven characteristics increases or decreases the [[Spellweaving]] potency. There is no "mana" in any formal way, only pieces of yourself to give as fuel, and the mental overload of complexity and [[Discordant Interference]] without a proper resolution.

For example, when casting something simple, like a [[Cindergale]] fireball. The [[Spellweaver]] only has to feel the [[Resonance]] within, determine the size of the fireball through [[Sufficient Precision]], and imagine the fireball committing to the effect they intend to by [[Absolute Certainty]] while paying the price for the spell with [[Essence Sacrifice]], which is likely just making them tired after casting the spell.

Dampening elements like [[Emotional Authenticity]] or [[Echoing Bonds]] depend on the context. If this same fireball was being cast to hit a former friend that betrayed them increases the power of the fireball but if they're doubtful because they don't want to hurt them [[Emotional Authenticity]] dampens it, effectively balancing the increase of the spell by adding power by [[Echoing Bonds]] and subtracting it with the lack of [[Emotional Authenticity]].

The complexity of weaving several bindings depends entirely on the context of the [[Spellweaver]] casting the spell. Let's analyze this with an example:

The context is a delusional psychopath [[Crystal]] [[Spellweaver]] that is hyped up due to bloodlust, and they want to cut through reinforced magical clothing with [[Crystal]]-made war scissors but they aren't smart enough to define "how". Additionally, they've been using their memories as the source of power and they person holding the magical cloth is a former lover but can't remember them anymore:

- [[Key of Attunement]]: Naturally, this psychopath feels certain about themselves and their sadistic desire, so [[Resonance]] effectively increases the spell's power.

- [[Sufficient Precision]]: If they don't know how to cut through cloth or how the enchantment of the cloth works, the lack of [[Luminance]] reduces the spell's power. Even worse, If they "just want to target everything indiscriminately" the effectiveness decreases by a lot and invites [[Discordant Interference]].

- [[Emotional Authenticity]]: Morality doesn't matter, the fact that they are true to the emotion and willingness to cut through the cloth will increase the spell's power because they're in [[Flux]] with their bloodlust.

- [[Essence Sacrifice]]: It entirely depends on what they're using to "fuel" the spell, if it's physical exhaustion it will have a standard power modifier but if in this instance they're actively burning part of their memories, in turn making them even more psychopathic, the effect potency will be greatly enhanced in a relationship directly proportional to the part of themselves that they are exchanging to the [[Void]] for power.

- [[Perfect Focus]]: Likely this psychopath is able to maintain focused on their bloodlust, cancelling all the outside noise and moving in bursts like [[Cindergale]] as they are channeling all of themselves with an undivided focus into cutting the cloth, increasing the power of the spell by a lot.

- [[Absolute Certainty]]: This is the strongest element of the [[Crystal]] user, their delusion of "it's just cloth" will feed their conviction and will that they are able to bypass the magical barrier because "scissors will always be able to cut cloth." The truthiness of their imagination, regardless of the actual reality, feeds greatly the spell potency due to their [[Crystal]]-clear conviction in what will happen.

- [[Echoing Bonds]]: If the target was to be someone random, the spell will neither have a positive or negative modifier. However, they actually knew the target, but can't remember anymore due to burning their memories with [[Essence Sacrifice]]. Thus, the severed [[Strand]] between their relationship will greatly dampen the spell's power.

In the end, the mixture between positive elements like their [[Absolute Certainty]], [[Perfect Focus]], [[Essence Sacrifice]], [[Key of Attunement]] and [[Emotional Authenticity]] will balance out the negatives of [[Echoing Bonds]] and [[Sufficient Precision]]. Although drastically reduced in power due to the severe penalties inflicted by the later, making the cloth be able to receive damage but backfire [[Discordant Interference]] back to the [[Crystal]] [[Spellweaver]].

### Advanced [[Spellweaving]] and Chord Compositions

Every binding in [[Arcanoria]] can also be combined to add nuance to spell effects, where they either may be struck as a [[Minor Note]] or a [[Major Note]], each reflecting a different depth of commitment and consequence. [[Minor Note]]s draw upon fleeting emotions and lighter expressions of elemental power; they are most suitable for simple charms but most commonly used in supportive enhancement effects. [[Major Note]]s channel core convictions, and are usually the Root of any solid magic. They are heavily influenced by the casted attuned element.

Though [[Minor Note]] spells cost little, usually only slight fatigue, even the most trivial enchantment can ascend to a [[Major Note]] when it's fueled by a significant and meaningful [[Essence Sacrifice]]. These vary depending on the context and current [[Ages]], but it has to be something proportional to the desired power, such as giving up a part of one selves physical body, a core memory that allows the [[Spellweaver]] to do magic, or by destroying an object of great emotional attachment.

Finally, the true artistry of [[The Principles of Magic]] lies in weaving multiple bindings together into a single composition. Spell craft can only hold one [[Major Note]] and it unfolds like a song, with three essential components that create any spell:

- **Melody**: The fundamental essence of a spell — a single binding cast as a [[Major Note]] that defines the enchantment's elemental theme and emotional direction. Whether it sings with purpose, blazes with focus, or glints with crystalline vision, the Root sets the spell's identity.

- **Rhythm**: Tempo governs the spell’s flow, with [[Perfect Focus]] keeping time as conductor. Spells pulse and release in five core patterns:

	- **Staccato:** Sharp, punctured bursts. (Instant effects, Rapid-fire attacks)
	- **Legato:** Smooth, continuous flow. (Sustained, Ongoing barriers)
	- **Accelerando:** Gradual acceleration for surging power. (Spell barrages)
	- **Ritardando:** Gentle slowdown for controlled release. (Fading effects)
	- **Polyrhythm:** Interleaved bursts and lulls. (Chaotic spells)
	
- **Harmony**:  Harmony adds color by layering up to three additional bindings as [[Minor Note]]s, crafting musical intervals that enrich and complicate the spell. A third interval brings warmth or precision, a fifth reinforces strength, and a seventh introduces dramatic tension that must resolve. Harmonic complexity is the basis of all advanced [[Spellweaving]], as these components intertwine into the four chord spell tiers:
  
	- **[[Unison]] Spells (Root Only):** Pure elemental magic with a single [[Major Note]]. Unisons create reliable, straightforward effects with minimum cost. It is the most common and basic magic.
	
	- **[[Dyad Chord]] Spells (Root + Third):** Advanced magic adding one [[Minor Note]] at the third interval. Dyads refine the Root imbuing properties of depth or specialized function while maintaining spell stability.
	
	- **[[Triad Chord]] Spells (Root + Third + Fifth):** The most common chord, expert-level magic combining three bindings in consonant harmony. These create powerful, balanced effects through the interplay of the [[Major Note]] Root, the third [[Minor Note]], and the fifth [[Minor Note]].
	
	- **[[Tetrad Chord]] Spells (Root + Third + Fifth + Seventh):** Master-level impossible compositions weaving four bindings into a reality bending tense chord of a [[Major Note]] and three [[Minor Note]]s. To cast, they demand profound sacrifice and require perfect focus and precise resolution to prevent catastrophic [[Dissonance]].

As complexity grows, so does unpredictability, demanding ever-greater [[Absolute Certainty]] to guide each chord through chaos into harmony and preventing [[Discordant Interference]]. The interplay of [[Major Note]]s and [[Minor Note]]s, varied tempos, and intervallic richness yields near-infinite spell variations, with creativity being the sole limitation.

Consequently, all basic [[Spellweaving]] can turn into advanced, expert, or reality-bending spells so long as there is sufficient mastery over the seven bindings and a [[Essence Sacrifice]] fuel potent enough to sustain it. The discovery and widespread application of chords depend on the [[Ages]] of [[Spellweaving]] and the discovery of the society's own magical research.

Root: [[Flux]], [[Major Note]] - A flowing spray of liquid with a strong caudal.
Harmony: [[Crystal]], [[Minor Note]], Third + [[Luminance]], [[Minor Note]], Fifth)

**Effect:** A prismatic rainbow-like spray that, on contact, creates pyramids of crystals. These structures then emit resonating light that interacts with each other, bouncing in waves between them and amplifying the spell's light the more crystals there are.

Root: [[Strand]], [[Major Note]] - Several time strings with deep ancestral connection.

However, some of the chaotic waves cause immediate amnesia. Casting this spell would require a heavy [[Essence Sacrifice]] for fuel and require mastery of [[Sufficient Precision]] to avoid psychological fragmentation.

### Utility: Medical & Healing Spells

[[Spellweaving]] isn't only meant for combat. On the contrary, most of the use case of spells are as utility that provides various creative approaches to problems that can't be resolved with drawing swords. One of these particular cases is the spectrum of healing magic and its inner workings. 

However, it is important to note that [[Spellweaving]] is unable to create tissue growth as there isn't any bio-magic element that modifies directly living beings like cells, yet it can provide a variety of methods for healing which mirror real-life medicine:

- [[Strand]] is the primary element for healing, as it draws on the concept of [[Object Permanence]] to maintain the structure and "remind the body how it used to be before the injury." It draws on a blueprint of how tissue was before receiving damage, and effectively merges the current self with the past. However, it is increasingly difficult with the more time it passes before the spell is applied as it requires a bigger [[Essence Sacrifice]] and mental overload in [[Sufficient Precision]] and [[Absolute Certainty]]. Finally, the greatest limitation of [[Strand]] healing is that it can only remind the body of a state it has previously known, it is impossible to "heal" blindness or deafness from birth as there was no previous healthy state. It can't create any body parts that didn't exist because it isn't tissue growth.

- [[Luminance]] and [[Crystal]] are both tied as secondary healing magic. [[Luminance]] due to its properties of manipulating radiation, heat, and other sensory lenses to realize the inner workings and methods of applying selective changes. [[Sufficient Precision]] is necessary for any magical surgery. On the other side, [[Crystal]] is great at providing on-demand tools for field medical magic on frontlines with spells like creating sterilized [[Crystal]] tools, orthopedic casts or a temporary sanitized safe shelter.

- [[Flux]]. [[Cindergale]] are the tertiary magic of healing, by themselves they're useful for dealing with regular properties of medicine such as cleaning, purifying, cauterizing, and sanitizing. Likewise, they can be paired with other elements to increase their potency such as [[Strand Pool]]s using both [[Flux]] and [[Strand]] to create a safe medium for [[Object Permanence]], and higher recovery in "healing water." [[Cindergale]] is useful with [[Luminance]] to create precise medical lasers, cutting jets, and with [[Strand]] to create cleansing fire for dispelling curses.

- [[Resonance]] is usually reserved for increasing the properties of other spells, such as increasing the radius of a healing [[Strand Pool]] or reverberating its potency by bouncing [[Resonance]] waves within the medium. It can also be useful for "freezing time" through applying Faraday waves to the [[Great Harmonic Loom]]'s [[Flux]] membrane mixed with [[Strand]] to prevent internal damage to the wound's tissue if it isn't fully healed, bleeding or exposed.

- [[Void]] is the hardest to combine with magical medicine but it has niche use cases for bending sub properties that require shadow or space of the physical healing mediums. Paradoxically, it can be used greatly for deteriorating health, such as for casting curses or mimicking venoms.

### Utility: Creative Sets of Properties & Shapeshifting

There is an array of unlimited interactions between elements that are really only bound by the [[Spellweaver]]'s imagination, creativity and ingenuity, common interactions between elements are the following:

- [[Crystal]] + [[Flux]]: [[Crystal]] allows to create traditionally rigid geometrical structures, however, by adding a malleable property of [[Flux]], they can become any type of texture be it bouncy, slim, elastic, soft, fluffy, the real limit to changing physical touch is the [[Spellweaver]]'s mental capacity to imbue said properties. It's very useful for creating a wide array of physical materials.

- [[Crystal]] + [[Luminance]]: Similarly to the interaction with [[Flux]], [[Crystal]] can provide means for physical objects while [[Luminance]] can instead mask them with any visuals by bending the light of them, it gets increasingly complex the larger the surface but it can perfectly mirror the look of anything if the [[Spellweaver]] has the mental capacity. It will remain rigid, however, no matter how "fluffy" does it surface actually looks, it's just an illusion and it remains as hard as the [[Crystal]] that was woven to create the structure.

- [[Luminance]] + [[Void]]: The combination of light and shadow can do the opposite of [[Crystal]], maintaining the illusory shape while there isn't any actual object behind it. Likewise, when pairing it with a third property, it helps to "sculpt" out physical things, it can't sever nor damage the object since it's bending the space around it but it can change how illusion, reflection, and the actual lack of the object feel like. A common use of this property is for "displacing" parts of one's body while showing that they remain attached and creating space for a sucker punch with the real body part. It can't damage while bending but it is useful for deceiving.
  
- [[Void]] + [[Crystal]]: Contrary to using [[Luminance]], [[Void]] can displace or create gravity towards physical objects with [[Crystal]], it can also increase the region of creation of [[Crystal]] by manipulating the shadows of objects as means to manifest [[Crystal]] structures. It is fairly hard to have a large effective range because of [[Signal Loss]], but [[Void]] can help bypass this issue by shortening the range of [[Spellweaving]] manifestation, and the properties it uses when manifesting.
  
- [[Resonance]] + [[Flux]]: This is the primary method to decrease [[Signal Loss]] by reinforcing either Coherence through applying Faraday waves to the [[Great Harmonic Loom]]'s [[Flux]] membrane or by adding effective range to the [[Soul Leitmotif]] by preparing [[Stable Harmonic Channels]] before another spell or property manifests. It also helps to amplify the potency of natural [[Resonance]].

- [[Flux]] + [[Luminance]]: This is the basic principle of forming optical lasers without a direct lens, it redirects the flow of [[Luminance]] by focusing the vectors of light in organized patterns. It usually requires a visual indicator to properly manifest but it's the basis of creating most lasers or convergent light.

- [[Flux]] + [[Strand]]: This pairing tends to create [[Strand Pool]]s and it's useful for applying properties of regular [[Strand]] time dilation. It's a great pairing for illusory magic, changing perception of the natural senses, and it's a basic principle to manifest clean [[Object Permanence]]. For most healing magic, a [[Strand Pool]] is always required as it greatly reduces potential [[Discordant Interference]] by giving a visual indicator for [[Absolute Certainty]] and a healing medium.
  
- [[Strand]] + [[Crystal]]: This pairing is highly unique as it effectively allows to connect [[Probability Amplitudes]] of the possible future with the unique temporal effects of [[Strand]]. It collapses [[Superposed Resonance]] to impose one of the possible futures essentially reversing the flow of time of [[Strand]]. A common use case is accelerating objects by doing reverse [[Strand]] and joining the possible future with the existing present. It creates a "time skip" into the future and can make time move fast forward. However, each future the second collapses is exponentially more difficult to maintain due to the sheer amount of possible futures closing.
  
- [[Crystal]] + [[Luminance]]: This pairing has both a physical effect in casting [[Crystal]] structures to refract [[Luminance]]'s light properties as much as it does in allowing "clarity" into the upcoming future. It is the pairing of focused lasers through lenses and the pairing of divination. Similarly to the [[Crystal]] + [[Strand]] pairing, each further second a [[Spellweaver]] "gazes into the future" gets increasingly more complex to maintain and invites a massive amount of [[Discordant Interference]] due to the unpredictable nature of [[Probability Amplitudes]].
  
- [[Cindergale]] + [[Void]]: This is the only niche use case of [[Void]] as healing magic because [[Strand]] can't interact directly with internal wounds in healing, applying a purifying flame of [[Cindergale]] as means of creating physical touch and bending space and the resulting shadow for a proxy connection to [[Stable Harmonic Channels]]. As a result, this pairing makes it easier to reach the internal damaged tissue. Likewise, it gives a clear visual indicator that previously [[Strand]] healing would not have about the internal body decreasing the complexity of the mental image required to apply [[Sufficient Precision]] with [[Absolute Certainty]]. ### Elemental Harmonic Circle

![[The Principles of Magic-1.png]]

Regardless of complexity, all elemental representations of attuned elements from each of the seven bindings interact with each other based on the [[Major Note]] of the spell. This happens on the principle of the Pentatonic Effectiveness Circle ([[Flux]], [[Cindergale]], [[Crystal]], [[Resonance]], [[Strand]]) and the Dance of Light and Shadow that are effective and weak to one another (Luminance vs Void).

The interactions are as follows:

**Pentatonic Effectiveness Circle:**

**[[Flux]] ▶ [[Cindergale]] ▶ [[Crystal]] ▶ [[Resonance]] ▶ [[Strand]] ▶ back to [[Flux]]

1. **[[Flux]] (Water + Wind) ▶ [[Perfect Focus]] ([[Cindergale]])**
    
    - Thematic: Unrestrained emotion sweeps away scattered thoughts, demanding renewed concentration.
        
    - Physical: Flowing water and gusting winds extinguish and disperse flickering embers.
        
    - Psychological: Honest feelings interrupt tunnel vision, forcing the mind to realign priorities.
        
2. **[[Perfect Focus]] ([[Cindergale]]: Fire + Wind) ▶ [[Absolute Certainty]] ([[Crystal]])**
    
    - Thematic: Sustained, directed will forges vague ideas into solid conviction.
        
    - Physical: Heat and wind reshape ore into precise crystalline facets.
        
    - Psychological: Undisturbed attention crystallizes beliefs, turning potential into unshakeable truth.
        
3. **[[Absolute Certainty]] ([[Crystal]]: Earth + Prism) ▶ [[Key of Attunement]] ([[Resonance]])**
    
    - Thematic: Rock-hard conviction emits a pure tone that realigns one's core purpose.
        
    - Physical: Faceted crystals act as both a trap and sonic resonators, amplifying and ensnaring even whispered intention.
        
    - Psychological: Firm beliefs "ring true" in the self, tuning the soul to its fundamental frequency.
