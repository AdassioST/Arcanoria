Also known as Time Wells or Time [[Crystal]]s.

A type of specialized [[Crystal]] [[Strand Pool]] that is an infinitely looping paradox that has no seemingly way out. It appears whenever [[Strand]] fractures time through an unresolvable causality of events. They are highly dangerous objects that can permanently trap any [[Spellweaver]] for the feeling of an eternity. 

They usually resolve by removing the [[Strand Pool]] at its center that is causing the [[Probability Amplitudes]] to fold upon themselves. For example, a [[Time Bubble]] is if a [[Strand]] user goes to the past to kill their grandfather, it becomes a paradox because without grandfather then his father can't be born, and if their father isn't born then they themselves shouldn't exist. It allows for paradoxes that require the object to start it to be already existing with itself. 

A common misconception of a [[Time Bubble]] is in the [[Ages]] X onwards, where there is a misguided perception that the formation of the [[Proto-Universe]] and [[Known Universe]] happened due to the [[Original Ripple]] and it all began with a theory between two scholars during [[Ages]] VIII when they studied the gravity properties of [[Void]] and its limitations:

_If we're in a finite universe and all the stars in [[Cosmic Motion]] attract each other together, would they not all collapse to a singular point, and if we're in an infinite universe with infinite stars, would infinite forces in every direction not affect all of those stars? How is that any different from a [[Time Bubble]]._

The theory of this [[Time Bubble]] cyclic universe emerges from believing that the [[Known Universe]] began with a loud [[Original Ripple]], but the source of the ripple exists within itself when the [[Known Universe]] retracts and create both a big crunch, and a big bounce which results in another [[Original Ripple]]. By that logic, it would mean that the universe itself is a [[Time Bubble]] but it ignores the reality of [[The Infinite Void]] not being bound by the [[Trinity Harmony]] as that is a construct built by the [[Auric Aria]] from outside of the [[Coherence]] of time.

The [[Looping Paradox]] is the [[Primal Discordia]] of [[Obsessian]] that heavily weaponizes the idea of [[Time Bubble]]s by using them as the infinite "backrooms" where it traps the victims through a complex layering of [[Triad Chord]]s of [[Void]], [[Luminance]], [[Resonance]], and the anchor of stabilized [[Strand]] through a [[Crystal]] [[Strand Pool]] ([[Flux]] + [[Crystal]] + [[Strand]]). It feeds on the victims despair of being stuck in a confine in time outside the perception of the real [[Arcanoria]], delaying time, and waiting until the victims fall to despair and merging the newform of the [[Atonalis]] as the entities roaming its [[Time Bubble]] infinite rooms. It kidnaps [[Spellweaver]]s to make them "props" of its own hallucinations.
