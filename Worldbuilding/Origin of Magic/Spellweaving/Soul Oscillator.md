A keyed instrument that is both a piano and synthesizer-like [[Primary Instrument]] born entirely from [[Resonance]]. It can evolve to develop functions based on the personal journey of the [[Spellweaver]]. Its default sound is that of a piano.

It is a projection of oneself through the [[Soul Leitmotif]]. It allows to create vibration in the same score sheet of [[The Eternal Symphony]], and creates unique sounds based on the [[Spellweaver]]'s [[Fundamental Frequency]]. 

It manifests for those who are the most "lost" on their identity and how they sound, while it is primarily for [[Resonance]] [[Spellweaver]]s, other [[Spellweaver]]s like [[Luminance]] or [[Flux]] can easily manifest it if they're a Stage Performer that doesn't know their true voice yet.

Historically, it predates the real invention of keyed instruments such as keyboards, pianos, harpsichords, as the instrument from the score of [[The Eternal Symphony]] itself which is noted in the pentagram and treble clef. For the early [[Ages]] it serves as a [[Primary Instrument]] and replacement to the lack of pianos, keyboards, and synthesizers while drawing from the own [[Soul Leitmotif]] of the [[Spellweaver]]. Any [[Spellweaver]] able to manifest one is highly valued.

It usually manifests an ethereal keyboard but on advanced [[Ornament]]al [[Spellweaver]]s it can appear as a large grand piano able to change the sounds based on the users' [[Soul-Key]]. It depends entirely on the ability of the [[Spellweaver]] to achieve [[Attunement for Magic]] as the sound they are playing is the literal manifestation of their own's [[Soul-Key]]'s [[Resonance]].
