#spellweaving

_"Formless noise becomes reality only through absolute will. What you cannot imagine cannot come to be."_

The "Future" of the threefold melodies of the [[Trinity Harmony]], and the first statement and popular magic principle of the [[Triadic Virtues of Spellcraft]] which binds [[Absolute Certainty]], [[Perfect Focus]] and [[Sufficient Precision]] together. 

It refers to the capacity of a [[Spellweaver]] of giving form to [[Primal White Noise]] through focused intention from their [[Consciousness]].
