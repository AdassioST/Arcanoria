[[Spellweaving]] can't manifest objects too far away from the caster as they originate from the [[Soul Leitmotif]], and whenever there is a long-range spell, it requires time and preparation to first create temporary [[Stable Harmonic Channels]]. If a spell suffers too much from [[Signal Loss]] they will lose their power and potency.

While [[Signal Loss]] exists from even a centimeter of separation from the [[Soul Leitmotif]], the real issue of the dampening effect of [[Signal Loss]] begins at a range of 3 meters. Usually, [[Spellweaver]]s tend to bypass this issue by channeling the spells directly from themselves and then throwing them or by using clever tricks with [[Void]] to bend the effective space. Likewise, [[Resonance]] can serve as anchor for decreasing the cast time of any [[Spellweaving]] if it is within the carrier wave range.

A few other tricks include using [[Flux]] to leave a trail beneath the spells as pre-made [[Stable Harmonic Channels]]. Finally, the interference of living creatures and their own [[Resonance]] makes impossible to create or cast instant objects within them. The conflicting sound of their own [[Soul-Key]] overrides most common [[Spellweaving]] unless amplified by enough [[Essence Sacrifice]] to bypass the destructive interference of a unique [[Fundamental Frequency]]. However, even then it's more of a gamble sucker punch than an actual practice because using that same excess [[Essence Sacrifice]] could create a spell powerful enough that is more effective at close combat than simply trying to manifest objects inside the existing target.

Conversely, this concept of interference between objects with a [[Fundamental Frequency]] makes also harder to heal internal wounds through [[Strand]] magic, as the healing spell has to practically bypass the same destructive interference from the [[Soul-Key]]. It is much easier to heal an external wound than an internal one as the latter requires a way to access the body with external properties, such as [[Void]]-powered [[Cindergale]] to act as purifying fire that burns through direct skin contact.

Another clever way to avoid [[Signal Loss]] depends entirely on understanding how the mechanics of the [[Known Universe]] function. As matter is just crystalized vibration from [[Consciousness]], a way to establish [[Stable Harmonic Channels]] can be [[Consensual Anchoring]]. This concept essentially refers to deceiving the target that the spell is still "real" effectively using the target's [[Consciousness]] as [[Resonance Anchors]] to increase its potency and effective range. This is a basic principle of Illusory magic, and it can release the burden of complexity on the [[Spellweaver]].
