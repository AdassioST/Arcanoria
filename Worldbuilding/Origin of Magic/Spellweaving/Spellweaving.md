#spellweaving

![[Spellweaving.png]]

Magic come true according to [[The Principles of Magic]]. it's called [[Spellweaving]] because it involves weaving the threads of reality into a composition that then manifests into the divinity of the stars.

Thus [[Spellweaving]] is the willful, deliberate manipulation of the world's inherent [[Resonance]] In the art of being heard, maintaining an ordered relationship against the encroaching entropy of [[Primal White Noise]].

The pipeline is:

 - Intent -> Bending the Strings of Reality -> These Threads become the Pentagram of a Musical Score Sheet -> The Magic Becomes the Threads Connecting the Stars of the Sky as [[Lyrical Fragment]]s and [[Constellation]]s.

Any [[Spellweaver]] requires a [[Soul Leitmotif]] to cast magic.

### The Overview of Magic and Societal Rules of [[Spellweaving]]

#### 1. Magic Always Has a Cost

_[[Spellweaving]] draws transforms power from [[Consciousness]]._

The act of weaving music into magic is a transaction of everything that makes the song of the [[Soul-Key]] governed by the subjective [[Weight of Value]].

This entire principle of transaction for fuel as energy, memories, emotions, lifespan, blood, etc. is governed by the principle of [[Essence Sacrifice]], where the stronger the [[Resonance]] of the fuel and the intent of the spell, the most powerful it will be.

However, paying prices of [[Miracle Magic]] requires a heavy cost to rewrite fate itself. There are many means to decrease the prices from practice, to understanding [[The Principles of Magic]], to feeling the music of your soul through the [[Key of Attunement]], to offloading the power of the spell through [[Emotional Authenticity]]. However, the act of singing against the [[Void]] costs in the dynamics and energy of your existence.

#### 2. Certain [[Spellweaving]] is Forbidden or Taboo

_The [[Great Harmonic Loom]] doesn't have a [[Consciousness]], only the caster does._

[[Civic]]s and acceptance of [[Spellweaving]] depends on an axis of [[Consonance]] and [[Dissonance]]. There are many practices that are considered forbidden or taboo by most [[Civilization]]s which tend to filter anything that is overly dangerous, destructive, indiscriminate, unpredictable, gruesome, cruel, or otherwise unsustainable.

The entire lists of [[Forbidden Magic]] is vast given the flexibility of [[Spellweaving]] but it primarily considers anything that is heavily dissonant and amoral, including:

- [[Velvet Nectar]] and [[Velvet Ambrosia]] which is outlawed almost everywhere.

- Overuse of concentrated [[Lunehymn]] or radiant [[Aetherlight]] to cause addiction and induce states of narcosis and dependency as magical drugs.

- Weaponized pleasure for [[Drowning in Flux]] or other similar sorts of addictions that involve the [[Weight of Indulgence]] for coercion and subjugation.

- Almost all technologies related to the [[Dissonance League]] and [[Atonalis]], such as [[Atonalis]] bombs, using deliberate suffering for experimentation in [[Institute]]s, etc.

- Certain aspects of [[Illusory Magic]] and Shapeshifting that require transforming objects by applying a [[Flux]] [[Crystal]] combo with the intent to have a trojan horse that seems harmless and impales the target when the [[Flux]] property is removed.
	
	- Assassination techniques involving [[Flux]] and [[Crystal]] to create "drinkable water" that then stops the [[Flux]] property once inside the body, its own [[Signal Loss]] causes them to become jagged [[Crystal]] dealing damage from the inside.
	
	- Other similar impalement techniques of creating devices that seem harmless but become jagged when in contact with the body, the worst applications of [[Drowning in Flux]] executions are heavily frowned upon due to its gruesome and degrading nature. They turn pleasure into execution through the manipulation of shapeshifting that become lacerations.
	
	- Invoking any element of [[The Principles of Magic]] right on top or inside of the body with the purpose of gruesome assassination. It usually is ineffective given [[Signal Loss]] and the interference of the [[Soul-Key]], but for those who try to brute force their [[Spellweaving]] inside anyone is intensely amoral.
	
	- Certain practices of the [[Hollow-Point]] that involve [[Crystal]] assassination.

- All types of [[Spellweaving]] that use some sort of disfiguration or self mutilation for fuel conversion:
	
	- This is primarily seen with the [[Flesh-Casing Ritual]] which is the most widespread but still taboo as it is seen as abandoning the body to become a carcass of a person inside their [[Soul Leitmotif]].
	
	- All the practices of hemomancy of the [[Age of Blood]] and onwards for empowering [[Spellweaving]] through bodily sacrifice.
	
	- Any magic considering sacrificing limbs, particularly seen with the Chimera Grafting of [[Spellweaver]]s during the [[Age of Chimeras]] which were using [[Flux]] [[Crystal]] [[Luminance]] flesh cables as glue to grafting limbs and then using them as fuel. It is the advanced version of the hemomancy loophole. However, this does lead the gruesome states of necrosis that are only survivable if the [[Spellweaver]] has undergone a [[Flesh-Casing Ritual]]. However, even with the physical decay the practitioners are incredibly likely to become a [[Carnalix]] [[Atonalis]] due to the sheer amount of physical suffering and emotional trauma of amputation and grafting.

- [[Illusory Magic]] used for forgery, mimicry, or otherwise deviant behavior involving deceased people and coercion. This is treated heavily as a [[Dissonance]] magic almost mirroring the [[Atonalis]] behavior of an [[Erosyx]], an [[Animach]] or a [[Violux]].

- Treating any [[Time Bubble]]s or [[Strand Pool]]s as means to create prisons which act effectively as time torture chambers.

- Hijacking someone's [[Emotional Authenticity]] in emotions like terror to make them complicit in their own destruction through parasitic [[Sympathetic Vibration]] as [[Stable Harmonic Channels]] for bypassing [[Signal Loss]] as seen with [[Syvanth]].

- [[Hyper Chord]]s of indiscriminate destruction as seen with [[The Grand Fusillade]]. While not [[Spellweaving]], [[Wolf Bomb]]s are largely considered in the same category given their genocidal nature.

- [[Flux]], [[Crystal]], [[Luminance]] flesh cables or anything using the connecting of artificial tissue to mirror biological horror, as seen with the [[Age of Chimeras]]. This is further used in its most advanced version through [[Hypermage]]s.

- Almost any type of powerful [[Outer Magic]], as it is inherently unpredictable, and they involve channeling power from an [[Outer God]], inviting an apocalypse into [[Arcanoria]], and increasing the scale of the consuming horror through its [[Flesh-Binding Ritual]] and [[Flesh-Exalting Transcendence]].

#### 3. Magic Cannot Create Something from Nothing

As seen with the principle of [[Essence Sacrifice]], [[Spellweaving]] can transform, manipulate, or transfer, but not truly create. 

This is the real difference of the permanence of magic within objects between normal [[Spellweaving]] and the [[Law of Relics]] on [[Ages]] IV onwards. Still, these [[Magical Relic]]s can only be obtained after [[Lacrimosa]]'s [[The Hollowing]] has been resolved, and the only type of permanence in objects lies as physicalized relationships that can become [[Magical Relic]]s and [[Cursed Objects]] depending on consent, [[Coherence]], and [[Dissonance]]. It transforms [[Echoing Bonds]] through their memory imprint and [[Emotional Residue]] rather than creating from nothing.

#### 4. Emotional Instability Affects [[Spellweaving]]

This section is fundamental as [[The Principles of Magic]] are governed by [[Consciousness]], psychology, and the bindings of states, the primary ones related to emotion are [[Emotional Authenticity]] which translates the gradient of impact from an object, [[Key of Attunement]] as identity and personal alignment, and [[Perfect Focus]] which requires maintaining peace of mind amidst the chaos of the ongoing storm.

All types of [[Spellweaving]] in [[The Principles of Magic]] require their set of constrains for functioning, which sublimates psychology and emotion into matter. This also affects deities like the [[Auric Aria]] which created [[Aetherlight]], [[Sky Glass]], [[Rose Seed]]s, [[Emberwhisper]], and [[Glimmerfern]] from emotion. [[Selenea]] [[Lunehymn]], and [[Luceatfilum]] and the [[Auroral Ribbons]] with [[Lacrimosa]].

No deity, mortal, [[Pure Light]] being or otherwise existence can bypass the emotional imprint that governs existence as [[Consciousness]] predates matter.

#### 5. Magic Leaves Traces Behind

Powerful spells, and emotions leave imprints of [[Emotional Residue]] which are combination of [[Flux]] and [[Strand]] that tend to be detected as [[Strand Pool]]s of many types. These traces are the basis and founding blocks for [[Detective Magic]]. These properties exhibit the following complex characteristics:

- The [[Strand Pool]]s born from [[Emotional Residue]] scale proportionally to the intensity of how many lives were affected and the intensity of the emotions felt. This is the principle of how psychology becomes geology as these [[Strand Pool]]s can sublimate into [[Memory Field]]s on very strong [[Emotional Residue]].

- As the [[Emotional Residue]] are [[Strand Pool]]s there are some that can collapse into a massive [[Soliton]] that sometimes becomes a [[Time Bubble]] which is both dangerous and a perfect preservation of a paradox stuck in the past.

- [[Emotional Residue]] is the main trace left behind on any [[Spellweaving]], which makes spells have a trace beneath depending on the type of fuel and emotion it was using governed by the binding of [[Emotional Authenticity]] and [[Essence Sacrifice]]. 

- The [[Echoing Bonds]] of a spell allow for detecting the traces that lead back to the original [[Spellweaver]]. It can be amplified through objects that carry [[Echoing Bonds]] such as belongings that meant something to that person, personal gifts, etc. This expands to [[Magical Relic]]s and [[Cursed Objects]] on [[Ages]] IV. The detection of [[Emotional Residue]] is the basis for [[Detective Magic]] and following trails. The technique was pioneered by [[Cordelia]] when following [[Amadea]] through the [[Waltz of Wandering Love]] that established [[Echoing Bonds]] between both of them.

- [[Emotional Residue]] has a complex relationship with [[Pure Light]] beings given their vitality and emotional nature, some of these creatures can track precisely [[Emotional Residue]] and sometimes they even digest the [[Strand Pool]]s as seen with the [[Memory Singer]]s. However, this also became the basis of [[Velvet Nectar]] on its most macabre and twisted applications.

- [[Pure Light]] beings that detect or heavily rely in [[Emotional Residue]] are considered as [[Spiritual Tracer]]s given their relationship to [[Strand]] and [[Echoing Bonds]]. The higher [[Pure Light]] over [[Auric Structure]] the more potent they are as a [[Spiritual Tracer]], but of course this comes at the disadvantages of not being adaptable.

- Some [[Atonalis]] also weaponize this principle for their own parasitic [[Resonance]] and tracking which victims have a wound frequency that mirrors their own.

- [[Emotional Residue]] can be analyzed through highly sophisticated lenses of a "time microscope" that can read even the smallest imprint of [[Strand Pool]]s. This is the counter measure to [[Illusory Magic]] to detect the exact state of the [[Emotional Authenticity]] left regardless of what [[Luminance]] may visibly signal.

- Divine [[Emotional Residue]] is the most potent type of [[Emotional Residue]], as seen with the [[Auric Aria]] whose actions in the [[First Reset]] make the ideas of everything that happened in the [[Old Testament]] bleed through the first [[Prophet]]s of the [[Auric Order]]. Keeping core ideas such as the [[Seven Dark Fates]], the [[Ark of Genesis]], [[Paradise Lost]], the lost [[Library of Heaven's Tower]], and other similar ideas such as the concept of demons, who are mistakenly grouped with [[Atonalis]] and fallen [[Auric Angels]], and the concept of the day of judgement in the split of heaven above vs hell below through the [[Celestial Vault]]. This is the reason why the [[Auroral Ribbons]] purified on the [[Cataclysmic Aftermath]] of the [[Great Fracture]] in the climax of the [[Polychord Crisis]], and allowed [[Lacrimosa]] to begin receiving signals from [[Arcanoria]] across [[The Infinite Void]].

Furthermore, the [[Strand Pool]]s of [[Emotional Authenticity]]  is why [[Humanity]] in [[Arcanoria]] can't remember facts as much as they remember feelings. It mirrors real-world psychology where a person doesn't remember the data you said in the words, but how those words made them feel. The record of emotions is [[Emotional Residue]], and it explains from a magical approach neuropsychology as [[Consciousness]] predates matter.

#### 6. Overuse of Magic Causes Physical Damage

This is the aftermath of [[Essence Sacrifice]], once the transaction has been done, the overuse of sacrificial energy of one's own [[Consciousness]] pays its toll, whether as physical exhaustion, damage, weakened senses, trembling hands, etc.

On the economy of [[Essence Sacrifice]] there are a few things to note:

- If a [[Spellweaver]] physically passes out after using too much of their stamina for sustaining magic, their [[Soul Leitmotif]] reverts from [[Awakened State]] or Wearable state back into its Dormant State, and will cause the [[Spellweaver]] to rest forcefully until they can sustain their own [[Coherence]] again.

- On the extreme uses of fuel with hemomancy, not having a [[Flesh-Casing Ritual]] proves fatal most of the time as the bodily decay causes the internal biology to rupture due to blood loss, blood transfusion, etc.

- Other types of fuel like memories, and foundational [[Miracle Magic]], come at a heavy cost that tends to create walking tragedies as seen with [[Elara]], [[Eyras]], and  [[The Cradle of Echoes]]. The hardest part about performing any [[Miracle Magic]] against fate is surviving the aftermath of the whiplash of [[Coherence]].

A [[Spellweaver]] engaging in self destructive behavior is heavily taboo, and they tend to be labelled as "Spiraling Fuel [[Spellweaver]]s." The strategy to surviving a fight with one of these kamikazes is endurance. Since the loop is unsustainable, a candle burning too bright will eventually run out of fuel, the only condition is to survive before, and given that their [[Spellweaving]] is heavily amplified by everything they are destroying, they are usually going to overpower a pure magical fight.

The optimal way to deal with one of these [[Spellweaver]]s is to wait for them to self-destruct, and only engage after they have burned down all of their identity, being taken either by the [[Atonalis]] they're leaving behind, or by the exhaustion of the [[Coherence]] whiplash they will suffer in the moments after the spiral cascade is over.

#### 7. Magic Requires [[Sufficient Precision]]

Mistakes, lack of [[Perfect Focus]], vague intent or faulty [[Absolute Certainty]] can completely alter a spell, failing into [[Discordant Interference]] which can fail in the best cases, and backfire completely in the worst.

[[Spellweaving]] is inherently dangerous given the flexibility it has to alter the physical space. The information and purity of the [[Frequency Harmonics]] embedded within any [[Spellweaving]] depends on the quality of the [[Sufficient Precision]] and [[Luminance]] binding, and the mastery between a terrible [[Spellweaver]] and a master lies in their ability to use through creativity and their personal strengths each of [[The Principles of Magic]]. Leaning towards the ones they master the best, and leaving the offload or simpler work to those who they are the least advanced in.

[[Spellweaving]] is as much of a science field of physics, philosophy, and mathematics of sound engineering in the study of magic, as it is an artistic performance of feeling the song of your own soul and playing it to the uttermost truth of your emotional gradient and subjective appreciation of beauty and personal history. It combines the awe of soft magic with the systemic rules, practice, and mastery of hard magic.

#### 8. Some Places Suppress Magic

[[Arcanoria]] and the [[Great Harmonic Loom]] are both a living infrastructure ecosystem that uses [[Coherence]] as its primary metric to determine harmony, [[Consonance]] and [[Dissonance]]. The atmosphere is layered by [[Vibrational Density]], and [[Leylines]] which act as the nervous system and lifeline of [[Arcanoria]].

As zones function through [[Vibrational Density]] and zones of [[Coherence]], the geology of a region determines stability and [[City Development]] for [[Civilization]]s.

High [[Coherence]] zones and [[Leylines]] act as the magical pathways for [[Stable Harmonic Channels]] that allow for magic to run free with little to no [[Signal Loss]]. These are primordial for [[Trade Nexus]], natural harbors, and [[Trade Nodes]] while allowing for [[Pure Light]] beings to exist within the environment. Moreover, this environmental regulation is governed by the [[Dual Confluence Stream]] that runs through the entire living system of the planet.

However, there are some other zones of low [[Coherence]] present [[Dissonance]] where magic is much less effective, less powerful, and actively harmful, particularly in areas of [[Vibrational Fallout]] where [[Chaotic Resonant Cascade]]s can make the [[Discordant Interference]] of [[Spellweaving]] unpredictable and dangerous.

The entire mesocosm of political conflicts depend on this shifting environment that is also regulated by the [[Grand Thread Rings]] that shift every [[Age Transition]], requiring [[Resonance Stabilizer]]s to keep the [[Coherence]] of a region by changing the flow of the local [[Leylines]]. Sites of exceptional [[Coherence]] are [[Sacred Site]]s, and other key [[Landmark]]s. The most interesting place of [[Arcanoria]] with this phenomenon is the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] that feature the complete spectrum.

After [[Ages]] IV, [[Luceatfilum]] exists in the transition zones of [[Coherence]] gradients.

#### 9. Magic Reacts Differently Under Certain Conditions

Magic is subject to [[Cosmic Motion]], [[Cycle]]s, [[Echo]]es, [[Phase]]s, and other environmental changes such as the moving [[Grand Thread Rings]] of the [[Great Harmonic Loom]]. Each establishes periods of high magical affinity, such as the [[Ritual Seventh]], the [[Rite]]s of each day, and the [[Echo of Crescendo]] as the climax of magical [[Resonance]].

Moreover, some elements of the classical alchemy can react to the application of magic which makes them useful as tools and also imbued with [[Civic]], culture, and theological value. Primarily, this is seen in [[Lunehymn]] and its concentrated variations with reacting properties it has on [[Resonance]] which makes them perfect clocks for tracking the [[Pulse]]s of the [[Leylines]].

The [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] is one of the [[Landmark]]s that best display this changing behavior:

- [[Sky Glass]] exhibits similar properties in changing with the environment, functioning fundamentally different in the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] as it does everywhere else.

- The crescent mist of [[Lunehymn]] that rises through the inverse waterfalls is functionally different in carrying narcosis and [[Emotional Residue]] at a much higher concentration than it would do elsewhere.

- The affinity of magic and the tides of the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] change, with the [[Echo of Crescendo]] being the best time to attempt pilgrimage on the mountain.

- The [[Lunar Abyss]] is one of the chambers with the most reflective properties that change and attune based on the [[Rite]] of the day and the [[Lunar Calendar]].

Magic changing is one of the most foundational pillars of [[Civilization]] building, [[Rite]]s, [[Civic]]s, major [[Events]], ability to commune with [[Outer God]]s, the presence of some [[Atonalis]] and [[Primal Discordia]] such as [[The Amber Wonderland]], and the individual perception of their own magic shifting in frequencies through the [[Pulse]]s and [[Bar]]s of each day, [[Seventh]], [[Cycle]], and [[Act of Fate]].

#### 10. Magic Can Become Addictive

As [[Spellweaving]] is a fundamental tool that can allow access to several layers of life, it is too subject to the [[Weight of Indulgence]] in several shapes:

- Magic is power, and power is addictive, it can transform anyone just like political power does, and it is intrinsically tied to both in most [[Regalia Pillar]] societies, particularly, when a large part of the populace is being used as [[Consensual Anchoring]] [[Resonance Anchors]] to increase the power of a single soloist, which is the basis of certain tyrannical rules in [[Fanatical]] [[Regalia Pillar]] societies that use the [[Coherence]] of their populace as means to increase the volume of their own [[Fundamental Frequency]].

- Aside from the [[Regalia Pillar]], the [[Motif Awakening]] can be a pivotal point for many [[Spellweaver]]s in [[Civilization]]s whose [[Civic]]s tend to aesthetize it as part of the coming of age, such as in the Duels of Severance, or in the case of [[Void]] users who use it to signal that this is proof of their loss, like [[Pollux]].

- [[Lunehymn]] and [[Aetherlight]] both have powerful effects on [[Consciousness]] and they can become addictive potent drugs due to the side effects they induce on continued use on their concentrated and radiant forms respectively. Moreover, in other substances such as [[Velvet Nectar]], they become ontologically addictive due to the ability transfer that rewards harvesting skills from other people rather than learning on your own.

- The commodification during industrialization makes every [[Dark Age]] start relying on cruel methods of using [[Spellweaving]] as power, it is seen in both axis as the [[Age of Velvet Elegance]], and in the [[Age of Sealing Iron]] despite being completely contrary [[Pillars]]. This tendency goes further in the [[Age of Chimeras]], and even to the [[Age of False Moons]].

There are some other instances and applications but they are all tied to the [[Weight of Indulgence]], and in some minor degree to the [[Weight of Value]], [[Weight of Change]], and [[Weight of Flaw]].

#### 11. Dead Magic Zones Exist

As [[Arcanoria]] stratifies by the [[Vibrational Density]] of the [[Great Harmonic Loom]], there are certain areas that are  dead zones that subject all [[Spellweaver]]s to be treated as mortals. These come from two primary sources:

- Natural Dead Zones: Like the [[Crescent Mist Peaks]] in its lower levels or these can be naturally made by advanced [[Vibrational Fallout]]. Other [[Atonalis Nest]]s are considered to be dead zones given the low [[Coherence]]. Similar [[Landmark]]s can exhibit dampening properties.

- Manufactured Dead Zones: Depending on the type it can be from [[Events]] like divine discharge such as [[The Silent Expanse]] after the [[Eighth Cycle]], backfire of [[Hyper Chord]]s, as seen with [[The Grand Fusillade]] in [[Ages]] V, or through the [[Wolf Bomb]]s with the lingering regions filled with the [[Wolf Tone]] and necrotic [[Symphonic Veins]].

#### 12. Magic Reveals Character

[[Spellweaving]] is fundamentally attached to the subjective experience of existence starting by which [[Armament]] and [[Primary Instrument]] does a [[Legend]] even use to survive and cast magic.

From there, it stratifies into many categories like the entire [[Stellar Legacy Score]] which considers from [[Legend Trait]]s that define origin, wound, [[Motif Awakening]] wish, personality traits and [[Spellweaving]] mastery that evolve organically based on [[Lyrical Fragment]]s, accumulated [[Legend Opus]], [[Legend Title]]s, and everything else that constitutes a [[Constellation]], their [[Stellar Covenant]]s, and [[Syncretism]] that reflects the relationship between its individuals, mortals, and [[Spellweaver]] [[Civilization]]s.

Similarly, since the music is tied as a [[Magical Catalyst]], the expression, composition, and performance of each piece of music tied to the [[Soul Sheet Music]] of a [[Legend]], they have different effects, just like how expression changes based on who plays a piece, the magic differs in strength, potency, side effects, etc., as personal nuance is added to the expression of the [[Soul Leitmotif]].

Finally, the [[Soul Leitmotif]] is the ultimate expression of the [[Legend]] as it functions as a mood ring, a bio feedback monitor, a [[Composure]] meter, and a personal biography with [[Ornament]]s and its primary binding signaling the attunement to one of [[The Principles of Magic]].

In a world where psychology, theology, magic, emotion, and physics are all united, the expression of the soul against entropy is the key for transformation through the [[Triadic Virtues of Spellcraft]] that determine as much character in life as a [[Legend]] as they do for a [[Constellation]] dictating the [[World Truth]]s of the next set of [[Legend]]s.

#### 13. "Ancient" [[Outer Magic]] is Fundamentally Different

Ancient magic is commonly referred to as [[Outer Magic]] due to its ties with [[Outer God]]s, it tends to predate [[Arcanoria]] given the nature of [[The Infinite Void]]. It all depends on when was the [[Outer God]] born, but it unequivocally follows the same patterns:

- As It relies on [[Outer God]]s, it tends to be reality warping and unpredictable with situations that are heavier, stranger, and harder to control while having existential threats and cosmic horrors that can twist the normal laws of physics, and the homeostasis of harmony in [[Arcanoria]].

- These events require a host for its [[Flesh-Binding Ritual]], and begin using [[Piety]] as [[Resonance Anchors]] and [[Consensual Anchoring]] of [[Humanity]] that can transform into a [[Flesh-Exalting Transcendence]] unleashing the apocalypse of whichever horror was invoked. The principle that allows the [[Regalia Pillar]] to increase the potency of an individual [[Spellweaver]] due to population is the same principle that allows an [[Outer God]] to incarnate and gather power through [[Piety]].

Moreover, there are several theological debates on this subject of what classifies as [[Outer Magic]] due to [[Selenea]] and the [[Auric Aria]]. This extends because both the influence of [[Selenea]] and the [[Auric Aria]] exhibit the same properties as [[Outer Magic]] given their divine nature:

- Particularly on the case of [[Selenea]], the moments she meets [[Civilization]] through the Selenic Set of [[World-Bending Relics]], she unleashes the Daydreaming Sky from her overwhelming joy. She may be lonely, but she is still an [[Outer God]] with unpredictable consequences that become entropy, chaos and loss of [[Coherence]], which is one of the main issues to solve during the [[Sonata]] of the Pale [[Moon]].

On the subject of the [[Auric Aria]], calling the [[Auric Aria]]'s power "[[Outer Magic]]" is considered a profound insult and blasphemy because it fundamentally contradicts the orthodox doctrine of the [[Auric Order]], which elevates her as [[The One True God]] and the singular creator of the [[Known Universe]] and [[Arcanoria]]. Traditionally, instead of being grouped with the powers of [[Outer God]]s, her abilities are exclusively revered as [[Auric Magic]] or Divine [[Spellweaving]].

The distinction between her power and [[Outer Magic]] is both deeply theological and mechanical, driven by several core reasons:

- **The Paradigm of Order vs. Chaos:** The [[Auric Aria]]'s influence constitutes the foundational physics of reality itself, structured through the [[Auric Heptacode]] and the [[Trinity Harmony]]. Her followers view her power as the ultimate expression of [[Mastery Over Chaos]] and absolute order which is provable by the existence of Primordial Stars. By contrast, [[Outer God]]s are entities born in [[The Infinite Void]], and their magic introduces unpredictable, reality-warping cosmic horrors and existential threats. Equating the structured architecture of the Creator with the chaotic entropy of [[Primal White Noise]] is deeply insulting to her faith, particularly because the [[Auric Aria]] heavily despises all other deities born in [[The Infinite Void]].

- **Mechanical Superiority of Manifestation:** There is an undeniable cosmological difference in how the [[Auric Aria]] wields her divinity. [[Outer God]]s lack the [[Sufficient Precision]] to directly modify [[Arcanoria]] and to even pierce the [[Stellar Veil]]. They are too vast and incompatible with physical reality, if they ever come through they instantly get forced to become [[Alien Star]]s. As such, to interact with the world, they have a host performing a [[Flesh-Binding Ritual]] to occupy an [[Anthropomorphic Shape]], which often culminates in a catastrophic [[Flesh-Exalting Transcendence]] that unleashes an apocalypse. In this regard, the [[Auric Aria]] is undeniably distinct because she possesses the unique capability to manifest and incarnate her own form at will with only the requirement that she takes three [[Seventh]]s to incarnate. She can create and modify her own [[Anthropomorphic Shape]] at will without needing a mortal vessel or triggering an apocalypse-inducing scenario.

- **The [[Aureus Pillar]] / [[Chorus Pillar]] Divide:** The classification of her magic is a central point of ideological warfare within [[Civilization]]. The [[Aureus Pillar]] zealously defends her status as the supreme sovereign, believing power is found in the [[Auric Aria]] and the indomitable human spirit which is provable due to Primordial Stars and her might in [[Mastery Over Chaos]]. However, the opposing [[Chorus Pillar]] rejects this anthropocentric view as hubris, favoring the esoteric and arguing that true power lies with the [[Outer God]]s of the infinite unknown. Labeling her power as [[Outer Magic]] effectively aligns with the heretical views of the Chorus Pillar, directly challenging her divine supremacy. In essence, calling her power [[Outer Magic]] is a heresy that undermines her role as the sovereign architect of existence, reducing her from the author of reality to just another of the parasitic deities from [[The Infinite Void]]. This is further pushed on the [[Scorching Truth]] that [[The Auric Aria is a Type G Star]], showing her limitations as a deity in contrast to the rest of the cosmos of the [[Lunar Abyss]].

However, her manifestation of power through [[Piety]] does have physical effects in the body of those who are her chosen and become [[Auric Angels]], particularly due to the divine [[Resonance]] they carry in their wings as their tether to the [[Auric Aria]].
