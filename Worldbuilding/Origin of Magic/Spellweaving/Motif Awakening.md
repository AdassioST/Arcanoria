#spellweaving

*"Your wound is not your fault, but your healing is your responsibility - the difference between a Monster and a Hero is not the absence of pain, but the mastery of it."

The moment an emotion becomes so vivid it breaches the veil to signal the skies.

You only receive a [[Soul Leitmotif]], the moment after hitting "rock-bottom." It is a deeply and intimidate personal moment of realization that awakens the magic of any [[Spellweaver]]. All magic starts unstable because these are awakened at the most vulnerable but the [[Spellweaver]] must equally find the strength to weave both magic and resolve their personal conflicts. If the user awakes mid cast their [[Motif Awakening]], it's likely to become a [[Catalytic Abyss of Emotion]].
 
Furthermore, a [[Motif Awakening]] is structured by a single "Wish" and whatever is vocalized is the phase-lock pairing of the soon to be [[Spellweaver]], and the [[Great Harmonic Loom]]. While these wishes aren't really granted as if they were given by a genie, they do serve as the philosophical backbone and driver of purpose against the [[Weight of Purpose]]. 

A [[Soul Leitmotif]] [[Motif Awakening]] marks the first time a [[Spellweaver]] hears the song of their own soul hence the name "motif" as a musical term. Moreover, it is too the explicit signal of defiance against the encroaching static of [[Primal White Noise]]. Thus, a [[Motif Awakening]] wish is the physical burning desire of signal isolation amidst [[The Infinite Void]] from the [[Superposed Resonance]] of a [[Fundamental Frequency]]. It is a declaration against the chaos to establish [[Coherence]] which sublimates into a physical object as the [[Soul Leitmotif]] and the embellishments as [[Ornament]]s of the song it carries.

All [[Motif Awakening]]s generate [[Fragment of Meaning]] and [[Fragment of Defiance]], while some may produce specific [[Lyrical Fragment]]s associated with their binding. 

It's important to differentiate that [[Motif Awakening]]s are not always tragedy, they are overwhelming expressions of [[Emotional Authenticity]], while it's more common that a tragedy can trigger a [[Motif Awakening]] to find meaning, intense joy can trigger [[Motif Awakening]]s as well. The [[Great Harmonic Loom]] does not distinguish between feelings, only a [[Consciousness]] does. Its [[Grand Thread Rings]] can only read the amplitude of [[Emotional Authenticity]], and if it crosses the threshold, the [[Soul-Key]] will sublimate into an expression of the [[Soul-Key]]. If its the first [[Motif Awakening]], it will create a [[Soul Leitmotif]], if it's already created, the song of the [[Soul-Key]] will be embellished with its [[Ornament]]s, primary and secondary, if its the second or the third [[Motif Awakening]] respectively.

In the case of a completed [[Soul Leitmotif]] with both of its [[Ornament]]s the overwhelming feeling will drown in the [[Catalytic Abyss of Emotion]] forcing an [[Awakened State]].

A starting [[Spellweaver]] just had its first [[Motif Awakening]] that seeded magic in them from their wound. An advanced [[Spellweaver]] will have experienced another critical event in their lifetime, granting them their first [[Ornament]]. A master [[Spellweaver]] will have fully embellished their soul with its two [[Ornament]]s, and only beyond the level of a virtuoso will that [[Spellweaver]] unleash their [[Awakened State]].

### Awakening to the elements of [[The Principles of Magic]]

_"The first time your soul has a song. Not because it didn't have any sound before, but because you couldn't hear it."_

A [[Motif Awakening]] can awaken to different elements on the exact same event, it all depends on how [[Consciousness]] process it. This is evident in [[Void]] and [[Luminance]] pairs. A [[Spellweaver]] who had lost a relative to the [[Great Plague]] and is basking on the depths of this loss could either:

- Awaken to [[Void]] if they begin to become nostalgic about the loss and the shadow it left behind, feeling the [[Void]] as a raw emotion before anything. The most important part of the loss is exactly how the defining relationship is severed and whether the [[Spellweaver]] continues to have any worth. They are being crushed by the [[Weight of Value]].

- Awaken to [[Luminance]] if instead of being overwhelmed by emotion, they rationalize the loss trying to understand the meaning of "Why did this happen?" if they are fixated on the truth as defensive mechanism, they will be aligned with the compulsion of the piercing light. They are being crushed by the [[Weight of Flaw]].

Another common pairing of opposites is with [[Flux]] and [[Cindergale]] which both interact with the innate condition of a [[Spellweaver]]'s internal architecture. The very [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Flux]], [[Orphael]] embodies this condition. His primary drive is the [[Lustful]] nature he was born as part of his [[Weight of Nature]], it's inescapable in the sense that he will always be [[Lustful]]. However, the awakening to [[Flux]] instead of [[Cindergale]] stems from the shame this [[Legend Trait]] is generating him. 

The inhibition of emotion due to shame from his [[Empathetic]] nature is colliding directly with his need of bodily urges, not just as a physical suppression but as an inability to express himself through emotion that eventually breaches the dam and becomes his [[Flux]] [[Motif Awakening]] Wish: _"I wish to feel in a way that creates meaning, to stop running from the shame and desire I've always carried."_

[[Orphael]] was being crushed by his [[Weight of Nature]] but focused on the [[Weight of Purpose]] as means to channel it without succumbing to the [[Weight of Indulgence]].

On the contrary side, [[Motif Awakening]]s can be born of pure joy or validation as well. This is demonstrated by [[Eyras]] on his final [[Motif Awakening]] to his secondary [[Ornament]] of [[Resonance]] when the lover of his new family reassures him during the fight against [[The Cradle of Echoes]] that he does belong in a family.

The sheer intensity of the [[Emotional Authenticity]], not trapped in the past but by creating a future by confronting what came before triggers a [[Motif Awakening]]. On the cusp between a moment of belonging and profound joy of finally finding a place to rest is the catalyst he requires to vanquish both [[The Cradle of Echoes]]. He heals his own wound and gets the happy ending of the family he couldn't have before.

Other joyful [[Motif Awakening]]s are regularly to [[Flux]] with wishes like: _"I wish for this moment to last forever."_ referring to the overwhelming feeling of a moment of vulnerability and intense satisfaction while being granted [[Fragment of Catharsis]].

### [[Ornament]]s in [[Motif Awakening]]s

The introduction of secondary and tertiary bindings is much more than mere specialization in hybrid elements. These represent the chronological scars of how has a [[Legend]]'s [[Ballad]] developed and the key moments of their character development. They aren't necessarily tied to the expression of the original wound but tend to thematically align with the choices of the [[Spellweaver]] as the wound gives the defensive mechanism towards surviving the [[Weight of Purpose]].

An example of this can be found in the story of [[Lacrimosa]] who is a [[Void]] [[Soul Leitmotif]] primary and [[Ornament]]s of [[Crystal]] and [[Resonance]]. Her magical career tracks her character arc:

- After witnessing the [[Betrayal of the Moon]] in the [[Seventh Cycle]] the loss of the identity she cherished and built around romanticizing collaboration with the [[Auric Aria]] on the creation of the [[Known Universe]] vanishes her self worth. This profound loss of meaning creates the first [[Motif Awakening]] to [[Void]] that becomes her [[Soul Leitmotif]] through her wish: _"I wish I was seen, and that I mattered."_

- Much later, when she's already incarnated in [[Mira]], at the [[Age Crisis]] of [[Ages]] II, the [[Purest of Love]] conducts the first [[Flesh-Exalting Transcendence]] of [[A Pact of a Thousand Eyes]]. [[Lacrimosa]] ascends from "The Dreamweaver" persona to the [[All-Loving Moon]]. This overwhelming, burning determination and ambition to change reality to become undeniable is her second [[Motif Awakening]] that becomes a [[Crystal]] [[Ornament]], while formally marking the start of the apocalyptic [[World Event]] of [[The Hollowing]].

- The final and secondary [[Ornament]] of the melody of her [[Soul-Key]] happens during the climax of [[The Hollowing]] when she has her third [[Motif Awakening]] to a [[Resonance]] [[Ornament]] during her Judgement of One-Million Eyes when she's on the ground holding her blindfold and crying through [[Mira]]'s eyes in human tears. This completes her fully [[Ornament]]al [[Soul Leitmotif]], and is the direct catalyst for any of the endings of [[The Hollowing]].

- On one of this endings, [[The Mass Suicide of Lacrimosa]], [[Lacrimosa]] experiences a final and fourth [[Motif Awakening]] after the despair overcomes her due to failing the broadcast gamble. This intense despair sends [[Lacrimosa]] into a loop of flagellant auto destruction and intense laceration that sinks her to the [[Catalytic Abyss of Emotion]], triggering a her [[Awakened State]] that unravels the fabric of reality with the purpose of self annihilation on an impossibly powerful large tear of the [[Great Harmonic Loom]] into [[Primal White Noise]].

Aside from [[Outer God]]s, another key [[Motif Awakening]] that isn't born from despair but rather overwhelming tears of relief was the [[Luminance]] [[Ornament]] in [[Amadea]] which manifested the moment the planted seed from [[Artus]] grew, and it turned out to be a seed of a regular [[Auric Peach]] tree. This happens because her whole reality and determination has existed alongside this ideal of the violet garden. 

The sheer cognitive friction processed as joy and liberation of learning that the seed was an [[Auric Peach]] all along, one of the most common trees and not a legendary one is a moment of pure [[Emotional Authenticity]]. [[Amadea]] laughs finally in tears of relief: _"Oh my, you adorable idiot, [[Artus]]. We were chasing the very end of the world all for an [[Auric Peach]] Tree. But now seeing it in the center of the [[Iridia]], it is the only golden light between all the violet. Perhaps that was the lesson all along."_ [[Amadea]] unlocks her [[Luminance]] alongside lots of [[Fragment of Lucidity]].
