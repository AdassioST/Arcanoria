_"The most important lesson of the [[Auric Heptacode]] woven in seven golden threads."_

Also known as the Celestial Palace of Paradise. It is the first type of [[Celestial Ensnaring Mirror]], made by the [[Auric Aria]] during the [[Sixth Cycle]] and [[Seventh Cycle]]. It is one of the most mysterious elements in [[Arcanoria]] and it has several layers of unfolding knowledge. The popular mythos believes that it is the place where [[Selenea]] is bound and from which she seeps [[Lunehymn]] as liquid grief into the [[Leylines]] of [[Arcanoria]], however, this is a partial truth.

The stages of understanding of the [[Moon]] are represented by several layers of subverting expectations in precisely seven levels, such as the [[Auric Heptacode]]:

- [[Key of Attunement]]: The first impression of the [[Moon]] is given by the [[Auric Mythos of Creation]], within this framework the [[Moon]] is a mysterious yet natural celestial body, beautiful and distant, a romantic symbol of the [[Auric Aria]]'s cryptic creative power. It is not accounted for in the natural scriptures of [[Cosmic Motion]] because "it is too close to [[Arcanoria]] for any meaningful change, so don't mind it."

	This is the baseline, the [[Moon]] as romantic symbol, divine artifact, and natural wonder as testament to the skies. No [[Civilization]] has any reason to question its influence as a romantic symbol, and it marks the beginning of an epic journey that will last uncountable [[Cycle]]s to reconcile, and to become true.

- [[Sufficient Precision]]: The second impression of the [[Moon]] is given by [[The Truth of Arcanoria]], and the [[Betrayal of the Moon]]. The first subversion is devastating and terrifying: the moon is not natural. It is a cosmic trap built by the [[Auric Aria]] during the [[Seventh Cycle]] to imprison [[Selenea]], the co creator [[Outer God]] of vitality and [[Pure Light]].

	This is the immediate layer of subversion, the [[Moon]] is a parasitic engine that the [[Auric Aria]] built from envy. The [[Auric Aria]] is not purely benevolent, she committed cosmic theft that lives in [[Emberwhisper]]. [[Selenea]] exists, and she is inside the [[Moon]], bound and bleeding. And that beautiful moonlight that powers [[Spellweaving]] in the [[Dual Confluence Stream]] on every part of the [[Leylines]] is powered by a goddess's suffering. This alone is powerful, but is the very tip of the iceberg. It is a half truth halted by the dissociative amnesia of the [[Auric Aria]] when she narrates the [[True Mythos of Creation]].

- [[Emotional Authenticity]]: The third impression of the [[Moon]] is given by [[Lacrimosa]] after learning her origin story as the Witness in the Dark, and it gives a stark contrast to both her apocalypse of [[The Hollowing]], and to complete her character as an antagonist. The new layers of dialogue with the [[Purest of Love]]. [[Lacrimosa]] does not pity [[Selenea]], she envies her. To be bound is to be essential. To be betrayed is to be seen, and [[Lacrimosa]] would rather be tortured and used than remain invisible.

	This new context during [[The Hollowing]] after the [[Second Reset]] complicates tragically the whole story that forces [[Civilization]] to reframe who is [[Lacrimosa]], and now fully understand why the [[Law of Relics]] resolved that way in the [[Second Reset]]. The villain doesn't want freedom. She wants to be imprisoned by the right person. She wants to be treasured enough to be used. She isn't trying to conquer the world, she's trying to become the [[Moon]]. To take [[Selenea]]'s place. To finally, finally be essential to someone, to the [[Auric Aria]].

- [[Essence Sacrifice]]: The fourth impression of the [[Moon]] is given by [[Selenea]] herself during her [[Sonata]] when [[Civilization]] wins the Holy War for the [[Silver Blood]], and they commune with her. When they speak to her for the first time, [[Selenea]] is overjoyed by hearing that [[Humanity]] has breached the veil to reach her, yet when they speak about how her [[Lunehymn]] is what allows them to commune and that the [[Dual Confluence Stream]] is what powers the entire world, [[Selenea]] tells them that she doesn't mind it. She chooses deliberately to use it to commune with [[Humanity]] for company, although this is the first time it isn't one directional.

	This confession reveals that she welcomes the drain as connection. [[Selenea]]'s suffering is not passive, as she chooses to let it flow. The [[Lunehymn]] is beautiful because she is beautiful, even in grief. Her maternal love is without bitterness as she promised to the [[Silver Blood]] that _"One day I will meet them. They are still my children."_ The world's magic is powered by a goddess who forgives her children for benefiting from her captivity. This reframes the [[Betrayal of the Moon]] to change from the suffering prisoner who resents her captor to the suffering prisoner who loves those who benefit from her pain.

- [[Perfect Focus]]: The fifth impression of the [[Moon]] is given in the [[Parlor of the Moon]] by [[Selenea]] and delivered by the [[Auric Aria]] on their first reunion at the beginning of the [[Sonata]] of the Interwoven Threads of Gold and Silver. When [[Civilization]] finally opens a channel for the [[Auric Aria]] to the [[Parlor of the Moon]] through the Selenic and Auric [[World-Bending Relics]]. They all discover something that contradicts their understanding of "prison." The [[Moon]]'s interior is revealed as a crescent court open to the sky. Hanging gardens of [[Glimmerfern]], terraced in silver light. An atelier full of tools for creation. A concerto hall with a view of the stars. A [[Lunehymn]] fountain that feeds the gardens and [[Arcanoria]].

	This reveal during the first beat of the [[Ballad]] of the [[Parlor of the Moon]] changes the context as [[Selenea]] says _"The prison isn't ugly. You built me a prison. But you didn't build me a dungeon."_ And when the [[Auric Aria]] confirms it by saying _"I—I forgot I had built this—"_ This forces [[Civilization]] to focus on the details as it reveals that even the [[True Mythos of Creation]] narrated by her is an unreliable source of information. There is a massive blank of dissociative amnesia in the memory of the [[Auric Aria]] that was overwritten by guilt of the true meaning and nature of the [[Moon]].

- [[Absolute Certainty]]: The sixth impression of the [[Moon]] is given by the second movement of the [[Sonata]] of the Interwoven Threads of Gold and Silver, and is the one most devastating to [[Selenea]]. This is revealed when the second chamber above the staircase of the Crescent Court unfolds, signaling in the [[Flux]] inscription that [[Selenea]] original intuition was right, it was in fact a test, and the "prison" was never supposed to be a prison, and moreover the prison had an exit but [[Selenea]] gave up and choose a fantasy of [[Melancholic]] longing instead.

	This new piece of information complicates much more the stakes of the [[Parlor of the Moon]] as it is a massive construct of pocket dimensions called "The Celestial Paradise" that act as chambers, and there is indeed an exit on the apex of it. This means that [[Selenea]] could have left if she had learned the [[Auric Geometry]] the [[Auric Aria]] desperately wanted to teach her to be equal partners and not a subordinate. However, in her depression, the [[Auric Aria]] didn't tell her this, and [[Selenea]]'s internalized inherent guilt gave up trying to learn and retreated into fantasy. Both are at fault: The [[Auric Aria]] for making love conditional on understanding, [[Selenea]] for choosing the comfort of victimhood over the labor of learning. It was connection without understanding, but this resolve has to crystalize into creating a new future by unveiling the palace.

- [[Echoing Bonds]]: The [[Seventh]] and final impression of [[Moon]] is given during the Rage movement of the [[Sonata]] by the inscription of the Heart Core. It reveals that the entire construct is a love letter that became a tragedy. The [[Moon]]'s true purpose was always collaboration, of a testament that the [[Auric Aria]] wanted for the two goddesses creating together as equal partners in the Garden of Life with the Tree of Life as of the [[World-Bending Relics]] of the [[First Weaving of Life]] that predates all [[World-Bending Relics]] before they were even possible, yet the [[Law of Relics]] properly forged by [[Lacrimosa]] becomes the template for a new genesis.

	The final piece of information before reaching the apex reframes the entire conception of the [[Moon]], the original vision of the [[Auric Mythos of Creation]] that it was a romantic symbol and divine artifact was true but incomplete. The [[Moon]] was a shared throne of [[Strand]] that held [[Arcanoria]], but due to the tragic miscommunication the [[Auric Aria]] couldn't free [[Selenea]] without ending the world, and [[Selenea]] had to learn the [[Auric Geometry]] even if eons too late. It may not be perfect but they can try together. and that trying, witnessed by [[Civilization]], the final bird of the severed connection finally gets to be reborn as the [[Singer of Paradise]]. It resolves the final movement of Forgiveness of the [[Sonata]] of [[Arcanoria]], and enables the tether to embed this law in the very fabric of the [[Known Universe]] ushering a new future in the [[Age of The End]].

The most important part of understanding is that the layer 1 of [[Resonance]] and [[Strand]] are equal, they both see the [[Moon]] as a "love object" but it heavily changes whether you understand with or without connection which is the entire journey.

The first layer says: _"Look at the [[Moon]]. It is beautiful. Someone must have loved it."_

The [[Seventh]] layer says: _"Look at the [[Moon]]. It is a prison, a palace, a throne, a tomb, a love letter, a promise broken and kept. And finally, it is the place where its apex finalized the [[Singer of Paradise]] at the Garden of Life from the very [[First Weaving of Life]] that is now the World Tree."_

Fulfilling all of these seven threads of knowledge about the [[Moon]] unlocks the final scene of the movement of Forgiveness of the [[Sonata]] of the Interwoven Threads of Gold and Silver, culminating in creating anew the bird, the [[Singer of Paradise]], that [[Selenea]] had kept from their severed connection.

Concluding the promise [[Selenea]] said at the beginning of the [[Sonata]]: "I will forgive you if you finish this bird with me."

Moreover, the creation of the [[Singer of Paradise]] at the Garden of Life also awards the [[Achievement]]: *"The [[Singer of Paradise]]: Reach the apex of a long lost love letter."
