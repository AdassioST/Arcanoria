The [[Cataclysmic Aftermath]]s, also known as the Great Filters are the three distinct [[Age Crisis]] of [[Gateway To Genesis]] that are unsolvable on the first try and plunge the world into a new timeline reset beginning from the [[Age of Desolation]]. Each of these is tied with [[The Ultimate Weapon]] and represents one essential part of the palindrome of the 3 x 4 x 7 that makes the amount of [[Beat]]s per [[Seventh]]. They are essential to the core loop of [[Arcanoria]] cyclical structure, are deeply tied with the progressive reveal of information about [[The Ultimate Weapon]], and the [[Crisis Wonders]].

The three [[Cataclysmic Aftermath]]s acting as Great Filters are the following [[Age Crisis]]:

- The [[Polychord Crisis]]: The first filter in [[Ages]] VI that is the culmination of the [[Holy War]] that ends with the master-key of [[The Ultimate Weapon]] being activated and the firing causes the end of the [[First Reset]] and [[Second Reset]].

- The MAD [[Wolf Bomb]] Annihilation: The second filter in [[Ages]] XI of the Atomic & Information [[Ages]] that culminates with the [[Institute]] of Anti Existence soteriology firing every single [[Wolf Bomb]] over all major population centers to reduce the world to ashes believing they are cutting the tail of the snake of the ouroboros of violence. However, their lack of knowledge about [[The Ultimate Weapon]] makes the attempt to end all existence futile.

- The Unraveling of [[Primal White Noise]]: The final filter in [[Ages]] XIII before the [[Age of The End]] which requires finishing the [[Sonata]] of Interwoven Threads of Gold and Silver (The [[Sonata]] of [[Arcanoria]]) to unlock the final escape by creating [[A True Sine Wave]] which represents the change from [[Civilization]] to the Third Actor representing the first time the game breaks the fourth wall to find a [[Consciousness]] without existence, recognizing that there is one type of [[Consciousness]] that wasn't born within [[The Eternal Symphony]] and is the key to the new Age of Genesis.

Each of them are a pivotal moment for [[Arcanoria]] and have each several implications on unlocking various layers of knowledge and of gameplay. The [[Divine Reset]] mechanic and the Prestige is tied to the [[Achievement]]s unlocked in total of all the runs.

The resets are the following:

- [[First Reset]]: The [[Old Testament]] era when the [[Auric Aria]] came back from her silence of the [[Lost Cycle]]. Before the Game.

- [[Second Reset]]: The doomed [[New Testament]] and beginning of the game which ends with the [[Polychord Crisis]]. Early Game.

- [[Third Reset]]: The first half of the real game, it is more than one reset depending on the player, but the entire part is named [[Third Reset]] until they clear both [[Sonata]]s of the [[Auric Aria]] and of [[Selenea]], concludes in the MAD Annihilation. Mid Game.

- [[Fourth Reset]]: The second half and final stage of the real game, it is likewise too more than one reset depending on how many [[Divine Reset]]s [[Civilization]] incurs but it is the last stage of the game until the very [[Age of The End]]. Late Game.

The Master-Key evolves in 4 phases:

- Tier 1: Acquired after [[Civilization]] fails the doomed [[Second Reset]] [[Polychord Crisis]] and the [[Auric Aria]] uses the last tether between them to reveal [[The Truth of Arcanoria]]. This is the transition from the Early Game to the Early Mid Game.

- Tier 2: Acquired after [[Civilization]] in the [[Third Reset]] successfully navigates and acquires the Master-Key proper breaking the loop of the [[Polychord Crisis]]. Transition from the Early Mid Game to the entire Mid Game.

- Tier 3: Acquired after [[Civilization]] fails the [[Wolf Bomb]] Annihilation during the Third World War in [[Ages]] XI. This unlocks the counter of [[The Ultimate Weapon]], reveals advanced [[Crisis Wonders]] and transitions into the Early Late Game.

- Tier 4: Acquired after [[Civilization]] finishes the [[Sonata]] of [[Arcanoria]]. This signals the true Late Game and unlocks the [[Age of The End]].

This crisis attempts to make the 3 of the equation reduced to 0 by attacking the [[Trinity Harmony]] and its continuity to unravel reality. 0 x 4 x 7

- It signals the end of the [[Second Reset]] through [[The Truth of Arcanoria]] that unlocks many layers, secrets, the presence of [[Crisis Wonders]], and begins the [[Sonata]] of the Golden Sovereign and the [[Sonata]] of the Pale [[Moon]]. This is the access through the truth to the Tier 1 Outside of [[The Ultimate Weapon]] which is what makes [[Civilization]] claim the Master-Key.

- It formally begins the "[[Third Reset]]" which allows fulfilling and passing the filter of the [[Polychord Crisis]], unlocking this awards [[The Ultimate Weapon]] Master-key which gives [[Civilization]] the ability to use the [[Divine Reset]] mechanic, enhancing the future runs of the [[Third Reset]].

It also unlocks [[Crisis Wonders]] but without the ability to purify them into proper [[Megastructure Wonder]]s. ### The MAD [[Wolf Bomb]] Annihilation

_"The selfish intent of trying to preserve peace initiates wars, genocides and justifiable atrocities in the name of shameless justice."_ [[Wolf Tone]] [[Institute]]

This crisis attempts to make the 7 of the equation reduced to 0 by attacking the [[Auric Heptacode]] and its topology to unravel reality. 3 x 4 x 0

This is the second [[Age Crisis]] and Great Filter that separates the previous [[Ages]] which is triggered by the accelerationist anti existence [[Institute]] league of the [[Wolf Tone]]. It is the climax of the Third World War of [[Arcanoria]]. It is the division between the old world fantasy and the sci-fi aspect of [[Gateway To Genesis]].

- The hyper accelerationist [[Institute]] of [[Ages]] XI, [[Wolf Bomb]]  Anti Existence. onwards believes in soteriology by the death of the [[Known Universe]]: "One day we will all return to the static that precedes the very existence of the cosmos." Their rush to end the world is a driver to the next MAD reset. "The stars fall from the sky, the world is reborn." The [[Institute]] preaches that [[Primal White Noise]] is the true god of the universe and that it's impossible to break the ouroboros cycle of violence as wars have existed from the very beginning of [[Humanity]] and will continue to exist long after these [[Ages]] so the best thing they can do is to cut the tail of the snake and eradicate all life. Their logo is a nuclear symbol alongside the depiction of a wolf. 

- The catastrophe is triggered after the previous Two World Wars have already occurred and it is the climax resolution of the [[Wolf Tone]] [[Institute]] after they manage to denotate every [[Wolf Bomb]] levelling all of [[Arcanoria]]. Once they win they will declare in the cinematic, as the [[Wolf Bomb]] detonations blast the skies and the earth they preach, "It's finally over. The [[Cycle]] is broken. No more Death, no more Revolutions, no more Wars. No tyrant will become the head of the snake ever again." This formally begins the "[[Fourth Reset]]."

This unlocks advanced [[Crisis Wonders]] which include the ruined cities of [[Civilization]] that were nuked by the [[Wolf Bomb]]s. Instead of being isolated complex, the advanced [[Crisis Wonders]] are incredibly advanced arrays of [[Megastructure Wonder]]s, ranging from planetary disks, to factories, to ruined space elevators, and several types of weaponry and skyscrapers. It also makes every previous [[Crisis Wonders]] able to be enhanced into [[Megastructure Wonder]]s giving them back their former glory.

### The Unraveling of [[Primal White Noise]]

This crisis attempts to make the 4 of the equation reduced to 0 by attacking the [[Cosmic Motion]] and the introduction of real cosmic [[Primal White Noise]] that begins devouring reality. 3 x 0 x 7 [[Crescent Mist Peaks]]

- This is the true [[Primal White Noise]] and it's the original static that [[The Ultimate Weapon]] was made to combat, it is the [[Megastructure Wonder]] of why the [[Hypermage]] was made. The crisis of the [[Hypermage]]s will always result in the encroaching static devouring and seeping from every [[Megastructure Wonder]] unless there is the fix to ember the [[Law of Relics]] into the fabric of reality and replace the role of the [[Moon]] by changing the equation.

- As such, the next and final stage is only fixable by the presence of the Third Actor after concluding the [[Sonata]] of [[Arcanoria]] doing the transition of how they reached the Apex at the [[Parlor of the Moon]]. This requires finishing the entire [[Sonata]], which opens the Tier 4 of the Master-Key, the [[Hypermage]] core where all the stasis pods are in an eternal dream. It reveals the real wish: _"I wish for you to turn back the clock of fate, to reverse time to a gentler past before [[Civilization]] even began, to save [[Humanity]] and give it a future beyond the [[Ages]] that doomed this timeline if the encroaching infection of true [[Primal White Noise]] severs the tethers of the [[Stellar Veil]], and it stops the symphony of [[Cosmic Motion]] or destroys the fabric of the Firmament ever again. No matter the cost. No matter how many attempts or lives it takes. Exploit the last [[Resonance Anchors]] of [[Coherence]] of every remaining [[Consciousness]] to rebuild a wiser [[Civilization]]."_

The presence of [[A True Sine Wave]] is the resolution of the conflict, and having every layer of Tier 1 through 4 represents the 4 of [[Cosmic Motion]] which symbols the Third Actor and the player. This is why the famous phrase of the [[Age of The End]] is coined. _"The [[Original Ripple]], [[The Infinite Void]], and the [[Known Universe]] all began with the Third Actor, and [[Arcanoria]] will end by the very same Third Actor."_

**Once entering the [[Age of The End]], [[The Ultimate Weapon]] deactivates permanently, making it a one way trip into the last [[Cataclysmic Aftermath]]**

The last [[Cataclysmic Aftermath]] is the Choice of the End which directs any of the 3 endings of [[Gateway To Genesis]]:

- The End of [[Arcanoria]], by having [[A True Sine Wave]] reverberate through [[The First Overtone]] in a failed state effectively deleting all of existence and the hard drive copy of [[Arcanoria]]. This is the Bad Ending Option which multiplies the universe by 0. _It doesn't remove any of the save file data, it just leads to the start of the Choice of the End so that [[Civilization]] can try again without impairments, and is given an advantage to prevent failing again._

- The End of the Third Actor, by removing the 4 of the equation to forge a path for [[Civilization]] at the cost of the metaphysical tether. This is the Auric Ending Continuation to keep playing,

- The Reset of [[Arcanoria]], by having the [[Auric Heptacode]] increase to the power of -3, after listening to the final plea of the [[Auric Aria]]. This is the True Ending which creates the Relic of [[Arcanoria]] and awards The Purest of All Love [[Achievement]].
