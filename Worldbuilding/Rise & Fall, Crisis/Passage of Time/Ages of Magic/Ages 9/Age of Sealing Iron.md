Subduing faes and other [[Pure Light]] beings to be used as machine engines through iron cages within the machines.

### Context

The [[Age of Sealing Iron]] is the ultimate, brutal culmination of a species deeply traumatized by its own magical infrastructure, terrified of divine intervention, and desperate to secure its survival through absolute control. To understand why [[Humanity]] resorts to subjugating faes and [[Pure Light]] beings within iron cages during this era, one must trace the psychological scars left by the [[Great Plague]] of Euphoria, the horrors of the [[Outer God]]s, and the bloody evolution of "forbidden" medical technology.

### The Trauma of the [[Great Plague]]: The Betrayal of [[Pure Light]]

The foundational trauma for humanity's relationship with magic and [[Pure Light]] begins in [[Ages]] I with the [[Great Plague]] of Euphoria. Before the plague, humanity lived in harmony with [[Pure Light]] creatures, most notably the [[Luminant Moths]], which were widely domesticated and beloved for their beautiful light and economic utility.

However, this reliance on magical infrastructure inadvertently triggered an apocalypse. The intense extraction of magic from the [[Leylines]] created a massive imbalance in the [[Dual Confluence Stream]], which in turn activated dormant fungal spores that weaponized the bacteria inside the [[Luminant Moths]]. The result was catastrophic: the beloved moths became invisible vectors for a bio-magical neurotoxin, [[EC]], that hijacked the human nervous system, causing uncontrollable dancing, Euphoria, and eventually, apocalyptic magical nuclear explosions known as [[Static Criticality]] cascades.

This instilled a deep, civilizational trauma: magic and [[Pure Light]] beings are inherently volatile and existentially dangerous. [[Humanity]] learned the hard way that their love for light and magic created the very catastrophe that nearly wiped them out. Relying on the natural flow of the [[Great Harmonic Loom]] proved to be a death sentence.

### The Trauma of the [[Outer God]]s: The Danger of Divine Dependence

As humanity reeled from the [[Great Plague]] and subsequent crises, they sought salvation from the heavens, making themselves vulnerable to the [[Outer God]]s, most notably [[Lacrimosa]] during [[Ages]] II and III. [[Lacrimosa]] took advantage of [[Humanity]]'s profound grief and desire to escape suffering by orchestrating [[The Hollowing]].

By creating a cult of the "[[Purest of Love]]," [[Lacrimosa]] convinced desperate populations to surrender their [[Consciousness]] to her in exchange for numbness and peace, actively attempting to merge all of humanity into the giant [[All-Loving Moon]]. This period, known as the [[Age of the False Messiah]], proved that appealing to the divine or relying on cosmic entities only leads to mass manipulation, the loss of human agency, and the erasure of individual identity. [[Humanity]] realized they were entirely at the mercy of entities that viewed them as either tools or vessels. This was further exacerbated by the wars of cults during [[Ages]] V with the [[Age of the Sacred Cross]], [[Age of Horrors]], and the [[Age of Covens]].

Later the development of hemomancy in the [[Age of Blood]] demonstrated that there's a loophole in [[Essence Sacrifice]] if you use someone else's vitality to fuel your own. [[Vampire]] mages that sacrificed their own blood for fueling spells and then used blood transfusions to earn their blood back proved in theory that [[Pure Light]] too could be rerouted.

### The Birth of "Forbidden" Technology and the Drive for Safety

Driven by the compounded trauma of apocalyptic plagues and predatory gods, humanity began seeking methods of survival that relied on physical laws, science, and [[Auric Structure]] rather than unpredictable magic and divine mercy.

This shift began painfully. In [[Ages]] IV, a doctor ([[Vals Jenner]]) studying the leftover traces of the [[Great Plague]] became the first epidemiologist to develop a vaccine. However, because society was still gripped by magical and religious superstition, this life-saving technology was deemed "forbidden," and she was executed for treason and witchcraft.

Despite this tragic setback, the seeds of scientific control were planted. This forbidden research directly inspired the brutal but revolutionary work of Dr. [[Cadmus Tacet]] in [[Ages]] V. Operating under the philosophy that [[Humanity]] must carve out its own survival, [[Cadmus Tacet]] pioneered the first surgeries and experiments on [[Pure Light]] beings to understand the [[Auric Structure]] vs [[Pure Light]] spectrum and [[Coherence-Binding Tissue]]. While his methods were exceptionally cruel, they represented a necessary scientific advancement in understanding how to manipulate and control the very beings that had once brought both wonder and plagues. By subjugating [[Pure Light]] biology to the scalpel of human reason, Dr. [[Cadmus Tacet]] sought to remove the volatility of magic, famously coining this era the first true "[[Age of Humanity]]".

### The [[Age of Sealing Iron]]: Progress Through Absolute Control

By [[Ages]] IX (the Industrial Era), [[Civilization]] enters its second "[[Age of Humanity]]". The compounding historical trauma has solidified a singular, unshakeable philosophy: **Safety is the primary drive of civilization, and safety can only be achieved through total structural control over magic and vitality.**

This leads directly to the [[Age of Sealing Iron]], a Dark Fae Fantasy era defined by subduing faes and other [[Pure Light]] beings. Having learned from the [[Great Plague]] that [[Pure Light]] creatures left to their own devices will metabolize ambient magic and cause catastrophic cascades, and having learned from the [[Outer God]]s that free-flowing magic invites cosmic predators, [[Humanity]] decides that magic must be caged.

**The iron cages inside their machines are the ultimate trauma response.** By trapping faes and forcing their innate magical essence to act as mechanical engines, [[Humanity]] successfully achieves a different path of industrialization. The fae engines represent the perfect synthesis of [[Humanity]]'s desires:

1. **Uninterrupted Progress:** They still get to utilize the boundless "Vitality" and energy of Pure Light.
2. **Absolute Safety:** By encasing the faes in iron—a symbol of rigid "[[Auric Structure]]" and technological control—they prevent the magic from flowing freely and creating [[Dissonance]], Plagues, or [[Static Criticality]].

In this tragic era, [[Humanity]] driven by the concept of [[Perfect Reflection]] essentially replicates the original sin of the creator deity, the [[Auric Aria]]. Mirroring the [[Betrayal of the Moon]] but now in an anthropocentric view as the crowned creation of [[Arcanoria]]. The [[Age of Sealing Iron]] proves that [[Humanity]]'s historical trauma has taught them that the only way to survive in a chaotic, magical universe is to become the ultimate jailers of it, and it is divine right to do so.
