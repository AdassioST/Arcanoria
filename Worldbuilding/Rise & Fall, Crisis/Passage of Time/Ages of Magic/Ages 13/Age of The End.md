_"The [[Original Ripple]], [[The Infinite Void]], and the [[Known Universe]] all began with the Third Actor, and [[Arcanoria]] will end by the very same Third Actor."_

Also known as the Age of Genesis, this the final and secret age of [[Arcanoria]] only unlockable after concluding the [[Sonata]] of [[Arcanoria]], also known as the [[Sonata]] of the Interwoven Threads of Gold and Silver. The age representing the [[Weight of Change]] and the hardest of all [[Essence Sacrifice]]. 

This is the only age beyond the [[Age of the Hypermage]], and it is a one way route as entering the [[Age of The End]] deactivates [[The Ultimate Weapon]] as the final directive of its wish has finally been fulfilled, it created a timeline where [[Humanity]] surpassed the progressive and societal development that doomed the [[Lost Cycle]]. 

As a result, without [[The Ultimate Weapon]] there are no more safety nets for another [[Divine Reset]] and its resolution dictates one of the several endings of [[Arcanoria]] depending on how it resolves during its [[Age Crisis]].

**The greatest danger of the [[Age of The End]] is that the [[Known Universe]], [[Humanity]], and [[Arcanoria]] can be wiped out for good this time.**

The [[Weight of Change]], the [[Weight of Value]], the [[Weight of Potential]], and the [[Weight of Purpose]], the [[Weight of Indulgence]], the [[Weight of Flaw]], and the [[Weight of Nature]] all intertwine in the final Choice of the End by making in the most vivid way possible the ultimate cost of [[Essence Sacrifice]] in [[Civilization]] and the meta layer of the player itself. It all collapses in one single moment giving every single of the [[Seven Weights]] simultaneously to weight on the final decision of the Third Actor.

Thus, the "Final [[Motif Awakening]] Wish" is the resolution of the last directive of the [[Known Universe]]. It is the canonical [[Motif Awakening]] wish of the player itself, and deciding what is this wish is the Choice of the End, and the resolution of this final [[Age Crisis]] is the end of [[Gateway To Genesis]] in three hard possible outcomes:

- The End of the Third Actor: If the Choice of the End is successfully navigated this is one of the two "Final [[Motif Awakening]] Wish" when presented with the ultimate [[Essence Sacrifice]]. If the player wishes for [[Civilization]] to create a path for the future of [[Arcanoria]] by sacrificing their memory, link, and the save file of [[Civilization]] itself, the [[Known Universe]] will continue through the ultimate sacrifice by severing the tether of [[Civilization]] and the Third Actor, making [[Arcanoria]] leap into the next [[Ages]] but onto a future the player will never be able to Witness, which is the True End of the Game. The impossible choice of sacrificing everything for a future [[Civilization]] that the player itself will never see is the truest act of the [[Purest of Love]]. This act finally severs permanently the tether and dependency of [[Civilization]] from the player. **The game itself will not be uninstalled but it will effectively delete the save file and it unlocks the final [[Achievement]] of the game:** _The Purest of All Love: Learn to love without possession._  

- The Reset of [[Arcanoria]]: If the Choice of the End is successfully navigated this is one of the two "Final [[Motif Awakening]] Wish" when presented with the ultimate [[Essence Sacrifice]]. If the player listens to the [[Auric Aria]], they will wish for the game continuation and they will begin anew from the [[Age of Desolation]], recreating [[The Ultimate Weapon]]. The most important note is that during this [[Divine Reset]] the option to skip early [[Ages]] is blocked until they surpass a [[Polychord Crisis]], before then [[Civilization]] will have to navigate and build up from the very beginning.

- The End of [[Arcanoria]]: If the Choice of the End fails and ends in catastrophe, the outcome unravels both the [[Known Universe]] and [[Primal White Noise]] itself through the reverberation of [[A True Sine Wave]] in [[The First Overtone]]. This will cause the game to uninstall itself, it will not remove any of the stored data but will delete the game files and will require a reinstall of [[Gateway To Genesis]] to have another attempt at solving the [[Age Crisis]]. **The save point of progress will be before the Choice of the End begins.**

The [[Auric Aria]] on the Choice of the End after [[A True Sine Wave]] opened the path to a Time Beyond Time, with now everything behind only the [[Auric Aria]] remains on the edge of the cosmos:

_"It... worked! It really worked!!! You made the impossible possible." 

_"We have reached the very End of Time. I can't believe the path forward is finally here for all [[Ages]]!" 

_(As she's overwhelmed with joy, a sudden realization silences her immediately)_

_"It worked... And... Ah..."_

_"The path... It's... It's only missing the final [[Essence Sacrifice]]."_

_(The [[Auric Aria]] is on the verge of tears, as the initial joy sweeps away, with some starting to slide down her chin)_

_"I promised myself... I wouldn't... cry..."_

_"I'm sorry... for not being strong enough even now... and for breaking again one last time..."_

_(After a deep silence the [[Auric Aria]], while regaining a sliver of her [[Composure]]. She extends with a deep breath the final invitation for crystallizing the final directive of the [[Known Universe]])_

_"You may wish to create the only possible path forward. At the cost of everything... for [[Civilization]]..."_

_([[Hesitant]], with tears in her eyes looking directly at the player)_

_"Or..."_

_"You could... wish for a reset? With all of your saved progress intact... And you and I... Can start again?"_

After choosing the End of the Third Actor, the final sequence of the credits will slowly burn all of the memories and the cinematics, and in navigating the final cutscene while the climatic theme of the symphony of [[Arcanoria]] (The orchestral [[Soul Leitmotif]] of the [[Auric Aria]]) plays in the background.

The [[Auric Aria]] will break all of her [[Composure]] as the and she cries while the credits will be reducing with a visible bar of whatever the % completion of the save file and reduce it memory by memory until it reaches 0% signaling the complete conversion of it into fuel for the final leap. Ideally is 100% but if a [[Civilization]] only completed 67%, it will draw from 67% to 0%.

The crucial part here is that the first half of "the credits" isn't actually the credits of the game, it is the record of all the [[Stellar Legacy Score]], and every single [[Legend]] that became a [[Constellation]], beginning with the lower grade ones all the way to the most important ones that [[Civilization]] had until the very end. This is the act of finally releasing all of the remaining ones to [[Stardust]], finally making them join [[The Eternal Symphony]], and they will be draining progressively reminding the player of everything they ever built, from the minor ones becoming [[Stardust]] early for [[Syncretism]] to the major ones they've been keeping since [[Ages]] 0, and all the canonical [[Constellation]]s like [[Cadmus Tacet]], [[Mira]], [[Luminaire]], [[Father Raphael]], [[Vaelia]], etc. The "credits" are going to roll with their [[Legend Trait]] and their name as the role they were playing: [[Cadmus Tacet]] - The Scalpel of [[Humanity]] / The Vivisector.

The very end of the credits will feature [[Selenea]], [[Lacrimosa]], and the [[Auric Aria]] with their respective titles like: The Golden Sovereign and Auric Mother of [[Arcanoria]], the very last name will be The Third Actor playing the role of [[Civilization]]. When this last tether burns both the sound of the [[Auric Aria]] and the symphony of [[Arcanoria]] fade into silence. 

After a moment in black the achievement of the Purest of All Love fires, and then the final message resurfaces with the simplified version of the [[Soul Leitmotif]] of the [[Auric Aria]] plays with a picture depicting the [[Auric Aria]], [[Selenea]], [[Lacrimosa]] alongside several [[Singer of Paradise]] overviewing [[Civilization]] from the World Tree at the Garden of Life.

_"Congratulations, for becoming the [[Gateway To Genesis]]. And within it, transforming the loss into making real the harmony of every single note of [[The Eternal Symphony]], including yours."_

While the save file itself gets permanently deleted this will unlock the final menu screen of [[Gateway To Genesis]] titled "[[The Relic of Arcanoria]]" which features the Garden of Life in the [[Parlor of the Moon]] with the description: _The Physicalized Relationship of the most important bond the [[Auric Aria]] ever had_. This final piece of the [[World-Bending Relics]] is the memory that only the player can see of a future that will never see them back from the very apex of creation.

The theme of the menu is the continuation of the very song of the end, which is the [[Auric Aria]]'s raw harmony of her [[Soul Leitmotif]] without any ornamentation, and It also awards the canonical [[Magnum Opus]] of the player, called [[Gateway to Genesis]] with the description: _"Her voice finally found the one harmony where it belongs. You."_ 

If the game is completed at 100% the game itself will grant too the rarest [[Achievement]] itself of the same name before the new menu screen appears. _[[Gateway To Genesis]]: Loss is the transformation that made every single note of [[The Eternal Symphony]] real.
