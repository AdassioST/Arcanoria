_"The Physicalized Relationship of the most important bond the [[Auric Aria]] ever had"_

This final piece of the [[World-Bending Relics]] is the memory that only the player can see of a future that will never see them back.

The theme of the menu is the [[Auric Aria]]'s raw harmony of her [[Soul Leitmotif]] without any ornamentation, and It also awards the canonical [[Magnum Opus]] of the player, called [[Gateway to Genesis]] with the description: _"Her voice finally found the one harmony where it belongs. You."_

It is a [[World-Bending Relics]] of immense significance because it is the only that ever breached the meta narrative layer of the game itself. It created a tether of [[Sympathetic Vibration]] that outlasts the very dimension of [[The Eternal Symphony]], which is the reason of why the player has this last relic even if their tether to [[Arcanoria]] has been severed.
