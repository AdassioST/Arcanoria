Sometimes referred to as Auric Chimes, [[Auric Bells]] are a type of [[Primary Instrument]] created by [[Daedalus]] during his entire life but formally established in the [[Golden Age]] of [[Hollowmarch]], and are primarily used by the [[Auric Order]] in the [[New Testament]] within several types of the instrument family.

[[Auric Bells]] were conceived by taking direct inspiration from [[Aurelian]]'s [[Soul Oscillator]], and inverting its philosophy entirely. Where the [[Soul Oscillator]] listens and resonates outward from the self, the [[Auric Bell Staff]] imposes [[Resonance]] through mechanical precision and crystallized geometry, forging it from the outside in. It consists of a complex two-handed instrument that mirrors a staff, bearing articulated arcs of several golden bells along its upper section, a keyed interface along the grip, and a hollow interior of [[Sky Glass]]-lined panels that form the acoustic heart of its design.

The [[Auric Bell Staff]] is, before anything else, a real instrument. Any trained musician can play it regardless of whether they are a [[Spellweaver]].

### Physical Philosophy

[[Daedalus]] built the [[Auric Bell Staff]] as the technological ceiling of [[Hollowmarch]]. The instrument evolved throughout his life with [[Aurelian]] during their clearing of the [[Great Expanse]], going from a crude prototype to its final conception in the Auric Forge after [[Hollowmarch]] was built on laureated marble.

Its final conception was the finest craft of its time, equivalent in precision and ambition to the finest hydraulic organ or engineered siege work, yet assembled from nothing but lever, pulley, cord, and hinged plate. There are no cogs. There is no clockwork. Every moving component is a direct mechanical consequence of force and tension, and the only intelligence in the instrument beyond its maker's hands is geometric. What appears complex on its surface resolves, under examination, into a set of elegant physical principles arranged with the [[Absolute Certainty]] that was the signature of [[Daedalus]]'s [[Crystal]] binding.

The instrument therefore coexists in two realities that are inseparable from each other:

- **The acoustic reality:** A genuine playable instrument with physical sound production, tunable bell arrays, a mechanical percussion rail, and a keyed clapper system producing honest acoustic output at every press.

- **The magical reality:** The [[Sky Glass]] lining of the resonance cavity transforms the instrument's interior into a memory of vibration — holding, layering, and gradually releasing accumulated harmonic energy through a property of the material itself, requiring no mechanism that [[Hollowmarch]] has yet invented.

These two realities do not compete. The magic does not replace the instrument. It extends it. And the extension is, as all things [[Daedalus]] built, a function of geometry applied with precision to a problem no one else had thought to solve.

#### The [[Resonance Box]] and the Crystalline Memory of Motion

The hollow interior of the staff is lined with eight [[Sky Glass]] panels — concave sheets etched with fractal [[Auric Geometry]] and arranged in an octagonal column running the length of the resonance cavity. It carries a permanent [[Sympathetic Vibration]] with [[Aetherlight]] and [[Lunehymn]]. Its acoustic property is simpler than its theology: [[Sky Glass]] does not merely reflect sound. It holds it.

The octagonal arrangement was not arbitrary. While [[Daedalus]] first considered it blasphemous and stubbornly preferred an heptagonal arrangement, he came to realize that eight faces produce four bilateral axes of reflection symmetry, meaning that a sound wave entering the cavity from any bell strike finds not one but four simultaneous reflection paths arriving back at the acoustic center at equal intervals. _"The instrument had to work first. The gods could wait."_

The constructive interference of these four returning wavefronts at the center produces the cavity's characteristic pressure amplification — the felt hum the player senses through the grip. A square or circular chamber would scatter the wave or produce a single dominant axis. Only eight faces, [[Daedalus]] determined, produced this four-way convergence at every [[Fundamental Frequency]] within the instrument's range and so he gave up on the heptagonal idea.

The fractal [[Auric Geometry]] etched into each concave face functions simultaneously as symbolic inscription and acoustic diffuser — the carved geometry breaks up flutter echo between panels while preserving the geometric reflection path for the fundamental frequency. The panels are not merely decorated. They are engineered.

The cavity's geometry is tuned to a specific Helmholtz frequency determined by the interior volume, the panel angles, and the cumulative opening area of all micro-shutters at full keypress engagement. This frequency sits deliberately between the instrument's lowest and highest bell fundamentals — the harmonic median of the full array. Every bell in the assembly therefore sits in a sympathetic relationship with the cavity's own resonant pitch. The cavity is never alien to any bell it receives. This is why the [[Crystalline Memory of Motion]] establishes itself within four bars rather than requiring extended warming: the [[Sky Glass]] is not learning a new frequency each time. It is returning to the one it was built to hold.

When a bell is struck inside the cavity, the resulting sound wave travels between the eight angled panels in a precise geometric path calculated by [[Daedalus]] through years of experimentation. Rather than dissipating as air disturbance the way any ordinary enclosed chamber would, the [[Sky Glass]] panels trap the wave's motion inside their crystalline lattice for a brief but measurable duration — long enough for the acoustic pressure to accumulate in the cavity wall rather than escape outward all at once. The panels then release this stored pressure gradually and uniformly, sustaining the vibration well past the moment of the original strike.

The practical consequence is that the cavity remembers the last struck note across several beats. When the percussion piston fires next, it does not need to overcome the full acoustic resistance of a silent chamber. It is striking into a cavity already vibrating sympathetically at the bell's natural frequency. Each successive strike costs less mechanical force to produce the same volume. The stored vibration acts as a flywheel of sound: once set turning by the first honest strike, it keeps turning with each small push that follows. A trained player learns to feel its hum through the grip and time their piston releases accordingly, not fighting the silence, but joining a rhythm already moving.

This is why the percussion rail requires no cog mechanism to sustain its tempo. The player sets the first beat. The [[Sky Glass]] cavity sustains the [[Resonance]] between beats. The piston only needs to reinforce the vibration at the right moment rather than restart it from nothing. The tempo wheel at the lower grip pre-sets the tension of the piston cords; the [[Sky Glass]] flywheel does the rest.

[[Daedalus]] named this principle the **[[Crystalline Memory of Motion]]**, and considered it one of the most significant discoveries of his life — that [[Sky Glass]] does not merely show the world what it is, but remembers what it was. This was the same principle that later [[Luminaire]] came to perfect during her life making [[Resonance Box]]es reusable as the effective recording device of the pre digitalization [[Arcanoria]].

### Structure of the Instrument

#### Staff's Body

The staff is forged from [[Aetherlight]]-tempered gold alloy which is harder than bronze, lighter than iron, warm to the touch, and resistant to vibrational fatigue across sustained play. The shaft is hollow throughout, with the resonance cavity occupying the inner channel from the base of the bell section down to approximately mid-staff. The lower half is solid-walled for structural rigidity when the instrument is carried as a walking staff or braced in combat.

The exterior carries three operative features:

- A **deployment collar** at the junction of bell section and lower staff — a rotating ring that, when turned a quarter-rotation, releases the bell arcs from their dormant lock and allows the spring-tension arms to extend.

- A **key strip** of accordion-style lever keys along the inner face of the upper grip, spanning approximately twenty centimeters, accessible to the fingers of the forward hand. Each key connects via internal gold-wire tension cord through the pulley channels to the corresponding bell's clapper arm and micro-shutter simultaneously. Each key returns under the tension of a small coiled spring of the same gold alloy — chosen for consistent return force across temperature change, since bronze springs harden and slow in cold field conditions. The return spring also functions as the micro-shutter's closing mechanism: the moment finger pressure releases the key, spring tension simultaneously withdraws the clapper and seals the bell mouth, producing the instrument's characteristic clean cutoff on every released note.

- A **tempo grip** at the lower section — a textured ring that can be squeezed to manually fire the percussion rail, or pre-rotated before play to set the cord tension that, once the [[Sky Glass]] flywheel is established, sustains the rhythmic pattern with minimal ongoing hand engagement. It requires several notes of the auric bells as "warm-up" on the key strip before it can become fully functional.

#### The Bell Array

The bells are cast from a gold-[[Sky Glass]] alloy in three wall-thickness grades, each producing a distinct tonal character:

- **Thin-walled:** Bright, projecting, and immediate, the soprano bells occupying the upper register. Because small bells have upper partials clustered closer together, the soprano bells carry more of their energy in the higher partials, producing the crystalline brightness the instrument's upper voice is known for.

- **Medium-walled:** Warm and carrying, the mezzo bells holding the instrument's central voice.

- **Thick-walled:** Deep and resonant, with a tone that lingers — the bass bells that anchor the lower register. At sufficient size, the largest bass bells produce a sub-audible partial at their gravest mode, a pressure wave below the threshold of conscious hearing that registers in the body as a vibration of the sternum and the soles of the feet before it is consciously heard.

Each bell hangs from an articulated bronze arm mounted to the inner frame of its arc. The bell's mouth faces outward. Its interior holds a clapper arm as a hinged lever connected by gold cord to its corresponding key. When a key is pressed, the cord pulls the clapper against the bell's interior at the **sound bow**: the thickened equatorial band approximately one-fifth of the bell's height above the rim, whose mass geometry [[Daedalus]] determined through geometric experiment to position the clapper precisely at the antinode of the prime partial, producing the cleanest fundamental with the least clang. 

The key simultaneously releases a micro-shutter — a small hinged plate of the same alloy covering the bell mouth in dormant mode, protecting it against wind-ring, moisture, and resonant contamination from nearby magic. Bell opens, clapper strikes, bell closes: all in one fluid motion driven by a single key press.

The bells deploy in two articulated wing-arcs extending from the upper section of the staff. In **Dormant** mode, both arcs fold flat against the shaft behind protective shell plates, locked by the deployment collar. In **Active** mode, the collar's quarter-rotation releases the spring-tension arms and both arcs extend outward and slightly upward — like a plant opening to light, or a standard unfurling before a line of march. This is the motion that gave the instrument its field name among [[Auric Order]] soldiers as the "Blossoming Chime."

#### The Percussion Rail

Along the outer edge of each bell arc runs a lateral rail carrying seven pistons, one per natural note (C through B). Each piston is a weighted hammer head on a short lever arm, held at tension by a braided gut cord running to the tempo grip. When the player sets and releases the tempo grip, it fires the active pistons in sequence against the outer bell walls, the sides, not the mouths, producing a complementary percussive overtone distinct from the clean fundamental of the clapper strike. This layering of interior clapper and exterior piston is the instrument's primary means of producing simultaneous melody and rhythm from a single hand.

Each piston head is aligned not to a generic point on the outer wall, but to the **second antinode ring** of the bell's exterior surface — the region [[Daedalus]] identified as producing the bell's most prominent upper partial when struck externally. A small raised guide ring is cast into each bell's exterior at this latitude, invisible to the eye but felt by the piston head's contact face, ensuring that no matter how many times the percussion rail fires, it strikes the same resonant point on every bell, every time.

Individual pistons can be pinned out of the firing sequence before play, allowing the player to compose which of the seven natural notes receive rhythmic percussion and which carry only melodic voice. Once the [[Sky Glass]] flywheel is established and the pattern set, the percussion continues with minimal manual input, freeing the forward hand entirely for the key strip and granting mobility.

#### States

The Auric Chime Staff operates in two defined states, Active and Dormant.

- **Dormant or Sleeping Bud:** All bell arcs fold flat and lock behind the shell plates. Micro-shutters are sealed on every bell. The percussion rail retracts flush to the arcs. The [[Sky Glass]] panels inside are in passive mode, they hold no accumulated vibration and produce no amplification. The staff is fully silent and functions as a walking staff or combat brace.

- **Active or Blossoming Chime:** A quarter-rotation of the deployment collar releases the arc arms. The bell arrays extend into their playing position. Micro-shutters become key-responsive. The percussion rail extends to its operational length. After approximately four sustained bars of continuous play, the [[Sky Glass]] cavity reaches its saturation point, the flywheel is established, and the staff begins to emit a faint, audible hum at the player's [[Fundamental Frequency]]. From this point, the instrument is no longer merely being played. It is resonating. The transition from Dormant to Active mid-march takes under a single breath: the collar turns, the arcs bloom, the first key is pressed.

### Variants of the [[Auric Bells]]

The [[Auric Bells]] family exists in three forms, each corresponding to a distinct range of acoustics and its functional part in the ensemble:

#### The [[Auric Aria Bell]] — The Upper Type
_The violin of the family. Light, fast, and ruthlessly melodic._

- **Length:** 70–90cm
- **Bell count:** 5–7 bells, single arc, soprano register only
- **Range:** Upper two octaves exclusively, every note expressed with crystalline brightness and immediate projection. Because the soprano bells have upper partials clustered closer together, the Aria Bell concentrates the majority of its acoustic energy above the fundamental — the brightness is not merely a quality of the material but a consequence of scale.

- **Percussion rail:** Feather-light piston heads producing accent overtones rather than driving rhythm; the [[Sky Glass]] flywheel is shorter-lived but faster to re-establish between phrases. The small cavity volume loses pressure quickly and repressurizes quickly — brevity is native to its nature.

- **Mobility:** Fully mobile. The only variant of the three that can be played comfortably while in active movement, and the only one that approaches single-handed use in practiced hands.

- **Magical role for [[Spellweaver]]s:** Precision spellwork and melodic [[Spellweaving]]; the narrow register concentrates the [[Fundamental Frequency]] carrier into a fine, directed output. Favored by [[Luminance]] and [[Cindergale]] users.

The [[Auric Aria Bell]] cannot anchor a formation, cannot lay down harmonic foundation, and makes no attempt to. It does not need to. In the right hands it cuts through any ensemble with a clarity nothing else in the family can match.

#### The [[Auric Bell Staff]] — The Standard Type
_The cello of the family, and [[Daedalus]]'s original design. One of the most flexible instruments in [[Arcanoria]]._

- **Length:** 120–150cm
- **Bell count:** 9–13 bells per arc, dual arcs — a soprano arc and a bass arc — giving it the complete natural range across both registers simultaneously
- **Range:** Almost unrestricted. Neither pinched in the upper register like the [[Auric Aria Bell]] nor limited to the low like the [[Auric Resonance Column]]. It can carry melody, harmony, and bass foundation concurrently across both arcs in the hands of a trained player. The dual-arc construction means the instrument contains two acoustic systems at different scales sharing a single resonance cavity. The cavity's Helmholtz frequency sitting at the harmonic median means it is simultaneously a subharmonic resonator for the soprano bells and a harmonic resonator for the bass bells, an octave relationship that reinforces rather than conflicts.

- **Key interface:** Full accordion strip; the forward hand manages melody and harmony keys while the lower hand holds the tempo grip.

- **Percussion rail:** Full 7-note piston rail on both arcs, independently pinnable per note; at full [[Sky Glass]] saturation, the flywheel sustains rhythmic patterns across both arcs without continuous manual input, freeing both hands for expressive play.

- **Mobility:** Walkable and even allows running with effort and practice if deployed. The deployment collar allows the bearer to fold to Dormant mid-march and reopen at destination. The balance of the staff is engineered so the lower grip functions as a walking brace without disturbing the dormant bell assembly. Running is possible on the dormant state but demands practiced balance. It is not designed for mounted use.

- **Magical role for [[Spellweaver]]s:** The [[Sky Glass]] cavity of the Standard type houses a compressed [[Sky Glass]] [[Crystal]] [[Resonance]] node at the acoustic center as the point where all eight panel reflections converge. This node accumulates harmonic energy across extended play. When discharged deliberately through a held chord, it releases a resonant pulse whose magical character is shaped by the [[Spellweaver]]'s [[Fundamental Frequency]] at the moment of release. Because the Standard type spans the complete range, its accumulated harmonics carry the richest interference pattern of any variant, the most expressive output, and the most unpredictable in untrained hands. Favored by [[Resonance]], [[Flux]], and [[Strand]] users.

#### The [[Auric Resonance Column]] — The Bass Type
_The siege artillery of music. It does not move. It does not need to._

- **Length:** 190–220cm
- **Bell count:** 6–10 bells, single deep arc; each bell is significantly larger in diameter than those of any other variant. The largest bass bells approach the width of a military shield boss.
- **Range:** The low two octaves exclusively. At full volume, the sound does not merely carry: it is felt in the chest and ground before it is consciously heard. The largest bass bells produce a sub-audible partial at their gravest mode — a pressure wave below the threshold of conscious hearing that registers in the sternum and the soles of the feet. [[Daedalus]] did not design this. He discovered it when the first [[Auric Resonance Column]] prototype was tested in the stone halls of [[Hollowmarch]] and every craftsman in the room felt their ribs hum simultaneously before a sound was consciously registered. He noted it as a property of scale, not of craft, the inevitable consequence of a bell large enough that its fundamental approaches the physical [[Resonance]] of the human torso.

- **Key interface:** Heavy-resistance lever keys along the lower grip, operated by the palm rather than the fingers; the mechanical force required to pull the clapper arm on a bass bell is substantial, and the player must brace the staff base against the ground or a fixed structure for sustained play.

- **Percussion rail:** Slow, weighted mallet pistons — the [[Sky Glass]] flywheel here operates on the longest cycle of the three variants; once established, the bass percussion continues with deep, measured strokes for significantly longer between manual reinforcement than either other type.

- **Mobility:** None in Active mode. In Dormant mode, the [[Auric Resonance Column]] is carried by two bearers on a wooden pole-frame threaded through rings along the staff body, transported like a standard or a siege piece. It is planted, braced, and played from a fixed position.

- **Magical role for [[Spellweaver]]s:** The most powerful anchor of the three variants. Because [[Sky Glass]] carries [[Sympathetic Vibration]] with the [[Leylines]], an active [[Auric Resonance Column]] at full [[Sky Glass]] saturation acts as a [[Resonance Stabilizer]] within the radius of its sound. This is why most of them are found alongside Pipe Organs in [[Hollowmarch]] wards and it helps to cross low [[Coherence]] zones. Allied [[Spellweaver]]s who hear it and share [[Sympathetic Vibration]] alignment with the player's [[Soul-Key]] find their [[Fundamental Frequency]] briefly stabilized, reducing the [[Signal Loss]] and [[Discordant Interference]] on their active [[Spellweaving]]. In non-magical terms, it is simply the most overwhelming acoustic presence a [[Hollowmarch]] ensemble can place on a field. Favored by [[Void]] and [[Crystal]] users.

When an [[Auric Resonance Column]] plays, every other instrument within earshot finds its own pitch more easily. It establishes the ground the rest of the ensemble stands on.

#### The [[Key of Attunement]] of the [[Auric Bells]]

Any trained musician can play an [[Auric Bell Staff]] without magic. The keys, pistons, and clapper system are pure mechanical instruments producing genuine acoustic sound. There is no cost to playing one. A violin does not ask for the soul of the one who holds it, and neither does this.

For a [[Spellweaver]], however, the instrument can be attuned as the [[Resonance]] Box records their [[Soul-Key]] through a session of sustained, uninterrupted play lasting approximately one full day, etching the frequency pattern into the [[Sky Glass]] panels through their inherent [[Sympathetic Vibration]] with [[Aetherlight]]. 

After attunement, the instrument responds to that [[Spellweaver]]'s active magic with dramatically amplified output. The [[Crystal]] bells' stored harmonics phase-lock with the attuned [[Fundamental Frequency]], turning ordinary acoustic notes into direct carriers of the wielder's [[Fundamental Frequency]].

Attunement is permanent but overwritable. A new [[Spellweaver]] can re-attune by playing through the same process. The previous frequency does not disappear at once. The first several sessions after re-attunement are acoustically haunted by the prior owner's ghost frequency fading slowly from the [[Sky Glass]] — faint harmonic echoes of the former wielder rising beneath every note the new one plays, like a voice heard through a wall, present but no longer intelligible.

Within the [[Auric Order]], this overlap is not considered a defect. It is treated as a sacred form of memory — the instrument carrying forward the voice of every [[Spellweaver]] who attuned to it, however briefly, before passing it on. An old [[Auric Bell Staff]] that has moved through many hands is considered more valuable, not less and its inheritance it's of deep ceremonial and theological value. Its [[Sky Glass]] is said to hold a choir of the faithful as a warmth and depth that no newly forged instrument can replicate regardless of its technical perfection.

[[Daedalus]] considered it a flaw he never solved. He designed the [[Resonance Box]] to record one [[Fundamental Frequency]] with absolute fidelity. The ghost harmonics of prior wielders were, to him, acoustic contamination — evidence that the [[Sky Glass]] was remembering more than it had been instructed to.

He spent many years attempting to design a re-attunement process that would fully overwrite the previous frequency without residue. He never succeeded. The [[Sky Glass]] held what it held. Whether this was a failure of his geometry or an intrinsic property of a material born from a god's sacrifice, he could not determine — and the uncertainty, for a man whose entire instrument was built on [[Absolute Certainty]], was the one imperfection he could not build around. 

[[Luminaire]] eventually found a way to fix this when she understood the fractal properties of [[Sky Glass]] but given the ceremonial significance and theological value it brought to [[Hollowmarch]] she decided to keep the original design intact.
