_Power should be concentrated in the hands of a few highly potent [[Spellweaver]] soloists, their individual performance harnesses the entire responsibility of magic._

Axis of [[Regalia Pillar]] vs [[Waltz Pillar]]. Part of the [[Pillars]] of [[Civilization]].

They argue in favor of having the harmony directed by a few virtuous soloists over the cacophony of an orchestra that can't achieve the same masterful performance.

The philosophical stance contrary to the [[Waltz Pillar]] on the distribution of magic and power. The [[Regalia Pillar]] argues that the best and most secure way to control the volatility of magic lies in having a highly trained [[Spellweaver]] that can conduct the entire symphony by their immediate will and individual skill in altering reality.

Major Arguments vs [[Waltz Pillar]]:

- Giving unrestricted access to the uneducated masses is a ticking time bomb that will use [[Spellweaving]] to blow up the city. _"If everyone has access to the nuclear codes, [[Humanity]] will last less than a day."_

- Having the means to act immediately and respond without requiring a consensus on everything makes a society more concise to act on a crisis. _"Should we all sit holding hands chanting about feelings while the horrors ravage and scream at the front door?"_

- A solo will be individually stronger when the [[Legend]] conducting the entire symphony than that same individual playing as one more note in an orchestra, it increases the maximum cap of what a single [[Spellweaver]] can achieve.
