_Power is found in the indomitable spirit of [[Humanity]], in our creator the [[Auric Aria]], and in the fate that [[Humanity]] carves out._

Axis of [[Aureus Pillar]] vs [[Chorus Pillar]]. Part of the [[Pillars]] of [[Civilization]].

They argue in favor of worshipping the [[Auric Aria]] over any [[Outer God]]s.

The philosophical stance contrary to the [[Chorus Pillar]] on the source of magic and power. The [[Aureus Pillar]] prefers the [[Auric Order]], the nature of [[Humanity]], and its ability to carve out its destiny by itself, it zealously believes in the capacity of the indomitable human spirit and its creator to define [[Mastery Over Chaos]] from anthropocentrism instead of submitting to the horrors of [[The Infinite Void]].
