The [[Pillars]] of [[Civilization]] are the spectrum of philosophical integration and nature of power and magic in [[Arcanoria]]. All [[Civilization]]s embody a position within these pillars that determines their relative standing, it mirrors the real left vs right and authoritarian vs liberty axis of the real life political spectrum but it places the question on the nature of power and music, its distribution, and its very origin.

Axis 1: _"Is power found in us, in the spirit of [[Humanity]] and its creator, or in nature, and the very unknown distance of the great beyond?"_

This dictates the relationship to the divine, and how existence finds symbiosis with nature at the cost of safety, unpredictability, and active predation.

[[Aureus Pillar]]: It is found in [[Humanity]] and its indomitable spirit to bend with [[Mastery Over Chaos]] its own fate. Believing in salvation beyond the creator of [[Humanity]], the [[Auric Aria]] is equal parts suicidal and misguidedly naive.
_To hear inward is to be confident in the composition of [[Humanity]]._

[[Chorus Pillar]]: It is found in the reverence of nature and the unknown. [[The Infinite Void]] contains many more secrets and latent potency in the esoteric [[Outer God]]s that are much more intriguing, alien, and awestricken than the [[Auric Aria]] or [[Humanity]].
_To hear outward is to be inspired by the impossible beauty of the beyond._

Axis 2: _"Should this power belong in the virtuous solo of a few or in the resonant orchestra of the voices of the many?"_

This dictates how many [[Spellweaver]]s exist in the population and how many people trigger a [[Motif Awakening]]. Less [[Spellweaver]]s means that the ones that exist have much more powerful because the total raw magical power is divided by less.

[[Regalia Pillar]]: It belongs in the power of a few very potent [[Spellweaver]]s at the top of the hierarchy of [[Civilization]] that dictate the fate on the course of [[Legend]]s and [[Great Spellweaver]]s to direct [[Humanity]]. It is the individual performance that should create very powerful [[Spellweaver]]s at the cost that not everyone is a [[Spellweaver]].
_The best part of a song is the masterful solo amidst the performance._

[[Waltz Pillar]]: It belongs in the power of the many, where every human should have democratized access to [[Spellweaving]], focusing on the voice of justice and under the veil of collaboration to channel potency through a [[Civilization]] of communal identity where all of [[Humanity]] has equal access to magic.
_The best part of a song is when all the instruments in the orchestra resonate together at once._

The axis functions to determine directly government types, alignment, religion, societal hierarchies, and priorities. All [[Civilization]]s differ based on their relative standing, and the extremes can become [[Fanatical]]. They are tiered in 7 categories from 0 for centrism, and 1 to 3 for being leaning, the whole standings are Centrist, Leaning, Pure Pillar, and [[Fanatical]], for example:

- Centrist: 0
- [[Waltz Pillar]] Leaning: +1
- [[Waltz Pillar]]: +2
- [[Fanatical]] [[Waltz Pillar]]: +3

_[[Fanatical]] is only available after realizing [[The Truth of Arcanoria]]._

This interplay allows for many combinations from Emperor of Mankind god emperors being a [[Fanatical]] Radical combination of pure [[Aureus Pillar]] with [[Regalia Pillar]] to [[Outer God]] absolute death cults of pure [[Waltz Pillar]] with [[Chorus Pillar]] from anything in between. Regular fantasy governments tend to fall in the centrist quadrants leaning slightly on one side or another.

[[Ages]] tend to have a favored type of the [[Pillars]] depending on its theme, and [[World Event]]s on opposed forces tend to be the ideological struggle of the [[Pillars]] axis. For example, during [[The Hollowing]] the [[Age of the False Messiah]] and the [[Purest of Love]] is a [[Chorus Pillar]] aligned age while the very same [[Age of Legends]] is an [[Aureus Pillar]] aligned as it relies on the resilience of [[Legend]]s defending the [[Auric Order]].

The climax of this struggle occurs in the early game during [[Ages]] V where several storylines converge in the crusades primarily between those ruled by the [[Age of the Sacred Cross]], [[Aureus Pillar]] vs the [[Age of Covens]] ruled by the [[Chorus Pillar]]. The back and forth creates a complex political climate that is embodied by the very [[Legend]]s directing its ability to exist arguing for the very nature and essence of magic, power, [[Spellweaving]], hierarchy, and how [[Civilization]] should be built.

The [[Purest of Love]] is an example of a [[Fanatical]] [[Waltz Pillar]] + [[Chorus Pillar]]. The Auric Orthodoxy is pure [[Aureus Pillar]] and centrist in [[Waltz Pillar]] and [[Regalia Pillar]]. Variations of magocracies are a powerful [[Regalia Pillar]] that can vary in the axis of [[Chorus Pillar]] if it's a dark coven with one [[Great Spellweaver]] master or an empire of will and conquest if it's [[Aureus Pillar]]. Both axis are fundamental.
