_"If the [[Pillars]] of [[Civilization]] are what builds its tenets, [[Civic]] are what makes them real through practices, tradition, culture, and everyday life. A [[Civilization]] is the song it chooses to sing."_

These are the unit blocks that form the identity and [[Resonance]] of any [[Civilization]]. It encodes activities, laws, culture, rituals, and practices that codify and integrate the [[Arcanoria]]'s deep magical laws with the governance of its empires. Thus [[Civic]]s are very versatile and evolve to reflect the progression of [[Humanity]] through the [[Ages]] and their evolving relationship with magic, crisis, and power.

[[Civic]] occupy too the spectrum of [[Consonance]] and [[Dissonance]], which can range between idyllic democratic practices to horrific military tactics. They are divided into the same categories as [[Enclave]]s:

- Agromagical [[Civic]]: Growth, Health, Sanitation, Sustenance.
- Militant [[Civic]]: Tactics, Weapons, Mercenaries, Hunters.
- Auric [[Civic]]: [[Aureus Pillar]], [[Piety]], [[Faith]], Knowledge, Science.
- Weaver [[Civic]]: [[Waltz Pillar]], Culture, Poetry, Songs, Communal Arts.
- Domestication [[Civic]]: Creatures, [[Pure Light]], Taming, Druids.
- Trading [[Civic]]: Economics, Wealth, [[Resources]], Efficiency. 
- Industrious [[Civic]]: Equipment, Forges, Artisans, Metalworks.
- Regal [[Civic]]: [[Regalia Pillar]], Politics, Diplomacy, Prestige, Espionage.
- Indulgent [[Civic]]: Entertainment, Pleasure, Joy, Luxuries,
- Esoteric [[Civic]]: [[Chorus Pillar]], [[Outer God]]s, Dark [[Magic Arts]], Witchcraft, Spirits.

### Tier 1 [[Ages]] [[Civic]]s ([[Ages]] 0 - III)

#### Agromagical, Indulgent, Weaver & Domestication [[Civic]]s

**Culinary Alchemists | Agromagical [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] I-III | [[Consonance]]**

Practical magical cooking using locally harvested ingredients to create temporary spell enhancements. All the recipes are shared communally, and each village maintains a Flavor Log recording which ingredient combinations produce stable [[Resonance]]. Chefs are considered essential laborers.

[[Ballad]]s:
- In a starving settlement, an untalented chef finds an ancient recipe capable of stabilizing the entire village's psychological [[Composure]]. Because the required rare ingredient is deep within a deadly [[Vibrational Fallout]] zone, the chef must convince a traumatized, half-broken [[Spellweaver]] to escort them, turning foraging into a desperate fight for survival.

**Feast of Abundance | Indulgent [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] I-III | [[Consonance]]**

Surplus goods and [[Auric Peach]]es are thrown from towers into public squares in a mandatory act of redistribution. Wealth held past the festival date is taxed as hoarding. [[Legend]]s in power who give the most during the feast earn higher social legitimacy, and some communities maintain records of each feast's yields and distribution, and farmers are honored as [[Civic]] [[Pillars]] equal to [[Spellweaver]]s.

[[Ballad]]s:
- A manipulative aristocrat attempts a bloodless coup by hiding vast wealth in magical [[Void]]-pockets to evade the hoarding tax, planning a massive distribution to instantly buy the public's loyalty. An undercover Justiciar must expose this economic sabotage before the feast crowns a tyrant.

**[[Ballad]] & Fantasy Plays | Weaver [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] II–IV | [[Consonance]]**

The formal organization of writers, singers, poets, and composers into state-sanctioned guilds designed to create culture, new stories, and preserve the [[Ballad]]s of [[Legend]]s. The classical civilization of [[Iridia]] is noted as a major patron of this [[Civic]], officially sponsoring writers of fiction and fairytales to weave them into the empire's culture.

[[Ballad]]s:
- A state-sponsored playwright uncovers a "[[Scorching Truth]]"— on how the [[Civilization]]'s founding hero was actually a cowardly deserter that became an [[Atonalis]]. Knowing the [[Auric Order]] executes those holding forbidden knowledge, the playwright encodes the truth into a propagandist play, sparking a psychological thriller of censorship and treason under the king's nose in a play akin to Shakespeare's Hamlet.

**Ritual [[Whistling Fans]] Dancers | Weaver [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] II–VI | [[Consonance]]**

Cultural and martial ceremonies inspired by [[Sephira]] (the [[Mythic Virtuoso]] of [[Cindergale]]), which utilize [[Whistling Fans]] as a formal patronage to the arts of [[Dance]] and martial combat.

[[Ballad]]s:
- During a grand martial ceremony, a young dancer realizes their rival's [[Whistling Fans]] are tuned to a [[Dissonance]] designed to shatter their internal [[Coherence]] and trigger a panic attack that interrupts the ceremony to crown them de jure heir. The dancer must instantly adapt their choreography to survive a deadly acoustic battle disguised as a synchronized duet.

**Moonlit Vigil | Weaver [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] 0-III | [[Consonance]]**

Courtship rituals in [[Glimmerfern]] groves during a full [[Moon]]. In silence people meet a matching frequency. Practiced by [[Waltz Pillar]], [[Chorus Pillar]], peasants and refugees. It is a _The folk tradition of the broken world._ in the last pockets that dim with hope to create love. 

[[Ballad]]s:
- A noble [[Spellweaver]] sneaks into a peasant Vigil and falls deeply in love by phase-locking in perfect [[Sympathetic Vibration]] with a commoner [[Spellweaver]]. When their family demands a loveless political marriage, the couple must choose whether to sever their true magical connection or ignite a civil war.

**The Ball Fluff Runs | Domestication [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] I-VI | [[Consonance]]**

Periodic runs through city streets pursued by specially bred [[Pure Light]] creatures that are giant fluff sticky balls, whomever reaches the finish line without being touched by one gets to win the produce of these domesticated creatures.

**Horned Flag Stampedes | Domestication [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] II-VI | [[Cusp]]

Runs inspired by the Ball Fluff Runs but using bull-like [[Pure Light]] creatures which have [[Crystal]]-tipped horns. During these runs, regular people can get injured, and there are several flags left in the arena, the survivors who earn the most flags while still standing in the arena of the bulls gets to have a feast with the cooked [[Pure Light]] creatures afterward.

[[Ballad]]s:
- A disgraced knight enters the deadly Horned Flag Stampede to win a feast for starving orphans. A conspirator in the crowd casts a polyphonic spell to frenzy the beasts, turning the game into a massacre. The knight drops their weapons, using only wits and evasion to protect the other runners.

#### Regal, Militant & Esoteric [[Civic]]s

**Truth [[Soul Leitmotif]] Testimonies | Regal [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] I Onwards | [[Consonance]]

A legal and social practice that requires individuals to use the dormant state of their [[Soul Leitmotif]] when signing social contracts. Because the [[Soul Leitmotif]] is a physical crystallization of one's [[Consciousness]] and emotional truth, this [[Civic]] effectively uses the bare soul as a flawless lie detector to build social harmony and trust.

[[Ballad]]s:
- An infamous spy infiltrates a rival empire to sign a doomed peace treaty, using [[Illusory Magic]] to project a false aura of Pristine [[Composure]] over their corrupted soul while framing the legitimate ruler of lying by flickering conflict to their [[Soul Leitmotif]]. A low-ranking clerk spots a microscopic flicker of [[Dissonance]] and must prove the deception before war erupts.

**Harmonic Quorum | Regal [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] I-III | [[Consonance]]**

Major policy decisions require a [[Resonance]] as majority vote. The assembled leadership must attuned their internal [[Coherence]] and [[Soul Leitmotif]]s onto a single frequency. If the leaders cannot achieve [[Sympathetic Vibration]] due to doubt or conflict, the policy cannot pass.

[[Ballad]]s:
- The council has one hour to pass a defensive edict against an encroaching demon swarm, but the Quorum fails because one hidden member is secretly terrified and generating [[Discordant Interference]]. The protagonist must interrogate the council's minds to find the coward before the walls are breached.

**Bridal Conquest | Militant [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] 0–III | [[Cusp]]**

A political and martial practice involving the capture of enemy rulers or high-ranking knights of the opposite sex to marry them into their [[Civilization]]. This [[Civic]] provides a the Bridal casus belli. Allowing [[Civilization]] to capture opposing enemies and instead of executing them, the ritual of courtship is meant to systematically create and secure strong lineages of magical heirs that unify cultures.

[[Ballad]]s:
- An undefeated warlord is forced into marriage with the empress who decimated their homeland. Over years, they develop genuine love. Just as they admit their [[Resonance]], the warlord's former generals breach the capital to assassinate the empress, forcing the warlord to turn against their own people to protect their captor.

**Hunting Parties of [[Legend]]s | Militant [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] II–IV | [[Consonance]]**

Coordinated hunting parties of great renown of top [[Spellweaver]]s in groups of 4 to 7, each assigned a specific role, to track and kill high-ranking [[Atonalis]] and [[Pure Light]] Behemoths. Successful hunts forge permanent synergy bonds, prestige, and yield pristine rare materials, pelts, and [[Rose Seed]]s.

[[Ballad]]s:
- Tracking an [[Ascendant]] [[Atonalis]] into a collapsed pass, a hunting party realizes the monster is perfectly anticipating them because it is the shattered [[Soul Leitmotif]] of their original mentor. Their fallen mentor uses the shared memories to turn the hunters against one another, and exploit their individual weaknesses.

**Duels of Severance | Militant [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] 0-III | [[Cusp]]**

When a child reaches the age of majority, they are required to duel their most direct guardian in a brutal, near‑death contest that ends only when one combatant is permanently marked. The wound is proof the duel is fought for honor, witnessed by the community, and won only when one opponent inflicts a decisive injury on the other. Killing is neither required nor forbidden, but the duel only ends through the submission of one of the fighters due to physical exhaustion. The ritual is proof that the chord of dependency has been severed so that both may now relate as equals.

[[Ballad]]s:
- A gifted [[Spellweaver]] must duel their chronically ill father. Inflicting a permanent wound might kill him, but refusing leaves them legally classified as a child, unable to inherit the magical legacy needed to save their estate. The duel becomes a heart-wrenching dance of pulled punches.

**[[Leylines]] Nomads | Esoteric [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] 0-III | [[Consonance]]**

Societies that move with the [[Grand Thread Rings]] instead of installing [[Resonance Anchors]] whenever [[Leylines]] move during [[Age Transition]]s, they follow the natural flow of [[Cosmic Motion]] to determine where to establish themselves. Similar to Air Nomads in Avatar.

**[[Sky Glass]] Burials | Esoteric [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] 0–III | [[Consonance]]**

Funerary and cultural ceremonies involving "sky burying" at the edges of high mountain peaks where [[Sky Glass]] forms naturally. These ceremonies are deeply connected to the tactile, infinite fractals of the glass, and are a reverence to the skies for allowing a sacred passage into the [[Auroral Ribbons]] and [[The Eternal Symphony]].

[[Ballad]]s:
- During a sacred [[Sky Glass]] Burial, the elder is terrified of death and begins spiraling into an [[Atonalis]] to at least have immortality in this state, before he can die the elder hatches into a violent [[Violux]] [[Atonalis]]. The unarmed mourners must use the howling wind and raw [[Resonance]] to push the demon off the cliff without destroying the sacred grounds.

**Chaos Reverence Sacrifices | Esoteric [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] 0-II | [[Dissonance]]**

Rituals of communal suicide and sacrifice to reverence the chaotic energy that emerges, these societies sacrifice and create suffering to pool [[Formless Masses]] that become revered as "divinity" when they emerge from a [[Dissonance]] Cocoon. This is one of the earliest ways to realize [[We Are Atonalis]], because the emerging [[Atonalis]] is what is being revered as a chaotic entity as divinity due to its anomalous properties made from the collective suffering these societies are ignorant to.

[[Ballad]]s:
- A runaway infiltrates a death cult about to pool enough trauma to birth a [[Fracted]] [[Atonalis]]. Armed only with a [[Resonance Box]], they must disrupt the chanting with a perfectly timed counter-frequency and crash the entire sacrifice ritual before the demon is allowed to hatch from the collective suffering.

Temporary magical costumes projected by [[Magical Relic]]s that allow participants to become someone else with shapeshifting [[Illusory Magic]]. Any citizen may wear the costume of a ruler, legend, or mythological figure during the [[Ritual Seventh]] of any [[Phase]]. Be it peasant as knight, widow as lost husband. The festival inverts social hierarchy for three days, releasing pressure through playful transformation.

[[Ballad]]s:
- A downtrodden tailor uses an illusion to assume the shape of the city’s ruthless [[Head of State]] to enjoy absolute luxury. The carnival of illusion turns lethal when elite assassins strike thinking he is the real king. Unable to drop the illusion without being executed by the state for treason, the tailor must outwit the killers while maintaining the cruel tyrant persona.

**Polyphonic Choral Singers | Weaver [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] IV-VI | [[Consonance]]**

An institution of organized state-managed choral singers of multiple overlayed voices using bells and other [[Magical Relic]]s to enhance the [[Coherence]] of regions. These choruses are versatile and can be used for a variety of applications from agromagical applications to recovery and rebuilding of societies.

[[Ballad]]s:
- A soprano in the royal choir realizes the state is using their complex sheet music to weave a massive Signal Override, erasing the fundamental frequencies of political dissidents. The singer must recruit choristers to secretly weave a counter-melody to shatter the mind-control without breaking harmony.

**Courting Grounds | Weaver [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] IV | [[Consonance]]**

Evolution of Moonlit Vigil where once per spring, [[Phase of Prelude]], unwed citizens process in a botanical sanctuary beneath the palace gardens. [[Spellweaver]]s in this tradition use their [[Soul Leitmotif]]s to pulse in unison as means to find a partner and conduct rituals of romance and falling in love. Hook: Two rival noble houses both send heirs to the Courts. The heirs resonate—but their families are locked in a blood feud. Romeo & Juliet [[Ballad]].

[[Ballad]]s:
- The heirs of two noble houses locked in a centuries-old blood feud pulse in absolute, flawless unison at the Courting Grounds. State law mandates they marry, forcing the two families into a deadly, covert race to assassinate the opposing heir before the wedding binds their houses. Romeo & Juliet.

Jerez Arena as an evolution of the colosseum [[Civic]]s but focused on slaves gaining freedom but with painful and humiliating dehumanizing punishments on loss. Some of the contracts are directly paired with [[Velvet Nectar]].

**[[Atonalis]] Bombs | Militant [[Civic]] | [[Ages]] V Onwards | [[Dissonance]]**

These already half-broken individuals are deliberately deployed into highly hazardous areas, such as [[Vibrational Fallout]] zones and the outer territories of wild magic, gambling on whether the [[Spellweaver]] will break toward an [[Atonalis]] demon or push through the trauma. _"They are already half-broken. The question is whether they break toward the [[Atonalis]] or through it."_

[[Ballad]]s:

Branded a traitor, she must ally with underground [[Void]]-weavers to destroy the weapon before the government drains the life force of every noble family sacrifice made in the empire.

- **The Narrative Hook:** A skeptical runaway infiltrates a death cult on the eve of their grand Chaos Reverence Sacrifice. The cult believes they are summoning a savior god through mass suffering, but the protagonist knows they are merely pooling enough trauma to birth an apocalyptic [[Ascendant]] [[Atonalis]]. Armed with only a Resonance Box, the protagonist must disrupt the communal chanting with a perfectly timed counter-frequency before the demon hatches.

**The Medieval Ages (Ages IV – VI)**

**The [[Ballad]] of the Poisoned Dough | Cooking Guilds (Agromagical Civic)**

- **The Lore:** The highly institutionalized evolution of the early Culinary Alchemists.
- **The Narrative Hook:** The grandmaster of the city’s most prestigious Cooking Guild discovers a conspiracy: a rival guild is lacing their bread with synthesized Euphoria Compounds to slowly hijack the nervous systems of the city guard. Stripped of their rank and discredited, the grandmaster must wage an underground culinary war, creating purifying, anti-toxin meals to break the addiction before the rival guild initiates a coup.

**The [[Ballad]] of the Painted King | Carnival of Shifting Reflections (Indulgent Civic)**

- **The Lore:** A three-day festival where citizens use Illusory Magic relics to wear temporary magical costumes, completely inverting social hierarchies.
- **The Narrative Hook:** A downtrodden, indebted tailor uses an Illusory relic during the Carnival to assume the shape of the city’s ruthless Head of State, intending to enjoy three days of absolute luxury. The prank turns lethal when a cabal of assassins strikes, believing the tailor is the real tyrant. Unable to drop the illusion without being executed by the state for treason, the tailor must outwit elite killers while maintaining the persona of the cruelest person in the empire.

**The [[Ballad]] of the Subversive Soprano | Polyphonic Choral Singers (Weaver Civic)**

- **The Lore:** State-managed choirs that overlay multiple voices using bells and relics to actively enhance regional Coherence.
- **The Narrative Hook:** A newly recruited soprano in the royal Polyphonic Choir realizes that the complex sheet music they are performing is slightly off. The state isn't using the choir to stabilize Coherence; they are weaving a massive, city-wide Signal Override to erase the fundamental frequencies of political dissidents. The singer must recruit other choristers to secretly weave a counter-melody of Cindergale and Strand into their performances to shatter the state's mind-control without breaking harmony.

**The [[Ballad]] of a Bleeding Unison Love | Courting Grounds (Weaver Civic)**

- **The Lore:** Unwed citizens process in botanical sanctuaries and use their Soul Leitmotifs to pulse in unison to find a partner. The lore specifically highlights a Romeo & Juliet hook.
- **The Narrative Hook:** Two rival noble houses, locked in a brutal, centuries-old blood feud, send their heirs to the Courting Grounds to find strategic political matches. To the horror of both families, the two heirs' Soul Leitmotifs pulse in absolute, flawless unison. The state law mandates that such a perfect Resonance must be honored with marriage, forcing the two warring families into a deadly, covert race to assassinate the opposing heir before the wedding binds their houses forever.

**The [[Ballad]] of the Velvet Ghost | Memory Markets (Trading Civic)**

- **The Lore:** Designated economic zones with exempted legal status where highly addictive Velvet Nectar—distilled from extracted memories and suffering—is actively traded.
- **The Narrative Hook:** A desperate smuggler in the Memory Markets steals a highly prized vial of Velvet Nectar to pay off a debt, unaware that the vial contains the extracted memories of a murdered Great Spellweaver. Upon accidentally consuming a drop, the smuggler inherits the dead mage's memories and magical mastery. Now hunted by the aristocratic connoisseurs of the Velvet Market who want the memories back, the smuggler must master their new, terrifying powers to survive the night.

**The [[Ballad]] of the Fleshy Mountain Pass | Legendary Hunting Charters (Regal Civic)**

- **The Lore:** Open, state-sponsored hunts for great beasts or Atonalis. The kill is distributed by a council and inscribed as a civic record, bringing immense prestige.
- **The Narrative Hook:** An aging, dishonored Legend takes up a near-suicidal Hunting Charter against a colossal Carnalix to clear their name and secure a civic record for their impoverished family. During the hunt, they are ambushed by a younger, arrogant aristocrat who plans to steal the kill. The two rivals are forced to work together when they realize the beast has trapped them both in a collapsed ravine, turning the hunt for glory into a gritty fight for survival.

**The [[Ballad]] of the Roaring Wards | Slayer's Amphitheater (Militant Civic)**

- **The Lore:** Public arenas where volunteers or prisoners fight captured Atonalis. The spectators' emotional resonance actively amplifies the slayer's magic.
- **The Narrative Hook:** A political prisoner is thrown into the Slayer's Amphitheater against an impossible Discant demon. As the fight drags on, the prisoner realizes they can manipulate the crowd's roaring emotions, turning the spectators' cheers into a massive wave of Flux magic. The prisoner decides not just to kill the demon, but to harness the amphitheater's collective Resonance to shatter the magical wards holding them captive and incite a bloody arena rebellion.

**The [[Ballad]] of the Exile's Sprint | Atonalis Bombs (Militant Civic)**

- **The Lore:** A brutal tactic where guilty Spellweavers are tortured until their consciousness shatters, then sent as Trojan horses into rival settlements to transform into destructive demons from within.
- **The Narrative Hook:** A devoted spellweaver is captured by an enemy Institute and subjected to unspeakable psychological torture to turn them into an Atonalis Bomb. Dumped in the center of their own hometown, they can feel their Soul Leitmotif cracking, mere hours away from hatching into a monstrous Primal Discordia. The spellweaver must use every ounce of their failing Crystal willpower to run far away from the people they love, racing against their own collapsing mind to find a wasteland where they can die without hurting anyone.

**The [[Ballad]] of the Broken Vanguard | Spiraling Conscription (Militant Civic)**

- **The Lore:** The state-sanctioned deployment of half-broken Spellweavers with critically low Composure into highly hazardous Vibrational Fallout zones, gambling on whether they will push through their trauma or shatter into an Atonalis.
- **The Narrative Hook:** A squad of four "Spiraling" conscripts—all suffering from extreme PTSD and hovering on the edge of demonic transformation—are dropped into a terrifying zone of wild magic. Instead of breaking, the squad forms an intense, desperate Echoing Bond. Together, they must navigate the fallout zone, using their shared trauma not to become monsters, but to trigger secondary Motif Awakenings and return to the capital powerful enough to overthrow the generals who sent them to die.

**Enlightenment and Reformation Ages (Ages VII – IX)**

**The [[Ballad]] of the Empty Hemovault | Bloodline Tithe (Regal/Militant Civic)**

- **The Lore:** A systemic tax where noble families must store their blood in a "Hemovault." This accumulated Essence Sacrifice serves as a massive military battery drawn upon during wartime.
- **The Narrative Hook:** The eldest daughter of a disgraced noble house prepares to offer her blood to the state's Hemovault, only to discover the vault is completely empty. A corrupt faction of the government has been secretly siphoning the nobility's generational Essence Sacrifice to power an illegal, apocalyptic superweapon. Branded a traitor for discovering the truth, the daughter must ally with an underground faction of Void-weavers to break into the capital and destroy the weapon before the government drains the life force of every noble family in the empire.
