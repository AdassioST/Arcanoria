_Power should be democratized in the hands of the many in a massive orchestra of [[Spellweaver]]s, their collective performance dictates equal access in responsibility.

Axis of [[Regalia Pillar]] vs [[Waltz Pillar]]. Part of the [[Pillars]] of [[Civilization]].

They argue in favor of having an resonant orchestra over the reliance on a few virtuous soloists who can miss a note and cause everything to fail.

The philosophical stance contrary to the [[Regalia Pillar]] on the distribution of magic and power. It argues in favor of the democratization of magic as a right that everyone should have, it follows the principles of justice, shared burdens, collectivism, and communal identity to be resilient against the crisis.

Major Arguments vs [[Regalia Pillar]]:

- Giving the entire burden of civilization to a single [[Spellweaver]] is at best a dictatorial tyranny and at worst genocides when they inevitably break under the stress of having the entire world on their shoulders. _"If we all depend on a single soloist what happens when that soloist becomes an [[Atonalis]]?"_

- Having the ability to distribute [[Essence Sacrifice]] across many will make a society much more resilient to crisis and the shared burdens help to recover faster when loss becomes inevitable. _"Will that soloist break and surrender all of their memories just to keep the beacon of [[Humanity]] afloat?"

- An orchestra has a much richer variation of sounds, voices, and sheer bandwidth than any [[Legend]] conducting the entire symphony. The real strength in numbers will always overpower a single mind, even if that mind is individually stronger, the collective is much more resilient and can depend in one another.
