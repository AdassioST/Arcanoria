#technology

A type of technology, similar to [[Mirrorbox Trap]], that precedes all of the invention of speakers and recorders, it is built from [[Sky Glass]] and was properly invented and regularized by the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Luminance]], [[Luminaire]].

It is the most basic type of [[Soul-Casing]], and has a clear indicator from [[Crystal]] pink when the [[Resonance Box]] is occupied with an existing vibration, and a hue of [[Resonance]] teal green when it is vacant for recording a new sound.

It is useful for all [[Resonance]] [[Spellweaver]]s, and is a necessary tool for all [[Regalia Pillar]] soloists and [[Dance]]rs to provide additional instruments like percussion, bass, or the music itself. They require mastery of [[Crystal]] and [[Resonance]] to use properly. It is available at the very beginning of [[Ages]] 0 but the technology evolves across the [[Ages]] as they become recording devices with magical properties with the introduction of the [[Law of Relics]]. The most significant advancement to this technology was made by [[Luminaire]] during her deep understanding of [[Sky Glass]] and its properties for applying noise cancellation to make [[Resonance Box]]es reusable instead of being a one-time use, one-time recorder device.
