Also known as the Auric Church is the institutional faith after the [[First Reset]] that establishes the [[Auric Aria]] as the deity of [[Humanity]], and creator of the [[Known Universe]].

In the classical orthodoxy, it also poses the [[Auric Aria]] as [[The One True God]], and is in direct contrast with the [[Chorus Pillar]] in the nature of [[Humanity]]'s spirit and the source of magical power, study of the esoteric, and understanding of divine will. The classical orthodoxy further pushes the narrative through the [[Auric Mythos of Creation]], and the concept of [[Perfect Reflection]] to justify the architecture of order and [[Mastery Over Chaos]] above all else. 

Everything has meaning and purpose within the [[Auric Aria]]'s divine plan: _"To suffer is to endure faithfully where suffering is the price of meaning."_

The [[Auric Order]] establishes theological virtues as tenets to faith that directly map the [[Auric Heptacode]]:

- [[Piety]]: Faith as fidelity to self and understanding of the divine through the lens of the individual, it is the theological virtue of [[Resonance]].

- Revelation: Perception of reality and seeking of truth as the divine mechanisms of the cosmos and of theodicy itself, it is the theological virtue of [[Luminance]]. _The universe is both intelligible and beautiful, understanding and feeling are the same act, to know a thing is to resonate with it._

- Contrition: Honest self-offering as means to alter reality, it is the theological virtue of [[Flux]].

- Sacrifice: The giving of self to moral and spiritual duty, to use the acceptance of maintenance in the meaning of struggle to weave meaning. it is the theological virtue of [[Void]].

- Contemplation: Undivided presence in the moment of truth, of act, of will, and duty, it is the theological virtue of [[Cindergale]].

- Hope: Confidence in the unseen and will of divine purpose, structure, and obligation through the convergence of the self as pinnacle of action, it is the theological virtue of [[Crystal]].

- Communion: Relationship as memory to create value in legacy and standing across time while harmonizing from [[Consonance]] of the self to others, it is the theological virtue of [[Strand]].

These theological virtues argue that the [[Triadic Virtues of Spellcraft]] comes from achieving an state of being. It is the interior life, faith, hope, love, contrition, contemplation, not solely as a private matter but also the very substance of order and power. To grow in virtue is to grow in magical potency. To fall into sin is to lose access to reality's fundamental forces and to invite chaos, evil, demons and [[Dissonance]]. The ultimate sins are known as the [[Seven Weights]], and invite to rigorous reflection of the divine geometry of the cosmos and the [[Piety]] in oneself to leave the burden of these and achieve transcendence through [[Lyrical Fragment]]s.

The interpretations of the [[Auric Order]] depend on the tenets of each individual religion practicing the adoration of the [[Auric Aria]]. However, most of them share the [[Auric Mythos of Creation]], and propose an unbearable nature of revelation. This is primarily due to the existence of [[Scorching Truth]]s which are theological crises as much as they are memetic hazards to order itself.


### The [[Ages]] of Reformation After the [[Second Reset]]

[[Lacrimosa]]'s apocalypse as [[The Hollowing]] is the first crack in the old system and, paradoxically, the first step toward the reformation of many [[Ages]] after by proving that collaboration through the [[Law of Relics]] is a better new covenant.

The reformation formally begins with the end of the [[Second Reset]] but only takes shape on [[Ages]] VIII onwards. With the wisdom that the [[New Testament]] was flawed it proved to the [[Auric Aria]] that ceding entirely from [[Humanity]] to respect their growth and authority ultimately lead again to the end of the [[Polychord Crisis]], which was the exact event that had ended the [[First Reset]] during her direct intervention as the Golden Sovereign. In her desperation, the [[Auric Aria]] conjured the last tetter of her own [[Consciousness]] to [[Civilization]] to explain the [[The Truth of Arcanoria]] before [[Arcanoria]] unraveled back to [[Primal White Noise]] in an attempt to change the future.

[[The Truth of Arcanoria]] allows for many things to change during the following civilization [[Cycle]]s, and it allows a vast reformation to the [[Auric Order]] after [[Civilization]] successfully navigates the [[Polychord Crisis]]. This happens particularly during [[Ages]] VIII, with extensive work in the [[Age of Auric Radiance]] and the [[Age of Enlightenment]], brings a new synthesis of a reformed [[Auric Order]] born from this necessity of order in chaos that is divorced from divine neglect.

Likewise, this also extends to the necessity of [[Scorching Truth]]s, in some variations of the [[Auric Order]] religions, particularly on those founded after the [[The Truth of Arcanoria]], they see the theological implication that theology is not an optional comfort and [[Scorching Truth]]s are sacred knowledge about the fundamental requirement of participation in existence. This event causes the great schism between the classical orthodoxy of the [[Auric Order]] on the nature of [[The Truth of Arcanoria]]: It becomes a debate of those who deny it as dangerous knowledge for the sake of security and order vs those who accept it as divine and sacred revelation to commune with the [[Auric Aria]].

In the latter framework, it is necessary to make a vow to face the revelation as sacred in [[Consonance]] about the creator, of oneself, and the nature of suffering to prevent losing the access to magic itself, and that order itself is a mandate from the very [[Weight of Flaw]] that crushed the creator reframing it as the sacred lesson: _"Cruelty and meaning are woven from the same thread. Only because the [[Auric Aria]] faltered we are given the depth of joy and suffering, of meaning in salvation. We can only know her love truly real because she's suffered with us and for us."_ 

The reformed variants of the [[Auric Order]] argue in favor that these revelations in fact prove even further the idea of [[Perfect Reflection]] because to feel doubt is to be in a sacred communion with the creator, reframing healing as collaboration, just as the [[Auric Aria]] herself did with [[Lacrimosa]] and the [[Law of Relics]] a divine mandate. Likewise, it proves that the [[Betrayal of the Moon]] to create them is a proof of her love for [[Humanity]] above all else, if there was no [[Moon]] there would be no [[Humanity]], and the fact she feels regret for [[Selenea]] embodies her genuine empathy: 

_"If the [[Auric Aria]]'s flaws are the true source of meaning, is faith in perfection a weakness?"_

_"The [[Auric Aria]] is unimaginably intelligent and creative, so much so that the very structure she made is immune to her own weaving without unravelling the very fabric of reality. If she had done a universe without the [[Seven Weights]], then it would be as sterile and dead as she hadn't created at all. And that's why even if she's not all-powerful in her precision; it the friction of existence what shapes purpose. She is doing her best in creating every layer that allows a [[Fragment of Meaning]], even if her best sometimes fails, this suffering is what allows all acts of love, devotion, courage, and sacrifice to surpass the [[Weight of Purpose]]. Thus, It is only through our hand and duty, in [[Consonance]] and [[Piety]], that establish the tenets of the relationship of [[Humanity]] and the Sovereign. And in which we will, together in communion, forge our own destiny amidst the sea of relentless, encroaching chaos by bathing in the light of the Brightest Star."_

Moreover, in [[Fanatical]] versions of the [[Auric Order]], and the [[Aureus Pillar]], they even recognize and embody the principle of communion in divine limitation as a core driver for action. These become the principle of [[Perfect Reflection]] to the upmost maximum, if it was in the [[Auric Aria]]'s will to subdue the chaos to her will, then it is a divine mandate for [[Humanity]] to subdue anything for control, even if it causes imperfect friction it is justified by [[Humanity]]'s spirit. This is the reason why the second and third [[Age of Humanity]] deal with such an anthropocentric view.
