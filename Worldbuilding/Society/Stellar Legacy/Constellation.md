_"Death is just the day of the publication of your [[Magnum Opus]]."_

A [[Legend]] that has ascended to the [[Stellar Legacy Score]]. They can form a variety of [[Stellar Covenant]]s. [[Outer God]]s and actual deities can't be [[Constellation]]s, they have to be [[Legend]]s exclusively.

All [[Constellation]]s live in a spectrum between [[Constellation]]s of [[Consonance]] and [[Constellation]]s of [[Dissonance]]. This alignment depends on whether they were "good" or "evil" during their lifetime as a [[Legend]]. The specific alignment depends on the type of [[Legend Opus]] and [[Legend Title]]s they have:

- [[Constellation]] of pure [[Consonance]]: [[Mira]] given that they were pretty selfless in their life, and did genuine good for those dying while she herself suffered the same condition. Her offer to [[Lacrimosa]] was also born from a place of [[Consonance]].
  
- [[Constellation]] of pure [[Dissonance]]: [[Syvanth]] which is the complete opposite as his [[Legend]] came to be born as the Killer of the Great Expanse and whose [[Legend]] is the ominous [[Ballad]] sung after his horrific beautification.
  
- Mixture of [[Consonance]] and [[Dissonance]]: [[Cadmus Tacet]] which is a complex moral character as he had great [[Legend Opus]] like the Scalpel of [[Humanity]]'s Hope, but also horrific [[Legend Title]]s due to the means he used to achieve said goals, like The Vivisector due to his brutal methods on mapping the [[Pure Light]] spectrum. Most [[Constellation]]s are placed in this bracket as a mixture of both.

Depending on the specific pairings on which [[Constellation]]s are receiving [[Constellation Worship]], they can unlock several effects, and the mixture between [[Consonance]] and [[Dissonance]] is a fertile ground for friction and unique [[Syncretism]]. Furthermore, [[Constellation]]s can change their affinity to either [[Consonance]] or [[Dissonance]] through [[Mythic Drift]] by adapting to the new ways on which they receive their specific [[Constellation Worship]].

Finally, [[Constellation]]s of great [[Constellation Renown]] and [[Constellation Worship]] can have the ability of [[Reincarnation]] due to having a strong tether between [[Arcanoria]] and [[The Eternal Symphony]]. This allows a character to be reborn in [[Ages]] different from the ones in their original mythos. They receive some bonus to their starting [[Legend Trait]]s based on their defining [[Constellation]], but are effectively "reset" of all their memories and previous titles. They are a blank canvas that shares the same character [[Soul-Key]] and name, considering both their original starting [[Legend Trait]]s and physical looks. They are back the moment before they began their original [[Legend]]. Moreover, some [[Dissonance]] [[Legend Trait]]s can be removed of their origin to accommodate to a new life. For example, removing "Orphan" from [[Mira]] since she has an actual family now.

[[Reincarnation]] is a mechanic destined to give the [[Civilization]] a chance to grow a stronger [[Constellation]] through [[Syncretism]] and give them a different "ending" with unique [[Legend Opus]] and different [[Legend Trait]]s. For example, if they worship [[Mira]], she can reincarnate during [[Ages]] XI as a magical girl during the [[Age of Starlight]] to give her a chance of a life without that much suffering as an orphan. Of course, whenever a [[Constellation]] is undergoing [[Reincarnation]], they are not available to the [[Stellar Legacy Score]] until they die again and become a [[Constellation]] with the new set of [[Legend Opus]] and [[Legend Title]]s. Their specific [[World Truth]]s however will be permanent independent of whether they are in [[Reincarnation]]. They just can't form [[Stellar Covenant]]s for the [[Ages]] where they experience being a mortal because they're temporarily not a [[Constellation]] anymore.

[[Reincarnation]] can only be triggered after the [[Constellation]] has experienced at least three [[Ages]] being a [[Constellation]]. Furthermore, the process itself is costly and requires a strong tether of [[Resonance]] between [[Arcanoria]] and [[The Eternal Symphony]]. Thus, [[Reincarnation]] can only happen during an [[Age Transition]], and its dependent on the slots [[Civilization]] has unlocked for [[Reincarnation]]. The default state is up to two concurrent [[Reincarnation]]s but after learning [[The Truth of Arcanoria]] the slots can be expanded and its cooldowns of three [[Ages]] can be reduced.
