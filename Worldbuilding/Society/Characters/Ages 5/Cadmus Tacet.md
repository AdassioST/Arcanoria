[[Cadmus Tacet]] is born as an [[Underdog]] and is one of the most important figures of [[Arcanoria]]. He is the very first person to coin the term [[Age of Humanity]] properly during [[Ages]] V and proposes that [[Humanity]] must forge its own future through the ruthless pursuit of progress. He is very devout to the [[Auric Aria]] and believes that truth and safety can only come from the scalpel of human reason and its creator.

The core philosophy of [[Cadmus Tacet]] cements during his childhood after being witness to the tyrannical oppression of draconic [[Pure Light]] beings who subjugated and enslaved the human populace on the region he was born. Treating human sacrifice as tribute and livestock.

The words of the [[Age of Humanity]] engraved in his mind by witnessing the execution of a man whose name he never knew yet would never forget his last words, vowing to make it a prophecy:

  - "Poor little, human. Too small to even serve a snack. Face it, you are weak, and you will always be the subject of the [[Pure Light]] rule forever while your kind cowers in the grass." - Demi Human Dragon executor.
  - "I swear, by my last breath, that one day... a reckoning will come. [[Humanity]] will outlast every single one of you, and your very essence shall become the fuel on which the sons of my sons will run. And then you will see, for that will be the [[Age of Humanity]]."
  - "Is that insolence your last words? Pathetic." The executor says as he impaled the neck of the man with a [[Crystal]] spear.

[[Cadmus Tacet]] is then driven in exile to escape the very same fate of this man, and makes a desperate search of anything that would make him question what do [[Pure Light]] beings have that [[Humanity]] does not. He awakens his [[Motif Awakening]] on the search of the truth beneath what could be the structure that makes [[Humanity]] outlast these very executors that treated [[Humanity]] as livestock and slaves.

He then goes on an expedition of several archeomagical sites and obscure [[Legend]]s to find the truth that would give [[Humanity]] an edge over the tyranny of [[Pure Light]]. On his search he finds the obscure work of [[Vals Jenner]], the first epidemiologist that was studying the remnants of the [[Great Plague]], and uses this knowledge to become the first means of developing his own techniques. He uses the knowledge on the vaccines that [[Vals Jenner]] developed while searching for further clues on how to even conduct surgery. This is his formal foundation in medicine albeit self-taught, he further polishes his knowledge by pursuing other [[Legend]]s during his expeditions.

His search eventually makes him come to the [[Legend]] of the Killer of the Great Expanse. He begins searching for the truth of this [[Legend]], and after much dedication he finally uncovers the "treasure trove of sculptures" of the killer which have detailed logs and compositions of [[Spellweaving]] designed to create the tools necessary. The Beautifier also has detailed logs on the anatomy of body parts and some of the sculptures feature [[Pure Light]] beings, this is where [[Cadmus Tacet]] comes to the knowledge of both human organs and [[Coherence-Binding Tissue]]. He later adopts his own style based on the teachings of [[Syvanth]]. He develops a vision too of _"There's even something to learn from monsters"_ to begin the dissection, study, and cruel experiments on [[Pure Light]] beings.

### Character Details

- He has a PTSD fear from his infancy of the clicking sound some of the dragons made before choosing a target for execution. This sound makes him instantly leave any areas during his expeditions, making him on the run as a permanent traveler of knowledge while knowing his own life is on the very line.
- When he eventually grows up, this ticking sound has still PTSD in him, he realizes by having his first "screw up" during vivisection precisely because of this noise that startled him while undergoing surgery as the patient's own throat bloated with blood. This is the visceral connection that reminds him that despite everything, he is still human.
- Despite being very violent towards [[Pure Light]] beings, he's actually fond of most humanoids that share similar structure preaching that they can actually coexist with humans, this includes a good amount of elves, human hybrids, demi humans that only have small differences such as fox ears or a tail. His "hard" limit for empathy is anything below 65% [[Auric Structure]].
- He's very gentle with human children and actually has a following around him by being a tutor in the arts of magic and primitive medicine. He actively teaches humans near him on how to do spells for healing and prevention of wounds worsening in both magical (without [[Strand]] magic) and non magical ways. He prioritizes non [[Spellweaver]]s as he sees that they are the most vulnerable.
- He is very devout follower of the [[Auric Order]], actively patronizing and sponsoring the pursuit of knowledge from both [[Humanity]] and the divine. He never misses any of the sermons and debates profoundly against [[Outer God]]s, the dependency on the esoteric and the [[Chorus Pillar]]. He actively lectures children as well on the tenets of the theological virtues and the importance of communion.
- On his [[Motif Awakening]] he received his cherished [[Auric Bell Staff]] from a mentor who took the risk of giving him the staff to attune for himself at 16. This was a transgression his mentor took because it usually requires being [[Lunar Cycle]] 20 of age to receive one. However, this was the first and most important comfort that he would receive his entire life. The hum of the chorus of everyone came before him were the bedrock for his psychological grounding for the [[Age of Humanity]] as he didn't even know his own parents. This "being on the shoulder of giants" to establish [[Mastery Over Chaos]] was his grounding principle on every hard choice. The time he almost lost the [[Auric Bell Staff]] he almost went insane looking for it as it was his entire support mechanism, which prompted him to establish a [[Soul-Binding]] ritual to keep it permanently with him to never lose it again.
- He loves animals that don't display magical properties, most commonly he prefers cattle and actively engages with [[Domestication Enclave]]s that aren't involved with [[Pure Light]] beings, sponsoring the development of human technology and coexisting with "safe" beings to the point of even pushing for animal rights. Furthermore, he has a deep respect for [[Agromagical Enclave]]s despite not sharing the same medicinal approach. He values them as the first "keepers of knowledge" due to the first uses of observation to create irrigation, culture and plantation techniques, he particularly praises the "domestication of magic" as means to enhance human survival.
- [[Auric Enclave]]s are his common ground during the early history of his exploration. Most of his knowledge comes from the mentorship of pious of the [[Auric Order]]. Although he never fully reveals his plans due to fear of retaliation and having a similar fate to that of [[Vals Jenner]]. Nonetheless, he relies actively on them as sources of information.

### Character Arc

**Arc 1: The Seed of Rage (Childhood Trauma and Exile)**

- [[Cadmus Tacet]] is born into one of the regions dominated by the [[Age of Horrors]] where draconic demi-human overlords subjugate humans as cattle.
- [[Cadmus Tacet]] is born from the result of one of the gruesome breeding rituals.
- [[Cadmus Tacet]] grows alongside humans cowering in the grass.
- [[Cadmus Tacet]] watches the execution of an unknown man by impaling his neck with a [[Crystal]] spear for not subjecting himself to one of the rituals.
- [[Cadmus Tacet]] burns the memory of the dying man into his memory. Before dying, the condemned man smiled a delivered a defiant prophecy "A day will come where [[Humanity]] will outlast their oppressors and the sons of his sons will use the very essence of these [[Pure Light]] beings as the fuel for the [[Age of Humanity]]."
- This was the seed of purpose that gave a young [[Cadmus Tacet]] hope in defiance.
- Before [[Cadmus Tacet]] reaches 13 years old, and he is forced to partake in the separation of new tributes, he manages to escape and is driven into exile, his terror was transmuted into a vow. He embarked on a desperate search to uncover the truth of what gave [[Pure Light]] beings their power and to survive.
- The seeds of his desperate escape cements his [[Legend Trait]] of [[Devoted]]. He will come to see this to the very end.
- Adopts his surname "Tacet" as that was the name of his homeland from which he had escaped.
- He represents the absolute bottom of the societal and magical hierarchy fighting back against the undisputed rulers of the world in memory of his home.

**Core**: Born into the cruelty of the [[Age of Horrors]] → Develops trauma and the seed of rage that will become the vow he dedicates his life to.
**Key Character Trait Formation**: Fear of subjugation becomes existential dread.
**Critical Purpose Seeded**: The quest of knowledge for the emancipation of [[Humanity]] from the chains of volatile magic.

**Arc 2: The Wandering Tutelage of the [[Auric Order]]**

- In his exile [[Cadmus Tacet]] seeks refuge in the nearest [[Auric Enclave]]. He pursues both safety, truth and knowledge from both himself and the divine.
- He learns the basic foundation of the [[Auric Order]], the concept of [[Perfect Reflection]], and the tenets of theological virtues.
- He even learns from the disasters that have preceded him, of both the [[Great Plague]] and of [[The Hollowing]]. He realizes that there's no answer in anything that isn't human. His education by wandering mentors and moving across [[Enclave]]s to escape the [[Age of Horrors]] is the start of his journey during the next four years.
- On his 16th birthday, after having moved thrice of location between [[Auric Enclave]]s, he tries desperately to understand why the heavens remain silent, and why is the underlying meaning of the world's cruelty. This pursue of the truth, of "Why is the world like this, what do [[Pure Light]] beings have have that we do not?" triggers his [[Motif Awakening]] to [[Luminance]], and in the depths of this crisis, he comes to understand that both his suffering and the [[Auric Aria]] gave him a divine mission: "To establish order into the chaos of [[Pure Light]]" just as the [[Auric Aria]] did when she wove pure chaos into structure when she built the [[Known Universe]] and [[Arcanoria]]. For him, this was the [[Perfect Reflection]].
- [[Cadmus Tacet]] becomes a fully fledged devout, and promises to mirror the achievements of his creator. His [[Ambitious]] [[Legend Trait]] evolves into [[Reality Bender]].
- He comes to the conclusion that while he appreciates the mentors that gave him a refuge for the remaining of his infancy, he has to pursue the truth on his own for anything truly revolutionary, he has to look at the records of those who were banished from society.
- [[Cadmus Tacet]] moves out of the [[Auric Enclave]] and into his quest for knowledge. He follows the lead of one scholar that was banished by the very [[Auric Order]], and starts his hunt for archeomagical sites and obscure [[Legend]]s.
- He starts his journey with nothing but a severe PTSD-induced fear of the clicking sounds the dragons made before executing someone.

- **The Search for Truth:** Seeking an edge over the tyranny of [[Pure Light]], [[Cadmus Tacet]] began his quest of knowledge across archeomagical sites and investigated obscure [[Legend]]s.
- **Psychological Shift**: From "Heavens are silent to injustice" to "The injustice of silence is the sacred mandate that I have to impose order" → messianic pragmatism.


**Arc 3: The Seed of Method (The Hunt for the Ghost of [[Vals Jenner]])**

- Driven by his newly evolved [[Reality Bender]] trait and his lead on the trail of an executed scholar, [[Cadmus Tacet]] scours archeomagical sites across the continent, looking for the very knowledge the [[Auric Order]] deemed too dangerous to keep.
- He comes to realize that the traces of the [[Great Plague]] are what drove this scholar to find a similar truth. He develops the mindset of _"I am not alone."_
- On several expeditions he hears the clicking of dragons which forces him to abandon his current location and continue with incomplete information but progressively unlocking more knowledge.
- He comes in contact with [[Indulgent Enclave]]s to create a party of someone that would manipulate [[Strand]] and allow him to uncover the further depths of [[Strand Pool]]s for following what would normally be "cold trials."
- After investigating several [[Memory Field]]s, he manages to triangulate the identity of [[Vals Jenner]]. This is the first grand case he has solved as a master detective.
- He uncovers the trove of suppressed and obscure research of Dr. [[Vals Jenner]], who he posthumously titled as [[Arcanoria]]'s first epidemiologist who studied the traces of the [[Great Plague]] and successfully developed a vaccine.
- From reading about her tragic fate, executed for treason and witchcraft by a superstitious society, [[Cadmus Tacet]] learns the foundations of rigorous observation, hypothesis, and self-taught medicine.
- More importantly, [[Vals Jenner]]’s death instills a dark psychological lesson: truth must be paired with power, and moral judgment is a function of power, not truth. To reveal a revolutionary truth prematurely is to invite destruction.
- He vows that his own work must be meticulously documented but kept entirely hidden from society until [[Humanity]] is ready to wield it. His [[Devoted]] [[Legend Trait]] evolves into [[Piercing Analyst]], allowing him to dissect both biology and human superstition without illusion and his newly honed skills in solving cold mysteries.

**Key Character Trait Formation**: Truth without power is suicide.
**Critical Purpose Seeded**: The quest of knowledge deepens as his own ability to solve cold trail cases hardens by his understanding of [[Memory Field]]s.

**Arc 4: The Seed of Technique (The [[Legend]] of the Twisted Beautifier)**

- [[Cadmus Tacet]]’s expeditions eventually lead him to the horrifying [[Legend]] of "The Twisted Beautifier" an unidentified medieval serial killer locally known as or "The Killer of the Great Expanse."
- This is the hardest case yet because he doesn't know if he's actually chasing a ghost, but the possibility of earning the knowledge of his allegedly [[Mastery Over Chaos]] killer techniques is a reward too high to pass by.
- He follows several leads across [[Memory Field]]s building incomplete knowledge but progressively getting closer to the truth.
- He passes several nights chasing a ghost and doubting the existence of the figure at all. Most leads leave him to cold or closed trials, and he reflects as the [[Legend]] truly seems like an elusive an elaborate fantasy of superstitions.
- During one of his expeditions, a nearby town is overrun by creatures of the [[Age of Horrors]]. He escapes a very near death solely because of his PTSD of the clicking sound. He anticipates before he's hunted himself and manages to survive by a very narrow margin.
- This experience makes him enter a despairing spiral but he grounds himself to the vow of making honor and justice to the surname he himself adopted. This is his motif awakening to [[Crystal]], and the dual-wielding of [[Ornament]]al magic allows him to finally key on the last piece of the puzzle. He uncovers an old name called [[Syvanth]].
- He follows this lead, and triangulates after much deliberate analysis of the possibilities of this assassin. The final breakthrough hits him after remembering the state of the [[Memory Field]] before he was nearly killed by the clicking of the dragon-kind. It happened that the PTSD clicking sound was rhythmic, and when paired in layers for the spells he barely survived plus his own panicking heartbeat it became a polyrhythm. The loud sound of his heart trying to escape his chest paired with the clicking sound were the key to cracking the code.
- He then mapped that exact same idea, of two overlapping frequencies, to the 21 cases he had been investigating. He realized [[Syvanth]] was operating on polyrhythms too and that his location wasn't erratic, it had a rhythm of a symphony he couldn't hear before. The eureka makes him understand that [[Syvanth]] was actually the legendary unknown killer.
- With this knowledge and determination, he manages to locate [[Syvanth]]'s jeweled location. When overlapping the rhythms of 1:3, 3:2, and 5:3 over the 21 cases, it mapped a giant star that pointed in the center to a location where [[Syvanth]] was. Following this lead, he found the hidden "treasure trove of sculptures," containing preserved, surgically disfigured bodies, incredibly detailed anatomical logs, and complex [[Spellweaving]] compositions for precision cutting.
- Opening the trove triggers a profound ethical crisis and epistemic vertigo. [[Cadmus Tacet]] is initially disgusted, recognizing the horrific cruelty of the serial killer.
- However, making a conscious choice to separate the monster's sadistic intent from his brilliant surgical technique, [[Cadmus Tacet]] adopts the philosophy that _"There's even something to learn from monsters."_ 
- This is the moment he accepts the weight of his own potential monstrosity for the sake of his mission. His [[Pragmatic]] [[Legend Trait]] evolves into [[Utilitarian Stoic]].
- He finally solved the impossible case of [[Syvanth]] the Twisted Beautifier and realizes he was using a combination of polyrhythms with elements and his own seductive whistling in an entrancing dance to dismember his victims while keeping them alive and suffering for as long as possible.
- He extracts [[Syvanth]]'s anatomical knowledge of human organs and his precise methods utilizing [[Crystal]], [[Cindergale]], and [[Luminance]] cutting jets. This grants [[Cadmus Tacet]] his first true understanding of the methodology of a surgeon.
- More importantly, alongside the sculptures of disfigured humans, [[Syvanth]] also experimented with [[Pure Light]] beings and established something he called as the "Dancing Organ," which for his twisted beauty was what made his sculptures fickle with light.
- The gruesome reports on this "Dancing Organ" were the final key that [[Cadmus Tacet]] required. He realized that magic came from this very organ, and that it was the holy grail of what he had been fighting to find so desperately. Later he coined the term [[Coherence-Binding Tissue]] after [[Syvanth]]'s sculptures.
- Solving the impossible mystery of the polyrhythmic killer that no one could pinpoint before evolves his [[Piercing Analyst]] [[Legend Trait]] into his first [[Apex Trait]]: [[Clarity of Infinite Light]].


**[[Piercing Analyst]] milestone**: He uncovers the truth about the [[Legend]] of a serial killer who no one could catch. This is a massive victory for his intuition and is his first [[Apex Trait]]. He has both the Why, and the How to commence his vivisection.
**Testing of Ethical Boundaries**: The quest of knowledge makes him separate function from work. [[Syvanth]] was a ruthless monster but this delusion made him the best surgeon of all contemporary history albeit by an aesthetic sadism.

**Arc 5: The [[Legend]] of the Vivisector (The Age of Humanity)**

- Transmuting his childhood trauma into a frozen, surgical determination, [[Cadmus Tacet]] begins with capturing faes and [[Pure Light]] creatures.
- During his first vivisection, the visceral reality of his actions sets in. He experiences a severe "screw up" during the surgery precisely because the creature makes a clicking sound that triggers his PTSD from the draconic executioners of his youth. The panic reminds him that despite his cold exterior, he is still human.
- He establishes a strict, utilitarian boundary to protect his sanity: he sets a "hard limit" for empathy, feeling absolutely no compassion for anything possessing less than 65% [[Auric Structure]] (the threshold for [[Pure Light]] beings).
- Despite the gruesome cruelty he inflicts on magical creatures, a profound duality emerges in his character. He is exceptionally gentle with human children, actively tutoring vulnerable, non-[[Spellweaver]] humans in primitive medicine and healing.
- This newfound boundary is his [[Motif Awakening]] to [[Void]], and evolves his [[Utilitarian Stoic]] [[Legend Trait]] into the [[Apex Trait]] of [[Void-Embrace Anchor]]. He will accept all the necessary losses and will take the blame so long as he can continue to protect [[Humanity]], its heirs, and its children.
- He then becomes patron of some [[Enclave]]s such as [[Auric Enclave]]s for preaching his doctrine of [[Perfect Reflection]] and of [[Domestication Enclave]]s that handle non magical cattle.
- He holds deep respect for mundane animals and [[Agromagical Enclave]]s, praising their "domestication of magic" as the first true keepers of knowledge for human survival.
- In the quiet hours after his dissections, he meticulously records his findings in anatomical logs. This act of documentation is an act of hope, ensuring future generations will understand why he committed these atrocities.
- Looking at his accumulated knowledge and the possibility it represents to subdue the volatility of magic through human reason, [[Cadmus Tacet]] proudly coins his era the first true "[[Age of Humanity]]". His final [[Legend Trait]] of [[Reality Bender]] evolves into [[World Shaping Maverick]] after conducting the first crusade after the very [[Age of Horrors]] in his homeland.

**Arc 6: The Legacy of Iron (The Industrialization of Magic)**

- [[Cadmus Tacet]] eventually reaches the end of his mortal life, having completed his [[Magnum Opus]]: "The Scalpel of [[Humanity]]'s Hope". He ascends to the [[Stellar Legacy Score]] as a [[Constellation]], forever remembered as "The Vivisector" and "The Surgeon of Progress".
- Generations pass, and by [[Ages]] IX (the Industrial Era), [[Cadmus Tacet]]'s anatomical research and surgical methods are uncovered and scaled to a civilization level.
- Driven by the compounding historical trauma of apocalyptic plagues and predatory gods, [[Civilization]] enters the second "[[Age of Humanity]]".
- Applying [[Cadmus Tacet]]'s exact anatomical discoveries, humanity initiates the tragic [[Age of Sealing Iron]]. They build massive machines containing iron cages, systematically trapping faes and [[Pure Light]] beings inside.
- Humanity forces the innate magical essence of these trapped beings to act as mechanical engines, prioritizing absolute structural control over magic to guarantee absolute safety.
- The horrifying industrialization of [[Cadmus Tacet]]'s work flawlessly achieves the defiant prophecy of the Unknown Man from his childhood: [[Humanity]] outlasts their oppressors, using the boundless vitality of [[Pure Light]] gods as the mechanical fuel for human progress.
- In the future this boundary of the [[Pure Light]] spectrum is tested even further in the third [[Age of Humanity]] with the rise of division between "True Pure Blooded" [[Humanity]], and all other Demi Humans. Twisting the legacy of the peaceful coexistence that [[Cadmus Tacet]] himself provided for anything above 65%.
