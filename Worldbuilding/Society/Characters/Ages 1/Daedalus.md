The [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Crystal]] who conquered the vast unknown of the Great Expanse, and cleared the vast areas of [[Atonalis]] alongside [[Aurelian]] to create the cradle of [[Hollowmarch]]. Originally, an demi human slave that lived in indentured servitude meant to die in an execution for transgression to his lord.

The story of [[Daedalus]] begins as sold into servitude, where he was deeply [[Jealous]] of everyone who had a better life than him. He was a brilliant inventor that was forced into labor until he met a fellow orphan who he named [[Aurelian]]. He was saved by him on the trial of execution fleeing into exile into the Great Expanse.

He was dependent on [[Daedalus]] after his emancipation and both pushed to create a better world born of [[Piety]] that eventually became [[Hollowmarch]] as the only [[Major Settlement]] of [[Humanity]] and Demi Humans.

[[Daedalus]] continued the legacy of [[Aurelian]] after his death during the [[Great Plague]] and was the teacher of [[Luminaire]] by the later half of [[Ages]] II. He ultimately died of old age when he reached 220 years old. 

He was the main warden, inventor, and architect of [[Hollowmarch]], the Auric Forge he left was the core economic and military center of [[Hollowmarch]] that developed the legendary weapons of Golden Arcs and the standardized [[Auric Bells]] of the [[Auric Order]] Orthodoxy.

[[Daedalus]] became the patron saint for all inventors, craftsman, and engineers. His training legacy was continued by [[Luminaire]] with her development of refined [[Resonance Box]]es that became a cornerstone of advanced [[Spellweaving]].
