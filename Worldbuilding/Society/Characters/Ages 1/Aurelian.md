The [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Resonance]] who conquered the vast unknown of the Great Expanse, and cleared the vast areas of [[Atonalis]] alongside [[Daedalus]] to create the cradle of [[Hollowmarch]]. Originally, an orphan left to die by the wolves that managed to survive by following the pilgrims of the Aurean Winds. 

He developed a profound meaning in the teachings of the [[Auric Aria]], and the primitive [[Auric Order]], and came to know a demi human slave named [[Daedalus]] who referred to him as [[Aurelian]], and properly accepted this name as his identity due to the ties it had to the Aurean Winds. He is the first of the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] by figuring out that [[Resonance]] was more than just wind or sound, rather he realized that it manipulated waves and cymatics. He was dependent on [[Daedalus]] after his emancipation and both pushed to create a better world born of [[Piety]] that eventually became [[Hollowmarch]] as the only [[Major Settlement]] of [[Humanity]] and Demi Humans.
 [[Legend Relationship]]
[[Aurelian]] died a [[Legend]] saving [[Hollowmarch]] during the [[Great Plague]] paving way to the most prominent empire of classical [[Arcanoria]] in [[Ages]] II and III until its collapse due to [[The Hollowing]]. He never met any of the other [[Mythical Virtuoso]] in his lifetime beyond [[Daedalus]] but became the inspiring [[Legend]] for founding [[The Principles of Magic]] by creating a golden empire of laureated marble out of dust.
