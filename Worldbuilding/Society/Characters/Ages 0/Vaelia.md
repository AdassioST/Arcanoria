- _"Oh, don’t look so solemn! I’m not going anywhere just yet, but someone will be born the day after I die." She said, in that familiar glint of their eyes with the mask in between her hands, with a voice dipping into vulnerability. 
  
  _"And that truth carries more strength than the [[Weight of Change]] I have spent my entire life chasing. Just think about it, neither you nor I were the first [[Legend]], and we will, definitely, not be the last — but that one note, your note, is yours alone in [[The Eternal Symphony]], and don’t pretend you don’t feel it humming in your chest right now." She tilts her head, reading the answer before it came, yet softened enough to drop the act._
  
  _"I spent so long telling myself I was nothing more than an echo. But I realized you can't echo nothing— I knew that, really, I just couldn't escape the tragedy of it. The fact that I survived, that I'm still here, still carving, still playing, still laughing with you... that's my note. Small. Maybe imperfect. But undeniably mine. And so is yours." A pause, then that sly smile again, as if they she hadn't just given her own mask away._
  
  _"Now, stop looking at me like that, or I'll start thinking you actually believe me!"_

### Quotes

**On her Soul Oscillator:**  
_“It plays songs I’ve heard. And now, sometimes, it plays songs I’m still learning. I don’t know where they end, and that’s fine. A song doesn’t need to be finished to be real.”_


### Romance [[Ballad]]

The romance unfolds in beats:

- **First Meeting:** She teases the Legend musically without introduction. She is curious, not impressed.
    
- **First Crack:** She plays a note that is clearly about herself, then stops and won’t explain. The Legend hears her before she is ready to be heard.
    
- **The Deflection:** When things get too close, she pivots to a joke or leaves. The Legend can chase or give space—both choices build trust.
    
- **The Confession:** She speaks without the Oscillator for the first time, tells the Legend something she has told no one. The voice she hides becomes the voice she trusts them with.
    
- **The Mutual Tease:** The Legend gives her a taste of her own medicine. She is _delighted and thrown off_—she didn’t expect to be the one seen.
    
- **The Climax:** She carves her own mask and shows it to the Legend before burying it. She no longer hides; she shares.
