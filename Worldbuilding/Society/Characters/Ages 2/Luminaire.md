The [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Luminance]] and legendary heir to [[Hollowmarch]], involved in the story of [[Father Raphael]]. She is one of the legendary founders of [[The Principles of Magic]] in the field of [[Luminance]] during [[Ages]] II, and she's inspired by the [[Legend]] of [[Aurelian]] in search of her own voice with her [[Soul Oscillator]]. 

She was formally trained by the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Crystal]] up to her coronation which was shortly after [[Daedalus]]' death. The training of [[Daedalus]] in craftmanship proved transcendent for [[Luminaire]] when she pioneered on the first reusable infinite recording [[Resonance Box]]es that couldn't come to be if it wasn't due to her innate intellect for [[Attunement for Magic]], and her former training in invention by [[Daedalus]].

It's important to note that while [[Daedalus]] didn't have any offspring, [[Luminaire]] considered the [[Demi-Human]] more of a father figure than her actual biological family. Her parentship under his tutelage was the closest [[Luminaire]] was to being treated as a person and not as an instrument.

### [[Luminaire]], the Prodigy Heir of [[Hollowmarch]]

**Part I: The Perfect Child**

[[Luminaire]] was born into the kingdom of [[Hollowmarch]], to the throne that has been waiting for her. Not for her specifically. The kingdom has been waiting for a strong heir. A magical heir. A bearer of exceptional gifts who will rule with clarity and power.

Her father, wanted someone to continue his legacy. Her mother, wanted someone to validate her bloodline. The priesthood wanted someone who could channel the [[Auric Aria]]'s will without question. The people wanted someone who could protect them from [[Corruption]] and the [[Formless Masses]].

And [[Luminaire]]—though no one bothered to ask her—was given to them. What no one expected was that [[Luminaire]] would be exactly what they wanted.

She displays signs of [[Attunement for Magic]] before she could walk, and came to be the direct apprentice of [[Daedalus]].

At six years old, she is able to hear the voices of [[Emberwhisper]] crystals—an act that normally takes years of training to trigger. It seemingly appears as if she simply awakens one morning able to hear its hum, what no one had realized yet is that she's only been able to develop this gift due to the sheer negligence where she has had to grow obeying without question nor voice.

By ten, she has accomplished magical feats of [[Attunement for Magic]], and establishing separate [[Resonance Anchors]] that took even the greatest wizards of the age decades to achieve. She was smart, she was a prodigy, and more importantly, she had no right to fight for her autonomy.

She is not just gifted. She is what every parent, every institution, every system of power in the kingdom has been unconsciously dreaming of: the perfect instrument.

The kingdom celebrates her.

Poets write songs about her.

The priesthood declares her a chosen one — a direct expression of the [[Auric Aria]]'s favor.

Her parents hold her up as the validation of their bloodline. _This is what our line produces. This is why we are meant to rule._

And [[Luminaire]], who has never been given the option to want anything else, becomes what everyone needs her to be: the future ruler. The perfect heir. The crystalline instrument through which power flows.

**Part II: The Machinery Of Expectation**

[[Luminaire]]'s education is not an education. It is a sharpening.

Every moment of her life is orchestrated to refine her magical ability. Every relationship is calculated to strengthen her political position. Every emotion is channeled into greater magical potency.

She is taught:

On Power: _"Your gift is not yours. It is [[Hollowmarch]]'s. Your role is to be the conduit through which our power flows. You do not use magic—magic uses you."_

On Emotion: _"Emotion is fuel. Transform your doubts into clarity. Transform your fears into focus. Transform your longings into precision. Never feel; only weave."_

On Identity: _"You are not [[Luminaire]] the person. You are [[Luminaire]] the future queen. Your preferences, your wishes, your authentic self—these are luxuries for ordinary people. You are not ordinary."_

On Sacrifice: _"Everything you love must be sacrificed to your purpose. Your friendships, your lovers, your dreams of normal life—all of these are distractions from your destiny."_

And [[Luminaire]]—who is precisely, impossibly skilled at channeling what others need her to be—complies perfectly.

She does not rebel. She does not doubt. She does not ask for anything different.

Because to ask for something different would be to acknowledge that she might want something other than what has been chosen for her.

And to acknowledge that desire would crack the perfect, crystalline structure of her identity. So instead, she becomes the machine they need her to be. A brilliant, flawless machine. But somewhere deep inside—in a space so small it barely exists—[[Luminaire]] begins to notice something:

The precision they value is a cage.

The clarity they demand requires the elimination of ambiguity—which means the elimination of choice.

The perfect instrument has no will. It has only function.

And [[Luminaire]], who has been taught since childhood to translate every feeling into magical power, and the burden of being the heir to the strongest empire of the old [[Ages]] begin to feel something that cannot be transformed into her performance:

Exhaustion. Not physical exhaustion. Existential exhaustion.

The exhaustion of being used. Of being the vessel into which others pour their needs. Of performing the role of the perfect heir while the actual person—the actual [[Luminaire]]—suffocates beneath the precision.

**Part III: The Crack In The Crystal**

The crack comes at a state ceremony.

[[Luminaire]] is sixteen years old. She is about to be formally presented to the kingdom as the future queen. In a few years, she will ascend to the throne. This ceremony is the symbolic coronation before the actual coronation.

She is dressed in robes woven from [[Emberwhisper]] and spun gold. Her hair is styled to perfection. Her makeup is flawless. Every inch of her has been crafted to represent power, beauty, and the crystalline clarity of the future.

She stands on the dais in front of thousands of people.

And as she stands there, feeling the weight of their expectations, feeling the pressure of her training pressing down on her from every direction, something inside her—something very small, something she has been suppressing since childhood—finally speaks.

It is not a voice. It is not even a thought, really.

It is simply the feeling: I do not want this.

That feeling lasts only a moment but it's a painful albeit small constant reminder of what she suppresses.

She performs the ceremony perfectly. She delivers her speech with crystalline clarity. She channels her affinity to the world's [[Leylines]] flawlessly. By all measures, it is her greatest triumph.

But the crack remains. In the coronation, when she is about to receive the crown, a tear softly runs down her cheek and to the [[Emberwhisper]] threads of her beautiful dress. No one was able to see the tear but they realize that the [[Emberwhisper]] glows more strongly with her. It is the ultimate sign that she's undeniable the magical prodigy that will dictate the future of [[Hollowmarch]].

[[Luminaire]] wants to run, she wants to escape, to feel a place where she can be something more but she doesn't, and when the [[Auric Order]] priests give her the blessing, and the oath to take their nation. She, albeit wavering, as if about to crack in tears, says "Yes, I accept, and I will rule for the bright future of [[Hollowmarch]]."

In the statement of saying those words, she's feels her deepest realization. _"What am I saying? Why can't I stop, I don't want to... Why did I accept? Why did I not say no?"_

And in the weeks that follow, [[Luminaire]] finds herself unable to escape those words that feels like condemnation. The words that came from her tongue were the sharpest cut of them all.

She lies awake at night, feeling the exhaustion that no amount of rest can cure. Two days pass, where she performs her duties flawlessly while something inside her slowly breaks. 

She is brilliant. She is perfect. She is everything everyone wanted her to be.

And she is utterly hollow.

In the third day, she finally has a crying realization, she questions why she couldn't bring herself to call everything off, in that moment of trying to pursue the truth of herself, she has the first [[Motif Awakening]] to [[Luminance]].

The gem solidifies in her hands, after desperately clutching to the stained piece of cloth from her [[Emberwhisper]] dress during her coronation.

And with the gem, she formally becomes a [[Spellweaver]]. Simultaneously, a prodigy, one of the earliest masters, and in her awakening, she also finds an instrument of her own voice. A pure white, untainted [[Soul Oscillator]] evoking in its clear ivory the sound of her very [[Soul-Key]].

A [[Spellweaver]] at such young age, and let alone one with a [[Soul Oscillator]], the rare mythical instrument only the very best can come to conjure. When her family realized, they were overjoyed. She was not just a prodigy for affinity to magic, she was the very best they could all ever possibly imagine. It was an incarnation rivalling that of the [[Auric Angels]].

For [[Hollowmarch]], she was the ultimate miracle. For herself, it was the final chains of not knowing where her voice even was.

In the years that follow, she grew to develop bonds with the rest of the [[Mythical Virtuoso]], particularly with [[Amadea]] and [[Cordelia]], and became a legendary master that would be unrivalled, figuring out the nature of [[Sky Glass]], achieving her [[Apex Trait]] as [[Clarity of Infinite Light]] and whose [[Soul Oscillator]] was the very first of the [[Resonance Anchors]] that sustained the whole infrastructure of the [[Golden Age]] of [[Hollowmarch]]. She was one of the most important and influential figures in all of classical [[Arcanoria]] with an unrivalled intellect, not even by the rest of the other [[Mythical Virtuoso]].

**Part IV: The Arrival Of [[Lacrimosa]]**

A few years after, when [[Luminaire]] came back from the abyss with [[Amadea]], [[Lacrimosa]]'s [[Auroral Ribbons]] transformed the sky as [[The Hollowing]] was initiating, and as the [[All-Loving Moon]] appears, the kingdom of [[Hollowmarch]] treats them as an invasion.

The priesthood condemns them. The military prepares defenses. The royal family issues proclamations declaring the [[Auroral Ribbons]] a corruption that must be stopped.

And [[Luminaire]]—who is now the empire's most powerful [[Spellweaver]], crown jewel, and ultimate regal card—is called to the front lines now that she's back in the kingdom.

She is told: _"You will defend us. You will use your unrivalled gift to protect the kingdom from this abomination."_

And [[Luminaire]], dutiful as always, agrees.

She is sent to observe the [[Auroral Ribbons]]. To understand their nature. To report back so that the empire's forces can develop a strategy to contain or destroy them.

What happens at the [[Auroral Ribbons]] changes everything.

She arrives expecting to see corruption. Instead, she sees something else.

She sees people.

She watches them enter the light. She sees the transformation. She watches [[The Hollowing]] occur.

And she sees the peace on their faces.

Not the peace of delusion. Not the peace of mindlessness.

The peace of finally being allowed to stop.

One person in particular catches her attention.

A young man—perhaps thirty, perhaps younger. He is hollowed, standing at the edge of the Ribbons with the serene expression of the transformed.

She recognizes something in him. A kind of perfect clarity. The same clarity that the kingdom has trained her to perfect. But in him, it is not a cage. It is simply, clear. Without the weight of expectation. Without the burden of duty.

She finds herself asking him: _"Why did you hollow?"_

And his answer destroys everything:

_"Because I realized that my clarity was not mine. It was being used by everyone around me. And I finally understood that I had the choice to stop being the instrument and start being myself."_

In that moment, [[Luminaire]] understands.

She is not defending the kingdom from [[Lacrimosa]].

She is defending the machinery that has consumed her entire life.

The machinery that transformed her from a person into a function. From someone who could choose her own path into a crystalline instrument serving other people's needs. A machinery that even weaponized the sound of her own voice, her [[Soul Oscillator]], as the core of the cage itself.

And standing at the edge of the threshold of the [[Auroral Ribbons]], with the weight of the crown pressing down on her, with the future of the kingdom hanging on her choice —[[Luminaire]] understands that she has a choice.

**Part V: What [[Lacrimosa]] Offers To The Prodigy**

[[Lacrimosa]] does not offer [[Luminaire]] escape.

[[Lacrimosa]] offers her something more specific: the end of obligation.

[[Lacrimosa]] is waiting at the [[Auroral Ribbons]]. Not waiting for [[Luminaire]] specifically. But [[Lacrimosa]], in her obsessive way, has seen [[Luminaire]]. She has watched the prodigy being shaped into an instrument. She has observed the machinery of expectation grinding down the person beneath the gift.

And she recognizes something in [[Luminaire]]: Potential that has been turned into a cage.

[[Lacrimosa]] speaks to her in visions through the [[Auroral Ribbons]] in a sweet, comforting voice:

 _"I see you. I see what they have made of you. And I see what you could be if you were not forced to be their instrument. Your [[Soul Oscillator]] is you, and it is only yours alone._
 
  _"But you have spent your entire life being used. Being shaped. Being refined into something useful. From the very confine of your soul._
  
  _"And you, my dear. Are as selfless as you are deserving. Who is there to save you when you're the savior to everyone. Who cares for [[Luminaire]] when she cares for all? It is tragic. You are as worthy, as everyone you've ever strived to serve, to save, to work. The voice in the keys you touch should be for none but for your most masterful song. They are you, and your beautiful soul."_
  
  The words [[Lacrimosa]] said strung her deepest string, but it was the following that broke her:
  
  _"What if you could stop? What if you could be something useless? Something that serves no purpose? Something that simply exists, without obligation? You have worth even if you have no function, let go of the chains in your own [[Weight of Potential]]."_

As her tears begin running down of the side of her cheek, the [[Sympathetic Vibration]] of the [[Lacrimosa]]'s [[Auroral Ribbons]] gently stroke her in an almost alluring embrace.

 _"What if you could be yourself, [[Luminaire]]? You too deserve the [[Purest of Love]]."_

This comforting presence is revolutionary to [[Luminaire]]. Because she has never been allowed to have a self. She has only been allowed to have a function. And she was finally being vulnerable and was not met with disgust but with the empathy she has always wished to receive as a child.

In her inauthentic self, every emotion, every desire, every authentic impulse has been channeled into magic. Every aspect of her being has been optimized for power, and she's been desperately trying to find a reason as to why would she even allow all of this.

But the very concept of being "useless"—of existing without serving someone else's agenda—is almost incomprehensible to her. And that's maybe the truth that she's been looking for after all these years.

It sounds insane to submit, but it is also the most seductive and comforting thing she has ever heard.

**Part VI: The Choice**

[[Luminaire]] returns to the capital.

The kingdom is waiting for her report. The priesthood wants to know how to defeat [[Lacrimosa]]. The King wants to know if the [[Auroral Ribbons]] are truly a threat.

And [[Luminaire]]—who has been trained to speak with crystalline clarity, to serve the interests of the kingdom, to be the perfect instrument— Luminaire does something she has never done before: she tells them the truth.

"The [[Auroral Ribbons]] are not a corruption. They are an alternative—" [[Luminaire]] begins, then stops to gather her focus once again. "The hollowed are not suffering. They are at peace." In a wavering voice, she adds devastatingly, "And I am going to choose that peace."

The reaction is catastrophic.

Her father declares her a traitor.

The priesthood condemns her as possessed.

The kingdom—which has celebrated her as its future savior—turns on her as its greatest betrayal.

They try to stop her from leaving. They offer her everything: power, love, validation. They tell her she is making a mistake. That she will regret this. That she will be throwing away the most important thing: her destiny.

And [[Luminaire]] responds with something no one has ever heard from her:

She laughs.

It is a bitter laugh. A laugh of intoxicating release. A laugh of someone who has finally realized that the destiny everyone has been forcing upon her was never hers to begin with.

"I have never made a choice. Every moment of my life has been a performance of someone else's dream. Every gift I have has been used for someone else's purpose. Even my [[Soul-Key]] has an obligation to name." In an almost screaming lament, [[Luminance]] says with tears. "For the first time, I am going to do something that serves only me. I will follow only the music of my soul." 

"And I am going to hollow." The words echo the cathedral.

**Part VII: Aftermath**
[[Father Raphael]]
When [[Luminaire]] enters the [[Auroral Ribbons]], something happens that surprises even [[Lacrimosa]].

Because [[Luminaire]] is not just any [[Spellweaver]]. She is the most gifted magical practitioner in [[Hollowmarch]].

When her [[Soul Leitmotif]] transforms—when her [[Luminance]] binding fractures and crystallizes into hollow clarity— The burst of [[Discordant Interference]] is felt across the entire kingdom. Her [[Soul Oscillator]] was the core of the [[Resonance Anchors]].

The precision that she had maintained so perfectly becomes a wound so vast that it tears through the fabric of magical reality itself.

The crystalline structure that has been her greatest strength becomes her greatest shattering.

But when she emerges, hollow and transformed, what the kingdom sees is something terrifying: The most powerful [[Spellweaver]] in the land has made a choice to no longer serve them.

[[Luminaire]] is no longer the instrument of the kingdom's will.

She is no longer the vessel for other people's needs.

She is simply... peaceful.

And that peace is more dangerous to the kingdom's control than any rebellion could ever be.

Because it proves something that the authority structure cannot tolerate:

The things it values—duty, obligation, sacrifice—are not intrinsically meaningful.

They are maintained only through the consent of the people who perform them.

And when someone as gifted, as trained, as essential as [[Luminaire]] refuses to consent— The entire system becomes vulnerable.

The people the kingdom considers most fulfilled—the brilliant, the gifted, the ones like [[Luminaire]] who never rebel—are often the most hollowed.

They have simply learned to call their hollowness by another name. Duty. Purpose. Destiny.

And when [[Lacrimosa]] offers them the chance to stop using that language, to simply be— They find that the hollowness of [[Lacrimosa]]'s peace is more real, more true, and more satisfying than the hollowness of the kingdom's purpose.

[[Hollowmarch]]'s greatest defender has become its greatest threat.

Not through rebellion. Not through fighting.

But simply by choosing to stop.

To be embraced in the [[Purest of Love]].
