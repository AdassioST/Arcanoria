#character #society #spellweaving

Formally known as the Seven Mythical Virtuosi, but also known as the Legendary [[Great Spellweaver]]s, the Pillars of the [[Auric Heptacode]], or the Founding Architects of [[The Principles of Magic]].

These great [[Legend]]s were the first mortal [[Spellweaver]]s to achieve complete mastery over a single binding of the [[Auric Heptacode]], and codified one of the seven of [[The Principles of Magic]], transforming raw [[Resonance]] into a teachable art. Moreover, these [[Legend]]s came to be the most important figures of the early [[Ages]] due to their cultural and sociopolitical influence in the world of [[Arcanoria]].

The title of [[Mythical Virtuoso]] was first attributed to [[Aurelian]], the [[Resonance]] [[Mythical Virtuoso]], and legendary founder of [[Hollowmarch]]. 

He was the first [[Spellweaver]] to properly determine the conditions of [[Resonance]] in [[Ages]] I beyond the immediate sound and wind it moved inspired by the [[Legend]] of the Mother of Bards [[Vaelia]]. His signature [[Soul Oscillator]] became a symbol of leadership, and supreme mastery of [[Spellweaving]] that continued as a tradition of the greatest celebration of [[Hollowmarch]].

Moreover, his [[Legend]] directly inspired [[Luminaire]] to become the Great Ivory Princess with her own [[Soul Oscillator]]. And his own [[Legend]] contrasts too with the rise of [[Iridia]] by the Violet Empress [[Amadea]] that is the founder of the second of the classical civilizations of [[Arcanoria]].

The Seven Virtuosi are:

- [[Aurelian]], the Aurean Wind, the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Resonance]] (Born in [[Ages]] I) [[Underdog]].

- [[Luminaire]], the Fractal Prodigy, the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Luminance]] (Born in [[Ages]] II) Tragic Prodigy.

- [[Orphael]], the Tide Singer, the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Flux]] (Born in [[Ages]] II)

- [[Amadea]], the Violet Empress, the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Void]] (Born in [[Ages]] II) [[Underdog]].

- [[Sephira]], the Eye of the Storm, [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Cindergale]] (Born in [[Ages]] II).

- [[Daedalus]], the Demi Human Architect, the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Crystal]] (Born in [[Ages]] I) [[Underdog]].

- [[Cordelia]], the Panacea, the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] of [[Strand]] (Born in [[Ages]] II) Tragic Prodigy.

### [[The Hollowing]] and the [[Age Crisis]] of [[Ages]] II

[[The Hollowing]] is the grand [[Age Crisis]] and [[World Event]] at the end of [[Ages]] II, and it becomes a pivotal point for the seven virtuosi, with varying reactions to it, and to the decisions of one another:

- [[Aurelian]] and [[Daedalus]] both die before the arrival of [[Lacrimosa]].
- [[Luminaire]] is the first of the [[Mythical Virtuoso]] to choose to embrace [[The Hollowing]].
- Driven by the tragedy of losing the greatest price [[Amadea]] wanted and to respond for [[Iridia]], [[Amadea]] becomes the epicenter of the resistance.
- [[Cordelia]] and [[Orphael]] both decide to embrace [[The Hollowing]] as the devastation of the early civilizations and [[Enclave]]s takes its toll in [[Humanity]].
- [[Sephira]] is driven to despair by [[The Hollowing]] of [[Orphael]] but ultimately chooses to continue fighting as the blazing heart of the resistance, joining forces with [[Amadea]] and [[Iridia]].
- [[Sephira]] and [[Amadea]] die of old age on the early stages of [[Ages]] III after vanquishing [[Pagiel]]. However, they create the seeds for the rebellion and continuation of [[Humanity]] in the [[Age of Legends]].
- [[Cordelia]], [[Orphael]], and [[Luminaire]] become bodily immortal due to [[Lacrimosa]]'s [[Lux Aeterna]] tether, and become major actors of the [[Purest of Love]] during the [[World Event]] of [[The Hollowing]].
- [[Cordelia]], [[Orphael]], and [[Luminaire]] merge alongside [[Lacrimosa]] and every other hollowed soul into the [[Law of Relics]] on the end of [[Ages]] III.
