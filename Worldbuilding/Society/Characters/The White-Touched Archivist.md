A [[Great Chronicler]] [[Spellweaver]] cursed by [[Primal White Noise]] containing the canonical knowledge and wiki of [[Arcanoria]]. The only survivor of the [[First Reset]] by finding a place beyond time as God's blind spot after escaping from the [[Cataclysmic Aftermath]] of the [[Polychord Crisis]] and the firing of the ultimate weapon.

His story is inspired by Galileo Galilei. He was pursuing knowledge of [[Cosmic Motion]] and what happened during the [[Library of Heaven's Tower]]. Came to the realization that there was something that infected every scientist mind which lead to their demise, a sort of noise intrusion in their thoughts that made them unable to communicate with each other rendering everyone a lunatic. He realized it had to do with the way the cosmos itself worked. To pursue the truth, he developed advanced [[Luminance]] [[Flux]] lens technology to observe the [[Moon]] and realize why it wasn't part of the scriptures of [[Cosmic Motion]]. 

When he was close to uncover the truth after realizing there was something living inside the [[Moon]] the [[Auric Aria]] smithed him. She wanted to prevent his theories of the models detailing the structure and the anatomy of the [[Moon]] containing something from spreading and causing another disaster like the [[Library of Heaven's Tower]]. For this, the [[Auric Aria]] created an artificial "sun" of [[Aetherlight]] in between the [[Moon]] his own lenses, weaponizing the tools he had developed in one great eclipse that would burn his eyes due to his search of knowledge and highly enhanced images.

The searing light rendered him permanently blind because of how focused the light became through his own spell, and the [[Auric Aria]] hoped that this was enough of a lesson so that he wouldn't be able to study the [[Moon]] ever again. However, the forced blindness eventually drove him to seek knowledge of the universe coming to the realization of what [[Primal White Noise]] is through creative work-arounds to "see without seeing." The new type of [[Imaginative]] "lenses" became his kaleidoscope for studying an even smaller fabric of the universe, the impossible apparatus became known as "The Eye."

### The [[Legend]] of Nowhere and the King of Madness

Due to his enigmatic nature and sometimes hazardous knowledge, [[The White-Haven Library]] is feared by some as a [[Primal Discordia]] [[Atonalis]], despite him being not one. This is due to three distinct primary reasons:

- **Memetic Hazards:** The knowledge he can offer can have devastating consequences, particularly when weaponized. [[The White-Touched Archivist]] himself sees knowledge as sterile but giving someone the tools to unleash a plague has devastating consequences, whether intentional or by accident a good amount of the truths he can give can become dangerous. Thus, some believe he's a curse incarnate and as dangerous, perhaps even more so, than regular [[Atonalis]]. Moreover, some of the people who have made it to [[The White-Haven Library]] become mad due to the sheer amount of information and the truths contained. _"Whomever comes in contact with this White Madman, becomes as Mad as the evil spirit itself."_

- **His Methods of Manifestation:** Both the link to enter the [[The White-Haven Library]] from [[Arcanoria]], and how [[The White-Touched Archivist]] appear is by clipping from seemingly impossible angles and objects are disturbing to look at. Moreover, clots of [[Primal White Noise]] leave temporary [[Vibrational Fallout]] on the exact point where he appears. This are dangerous and appear as a white version of [[Formless Masses]]. The appearance of these clots tends to be misinterpreted as anomalous behavior mirroring that of an [[Atonalis]], and whomever becomes mad by the knowledge tend to become an [[Atonalis]]. _"Hush now, when the silence appears laughter will die. Run now, far away from the madman before he takes you to the place in-between."_

- **The More Permanent Tethers are Filled with [[Atonalis]]:** This is a very paradoxical property because the strongest [[Resonance Anchors]] linking the impossible entrances of [[The White-Haven Library]] tend to be filled with [[Atonalis]] nests which makes a lot of regular people think he is the [[Atonalis]] King. This, however, could not be further from the truth. The [[Atonalis]] that appear nearby, particularly [[Anxithor]], do this because the demons themselves fear [[The White-Touched Archivist]]. The [[Atonalis]] are scared of him, and that's why they group in his [[Resonance Anchors]] because they want to wipe them out but since they are too dangerous to dismantle, they instead tend to group around in a permanent preemptive strike before [[The White-Touched Archivist]] appears or before anyone attempts to make contact with him. _"If there's a leader for all the demons, it has to be him and the [[Atonalis]] army that defends the doors of that forsaken palace."_
