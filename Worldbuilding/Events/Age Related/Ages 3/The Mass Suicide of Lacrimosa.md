One of the possible endings of [[The Hollowing]], and statistically the one most plausible. It represents the tragic but logical conclusion of both deities failing to overcome their unhealed traumas and the catastrophic loss of [[Lacrimosa]]'s entire identity.

This dark alternate timeline unfolds when [[Lacrimosa]] attempts her gamble and the [[Auric Aria]] isn't compelled to act due to the lack of loss of either [[Father Raphael]] or more than 70% of the [[Auric Order]]. 

However, even if the [[Auric Order]] loses less than 70% but [[Father Raphael]] still submits to [[Lacrimosa]], she will be compelled to act. The most important figure is [[Father Raphael]] but even if he doesn't submit, losing 70% of the [[Auric Order]] will compel her to act. Thus, this annihilation ending resolves through a specific sequence of psychological failures and cosmic mechanics:

**The Failed Gamble and Aria's Paralysis** This timeline triggers if Lacrimosa decides to take her "broadcast gamble"—prioritizing her Possessive obsession over her hunger to consume humanity—and broadcasts an ultimatum across the Great Harmonic Loom to lure the Auric Aria out of hiding. However, in this scenario, the Auric Aria is not compelled to act.

For the Auric Aria to intervene, she requires a deeply personal emotional catalyst which can be either [[Father Raphael]] or a lot of the original priests of the [[Auric Order]] (70%). In the example of the first, if her most devoted follower, Father Aldric, remains stubborn or simply dies of natural old age while clinging to his empty altar, instead of submitting to the [[Purest of Love]] and the threshold of how many priests the Auric Aria has lost, then she never experiences the specific, acute heartbreak necessary to shatter her paralysis. She continues paralyzed by her own wisdom and the crushing guilt of her flawed creations, the Auric Aria remains hidden in the Infinite Void forever, watching perfectly but accomplishing nothing.

**The Triggering of Lacrimosa's Deepest Wound** When the Auric Aria fails to respond to the broadcast, Lacrimosa loses her gamble and feels the entire void of all the [[Attention Addict]] and [[Compulsive Overthinker]] [[Legend Trait]]s she was suppressing so desperately to try this instead of [[The Birth of the Hyper Chord]]. This silence triggers Lacrimosa's deepest, most agonizing psychological wound: the absolute terror of cosmic irrelevance and invisibility.

For eons, Lacrimosa's greatest underlying fear was that she could orchestrate the entire Age of the False Messiah, perform all her rituals, and still remain entirely invisible and irrelevant to the Creator she obsessed over. The Auric Aria's silence confirms Lacrimosa's most devastating insecurity: that she was never meant to matter, and that trying and failing to be seen is infinitely worse than never having tried at all. This realization shatters entirely her self-worth and will to exist.

**The Annihilation of Reality** Unable to bear being ignored, Lacrimosa's terror and despair push her to act first. First becoming a self-destructive lunatic that tears apart her own mortal vessel as flagellation, and in the crux of her self-inflicted wounds she decides that instead of attempting to cast the reality-bending Tetrad Chord to manage her hollowed followers (which in the canon timeline accidentally fractures reality and allows the Auric Aria to safely manifest), she should deliberately turns her destructive power outward.

Driven by the realization that her existence is meaningless without the Auric Aria's recognition, Lacrimosa targets reality itself to tear an impossibly profound rift of **Primal White Noise**. This act of ultimate, apocalyptic despair annihilates both herself and all of humanity simultaneously in a single cataclysmic event.

Statistically, this timeline—along with the [[Alternate Hollowing]] of Aria's submission—is far more robust and plausible than the canon resolution because it relies on the natural entropy of the deities' unhealed traumas: a guilty creator remaining paralyzed, and a possessive, invisible observer destroying everything out of pure despair of never being acknowledge after gambling everything in a desperate effort.
