## Seven bindings as psychological axes

In your canon, each binding corresponds to a psychological domain, which we can use as latent factors for wishes:

- **Resonance (Key of Attunement)** – purpose, identity, belonging, self-integrity ("Who am I? Where do I fit?").
- **Luminance (Sufficient Precision)** – clarity, truth, cognition, insight into flaws and reality ("What is true? What am I blind to?").
- **Flux (Emotional Authenticity)** – emotion, indulgence, aliveness, catharsis ("What do I feel? Can I feel at all?").
- **Void (Essence Sacrifice)** – loss, self-worth, giving up or giving of oneself, cost of change ("What must I lose or give?").
- **Cindergale (Perfect Focus)** – state, attention, momentum, rest vs motion, bodily vitality ("How do I move or stop?").
- **Crystal (Absolute Certainty)** – vision, imagination, conviction, mental models of reality ("What future or structure do I believe into being?").
- **Strand (Echoing Bonds)** – legacy, memory, relationships, loyalty, temporal continuity ("Who/what am I bound to across time?").

Clinically, these map well onto known motivational clusters: **meaning/self-actualization**, **agency/mastery**, **communion/attachment**, **avoidance of anxiety/pain**, and **moral/virtue scripts**, which organize 161 human motives into Meaning–Communion–Agency clusters.

Wishes below are informed by common patterns in:

- **Cognitive distortions and self-blame** ("I should have known better", hindsight bias, personalization).
- **Perfectionism and contingent self-worth** (self‑oriented and socially prescribed perfectionism, worth tied to performance).
- **Attachment styles** (dismissive "I don't need anyone," anxious "don't leave me," fear of intimacy).
- **Basic human motives** (achievement, affiliation, power, meaning, avoidance).

Weights are approximate and meant as *training/benchmark priors* for a wish‑identifier AI. They need not be exact; the important part is the pattern of which bindings are most and least activated.

**Void and Strand are never primary.** Across all 200 entries, Void tops out around 25% and Strand rarely exceeds 15%, always as a secondary flavor of something else. **Joy‑ and gratitude‑driven awakenings are absent.** Canon explicitly states Motif Awakenings can come from "pure joy or validation," not only tragedy — Eyras's belonging-awakening and "I wish for this moment to last forever" are given as examples — yet the corpus is almost entirely deficit/pain-coded.

**Power, control, and other‑directed (communion) motives are underrepresented.** McClelland's three‑needs model identifies achievement, affiliation, and power as core human motivators, and power specifically (desire for influence, control, status) barely appears at all in the corpus, nor do wishes made _for someone else_ rather than the self.

**Stylistic range is narrow.** Every wish reads like a therapy-session sentence. There's no blunt, short, plain, or culturally casual register — the kind of wish a real person might actually type.

## Psychological grounding for this batch

**Meme-culture wishes** often function as _ironic distancing_ — using absurdity or internet vernacular to voice a real underlying need without the vulnerability of saying it plainly. "Main character energy" wishes reflect the documented social-media phenomenon of narrative self-centering as an identity/significance need, not just narcissism.
