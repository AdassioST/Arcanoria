# Symbology — Canonical Iconography

> Canon Ledger entry. Author-confirmed visual and symbolic motifs for [[Arcanoria]].

## Primary Symbols

### [[Auric Heptacode]]
- **Form**: Seven-pointed golden heptagram
- **Meaning**: The seven [[Principles of Magic]] and their interconnections
- **Status**: PRIMARY symbol of the magic system
- **Usage**: Appears in Spellweaving diagrams, the [[Celestial Astrolabe]], knightly insignia

### The Violet
- **Form**: Violet/purple color, violet imagery
- **Meaning**: [[Amadea]]'s signature — the Violet Empress
- **Status**: PRIMARY character symbol
- **Usage**: Associated with [[Amadea]]'s presence, her court, her magic

### Obsidian
- **Form**: Black glass, dark reflective surfaces
- **Meaning**: The Obsidian Empress variant of [[Amadea]] — hardened grief, beauty through darkness
- **Status**: PRIMARY character symbol
- **Usage**: The [[Obsidian Feather]]s, the Obsidian Empress title

## Character Aesthetics

### [[Amadea]] — Lacrimosa Aesthetic
- **Visual**: Dark elegance, muted power, starlight through void
- **Musical**: Featherwood Threaded Violin, [[Dance]]
- **Emotional**: Grief that doesn't break; sorrow that deepens
- **Key image**: Stars visible only in darkness

### [[Lacrimosa]] — The All-Loving Moon
- **Visual**: [[Auroral Ribbons]], pale silver-white light, the False Moon
- **Sound**: Weeping that sounds like mercy
- **Emotional**: Compassion without limit — the mercy that eats the world
- **Key image**: A moon that weeps for you

## Forbidden / Conflicting Iconography

> **RULE**: Avoid these unless specifically directed.

| Motif | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Avian / Bird motifs | **AVOID** | Conflicts with established visual language; may reference discarded themes |
| Bright primary colors | Use sparingly | [[Arcanoria]]'s palette is dark — violet, gold, obsidian, silver |

## Symbolic Architecture

Each major character's visual design should trace to:
1. Their **Soul Leitmotif** element
2. Their **Ornament** elements
3. Their **core wound/emotion**
4. Their **role in the narrative**

Example — [[Amadea]]:
1. [[Void]] → darkness, absence, the space between stars
2. [[Crystal]] → frozen grief, transparency, fragility that doesn't break
3. [[Luminance]] → the light that darkness makes possible
4. Core wound: Her mother's death, [[Artus]]'s sacrifice
5. Role: The North Star — guidance through darkness
