## 🏛️ Nexus 1: Inscription of Will (Glyphics, Amadea's Craft & Vow Marks)

### Metaphysical Anchor
- **Radicals of Glyph-Making:** Glyphs operate under the [[Glyphic Heptastave]]. Radicals consist of:
  1. *Stave Clef (Tessitura Radical):* Selects which of [[The Registers of Magic]] the spell draws from.
  2. *Harmonic Core (Interval Radical):* Defines the chord root (Dyad/Triad) and acoustic binding ([[Flux]], [[Void]], [[Crystal]], etc.).
  3. *Cadence Gate (Rhythm Radical):* Regulates release timing, attack envelope, and re-excitation triggers.
- **Parasitic Dissonance Glyphs & [[Vow Mark]]s:** Parasitic scores tattooed onto the cutaneous tissue and anchored directly into the [[Soul-Key]]. Any frequency divergent from the master command triggers localized [[Discordant Interference]] (convulsive pain, neural burn).
- **Silent Playback & Synchronization:** The score plays directly into the attuned consciousness via [[Sympathetic Vibration]]. To outsiders, the dancer moves in terrifying silence; to the user, they are locked into an internal tempo.

### Amadea Narrative Application
- **The Early Devotion:** Feeling inadequate compared to the effortless rhythm of [[Lynette]], [[Carmina]], and [[Sonia]], young Amadea devotes her sharp intellect to mastering glyph radicals. She paints and prepares stabilization glyphs to shoulder the load for [[Artus]] while she cannot yet safely weave.
- **The Act 20 Climax:** This early foundational discipline pays off when subjected to the noble's [[Vow Mark]]s. Knowing her body is conditioned to collapse under disobedience, she utilizes the downward pull of the command and shifts her collarbone to fling a hidden [[Music Glyph]] upward, severing the chandelier and crushing her captor.
