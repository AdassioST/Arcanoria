## 🎵 1. Acoustic Systems & Spellweaving Mechanics
- [ ] **Tessituras & Disciplines of Spellweaving:** Define the exact boundary layers for the vocal/instrumental registers and how [[Amadea]] and [[Luminaire]] revolutionize them.
- [ ] **Chord Layering — the Pivot Rule:** Mechanize [[Luminaire]]'s discovery. Five elements cannot be bound as a single chord — a pentachord is musical nonsense and will not hold. The set A–B–C–D–E is instead played as two triads sharing a common tone: A–B–C layered with C–D–E, joined at the pivot C. Because a triad is naturally stable, the layered pair carries what the single chord could not. Chord Layering is the technique; the shared tone is the pivot. [[Beam Arts]] already exists as the precedent — the unbuilt work is the layering mechanism itself, and the empty [[Triad Chord]] / [[Tetrad Chord]] / [[Dyad Chord]] / [[Unison]] notes it should be written into.
- [ ] **The Radical Taxonomy of the [[Glyphic Heptastave]]:** Structure the visual stroke system (clef, interval core, gate envelope) for field drafting.
- [ ] **The Circle of Fifths Pentagram Mechanics:** How the heptastave interfaces with five-point circular geometry to freeze complex harmonic loops into permanent stones/cloth.
