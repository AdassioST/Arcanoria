# Acoustic Ontology — Core Rules

> Canon Ledger entry. Author-confirmed rules for the [[Arcanoria]] magic system.

## The Three Foundations

1. **Matter = Stabilized Sound**
2. **Magic = Crystallized Emotion**
3. **Reality = Resonance**

These are not metaphors. They are the literal physics of [[Arcanoria]] at every scale — cosmological, biological, psychological, mechanical, and theological. ([[The White-Touched Archivist]])

## The Seven Principles of Magic

The [[Principles of Magic]] (7 bindings) govern all magical phenomena. Every spell, ability, and magical effect MUST trace to:

- A specific **Spellweaving chord** (Root / Harmony / Tempo)
- One or more **Elements** ([[Void]], [[Crystal]], [[Luminance]], [[Flux]], [[Cindergale]], [[Strand]], [[Resonance]])
- A **Soul Leitmotif** or **Ornament**

## Spellweaving Chords

All magic is structured as a chord with three components:

| Component | Function |
|---|---|
| **Root** | The foundational element — what the spell *is* |
| **Harmony** | The emotional resonance — what the spell *feels* |
| **Tempo** | The temporal structure — how the spell *unfolds* |

## Soul Leitmotifs

Every [[Spellweaver]] has a **Soul Leitmotif** — a crystallized expression of their fundamental frequency. It exists in three states:

1. **Dormant** — Resting. Stable but limited power.
2. **Wearable** — Active. Full power, but demands constant emotional honesty.
3. **Ascendant** — Transformed. Maximum power, maximum risk.

If a Soul Leitmotif fractures (through surrender to wound), the Spellweaver becomes an [[Atonalis]].

## Ornaments

**Ornaments** are secondary bindings that augment a Soul Leitmotif. A Spellweaver with Ornaments is exponentially more powerful than one with only a primary binding.

Example: [[Amadea]] has:
- **Soul Leitmotif**: [[Void]]
- **Ornaments**: [[Crystal]], [[Luminance]]

## Generic Spells — Mechanical Grounding

Generic spells (fireball, teleport, telekinesis, etc.) are allowed BUT must be mechanically grounded:

| Generic Spell | Acoustic Grounding |
|---|---|
| Telekinesis | Resonance field manipulation |
| Wind | Flux fluid dynamics |
| Gravity repulsion | Soliton polarity inversion |
| Spatial warping | Topological Arts bending [[Auric Geometry]] |

## [[Auric Heptacode]]

[[Auric Heptacode]] is the seven-pointed golden heptagram — the foundational symbol of [[Arcanoria]]'s magical framework. It represents the seven Principles and their interconnections.

**Iconographic rule**: The golden heptagram is the PRIMARY symbol. Avoid conflicting iconography (e.g., avian/bird motifs) unless specifically directed.

## Signal Loss

[[Signal Loss]] — the degradation of magical effect over distance or through interference. All magical signals degrade. This is why proximity matters in Spellweaving.

## [[Law of Relics]]

[[Law of Relics]] — magical artifacts retain the resonance of their creators. Relics are not inert objects; they are frozen chords that can be reactivated.

## Trinity Harmony

[[Trinity Harmony]] — the principle that three-part structures (Root/Harmony/Tempo, Matter/Magic/Reality) are inherently more stable than binary or quaternary structures.

## Multi-Angle Thinking

When solving magical problems, always offer **several distinct Spellweaving approaches** — different element/chord/tempo combinations. Flexibility in method, rigidity in system coherence.

## Absolute Rules

- **No handwaving.** Every magical effect must have a mechanical explanation.
- **No generic mana.** All magic traces to the Principles.
- **No elemental schools unmapped to the Principles.** If it exists, it connects.
- **Flag violations.** If a proposal violates acoustic principles, flag it as incompatible and suggest a canon-grounded workaround.
