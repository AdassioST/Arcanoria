# 2026-09-15 — Chord Layering, and a register-drafting skill

## Canon established

- **[[Chord Layering]]** was written from the author's own account. Five bindings cannot be
  bound as a single chord; the set is played as two [[Triad Chord]]s sharing a common tone as
  the pivot, and a triad is stable because three mirrors the [[Trinity Harmony]] division of
  Past, Present and Future already stated in [[Triadic Virtues of Spellcraft]]. The note lives
  at the vault root as `Chord Layering.md`.
- The cost of the technique — pivot must still be sounding when the second triad enters, both
  chords fail together when it drops, and holding a binding steady taxes [[Composure]] — was
  drafted, not authored. It awaits the author's ruling.

## Ledger corrections

- `Canon_Ledger/Gateway_Backlog_Pipeline.md` §1 carried an item, "Dual-Triad Layering vs.
  Tetrad Chaos", that paraphrased the author's own line in `Worldbuilding/Gateway To Genesis.md`
  and drifted from it in three ways: it dropped the `+` from "[[Tetrad Chord]]+", so tetrads
  themselves read as the failure mode; it introduced [[Discordant Interference]], which the
  author's line never claimed; and "invariably collapse" overstated "nonsense". The taxonomy
  contradicts the paraphrase — 43 entries use four-binding chords without incident. The item has
  been rewritten as **Chord Layering — the Pivot Rule**.
- Standing rule confirmed: the author's wording in `Gateway To Genesis.md` outranks any ledger
  paraphrase of it.

## Gaps flagged, not filled

- The chord vocabulary is empty. `Tetrad Chord.md` holds only `#chaos #spellweaving`;
  `Triad Chord.md`, `Dyad Chord.md` and `Unison.md` hold a single tag each; `Minor Note.md` is
  zero bytes; `Major Note.md` is a tag line. Every entry in [[The Registers of Magic]] invokes
  [[Major Note]] and [[Minor Note]], and neither is defined.
- [[Dimensional Arts]] is the only five-binding entry in the taxonomy. Under the pivot rule it
  cannot be a chord; which two triads it uses and which binding pivots is unnamed.
- Notation consequence: writing five bindings in one parenthesis now reads as a pentachord, which
  cannot exist. The taxonomy needs a layered-chord notation.
- `arcanoria-canon` describes Spellweaving chords as Root / Harmony / Tempo while
  [[The Registers of Magic]] uses [[Major Note]] Root and [[Minor Note]]s. Worldbuilding naming
  wins; the skill was left flagging this rather than editing `arcanoria-canon`.
