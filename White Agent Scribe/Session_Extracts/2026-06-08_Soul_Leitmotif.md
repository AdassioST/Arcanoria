## Musical Signature

The ideal [[Amadea]] leitmotif should embody:
- **Sorrow with starlight** — sad but not hopeless, beauty visible only in darkness
- **Quiet, controlled power** — not explosive; the steady force that outlasts
- **Crystalline acoustic purity** — organic instruments, not synthetic
- **The voice of silence** — instrumental, no words; her silence speaks
- **Heartbeat tempo** — slow (55-85 BPM), like a pulse at rest
- **Grace in motion** — the Dancing Blade Arts; sorrow transformed into movement

## Selected Primary Leitmotif

> **"On the Nature of Daylight" — Max Richter**

**Why**: It contains all elements — sorrow without despair, strength without aggression, beauty that generates light from within. The strings don't weep; they *hold*. Like [[Amadea]] holds [[Iridia]]. Like she holds [[Artus]]'s memory. Like she holds the line against [[Lacrimosa]].

The track builds the way [[Amadea]] builds: slowly, quietly, each layer adding depth rather than volume. By the end, you're not hearing sadness — you're hearing the sound of someone who has made peace with their grief and chosen to let it make them *more*, not less.
