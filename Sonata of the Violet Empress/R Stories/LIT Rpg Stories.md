In the canonical victory, she chooses the first path. She uses her cunning, her memory of Artus, and her refusal to accept the nymph's framing to escape the conservatory and continue her journey.

In the bad endings, she chooses the second path. She loses herself to pleasure, to pain, to the nymph's art. She becomes the lotus, the canvas, the sculpture—beautiful, but empty.
