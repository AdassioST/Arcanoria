### Character Arc

- The [[Ballad]] [[Artus]] hears is a confusion of his own mind, he was confusing the [[Legend]] of [[Medea]], the [[Silver Blood]], during [[Ages]] 0, with the name "[[Amadea]]" that's why the tree he remembers is a [[Glimmerfern]] grove with concentrated pooled [[Lunehymn]]. The life of [[Medea]] is one of ultimate tragedy, and it's very different from what he says about the violet grove as the [[Silver Blood]] kingdom falls during [[Ages]] I unless saved in the [[Age of Hymns]] as part of the [[Sonata]] of the Pale [[Moon]]. The confusion is a fabricated story mixing a good ending vastly different from what actually happened, it's the phenomenon of [[Mythic Drift]] in the canon story.

- [[Motif Awakening]] to [[Crystal]] during the coronation of the first [[Saber-Knight]] of [[Iridia]] by a young [[Amadea]] on a watch tower armory [[Outpost]].

- First [[Ornament]] to [[Resonance]] after spending the night with [[Amadea]] during the waltz of the [[Resonance Box]].

- Dies before he fully develops his secondary [[Ornament]] to [[Cindergale]] or [[Strand]]. His death in battle against an [[Ascendant]] is the [[Motif Awakening]] to [[Void]] of [[Amadea]].

- He is one of the youngest [[Legend]]s to have slain an [[Ascendant]] [[Atonalis]] without a squad of [[Spellweaver]]s. He was a prodigy with the sword that would've likely became a saber master virtuoso if he would have lived for longer.
